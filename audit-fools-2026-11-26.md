# Fools corrected copying mechanism, 26 November 2026

Earlier note treated implanted personalities as alteration of one same self. Paul McAuley's publisher-hosted finished review identifies Marna as a replica inside Marceline and a deep-cover police persona that transfers between hosts. Strange Horizons independently identifies Fools and Mindplayers as one future, with alternate role personalities and identity mystery; Mindplayers is not currently included, so this correction neither adds that novel nor creates a duplicate card. One included novel retained.

Root-down: Speculative fiction > Science fiction > Futurist SF > Future of Earth fiction > Techno fiction > Technomorphosis > Posthuman > Remade minds > Copied minds. Technological mind machines and transferable/replicated patterns, no proven magic. Future city and society shaped by memory/personality trade, not intentional period history, recognizable present, unanchored cosmos or actual time travel. Fragmented subjective narration and stolen earlier memories are not travel to another historical era. Not Hard SF physical-science problem solving, Spacefaring or Alien fiction: Earth-human urban mind economy. At Future of Earth, technology changing personhood outweighs regime design, psi as science, war or alternative society. At Techno fiction, machine mediation does not make subjects machine minds.

Cyberpunk > Cyberpunk noir is a serious competing path: undercover police, assassins, black market and crime plot. Neural fiction also has mind-to-mind interfaces. But the mystery is literally which independent person's replicated persona occupies the narrator, and the story makes exploitation, memory addiction and multiplied self its reading experience; no VR place or neural access experience organizes it as strongly. Technomorphosis > Posthuman, not collective-life Post-society; Remade minds rather than bodies. Copied minds explicitly includes a recorded personality or persona pressed into others, while Rewired minds explicitly excludes a transferred/duplicated pattern. The protagonist is not merely the same person changed in place. This is a correction under unchanged gates, not a new definition.
