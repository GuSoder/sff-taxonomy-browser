# The Child Garden bounded recheck, 26 November 2026

Retain Rewired minds, but correct the prior factual claim that living people are not copied: the Consensus explicitly stores personality models of citizens and Milena is copied into it near the end. EBSCO's ending summary, Jesse Cohn's Culture Machine essay and Glasgow Guardian independently establish this. Copies are real in the story, not dismissed because they form a gestalt.

Full root-down: Speculative fiction > Science fiction > Futurist SF > Future of Earth fiction > Techno fiction > Technomorphosis > Posthuman > Remade minds > Rewired minds. Engineered viruses carry education, conformity and sexuality conditioning in future London. Genetic engineering/organic machines, not established supernatural magic; Dante's opera is staged via holograms, not a literal mythic portal. Final souls/angels and all-times-now language sit within the Consensus' mental/information-space transcendence. No positive actual historical-era travel is established by non-linear memories or transcendental experience. Historical/Modern fail explicit transformed human future; Spacefaring fails because orbital performance/ambassador plans support Earth's human story; Hard SF lacks rigorous physical problem-solving; Alien fiction fails human-origin technologies.

Future societies > Cultural futures is a serious higher-level rival: opera, transmission of knowledge and the value of art organize much of the action, as Vector's multiple critical readings stress. Dystopian control, gene-engineered bodies and shared minds are other substantial rivals. But the directly changed personhood and struggle for an unprogrammed self are more specific: Milena's viral immunity, Rolfa's personality erased by imposed treatment, love and artistic originality confronting neurological normality. Technology does not merely provide scenery to art. At Techno, neither machine intelligence nor VR/neural immersive place is the main subject. Posthuman not abstract Post-society collectives; mind/personality rather than designed anatomy or species succession. No Genetic caste attempt: engineered Bears are not the primary rank hierarchy. Not copied-minds by an unsupported denial, but by weighing what the reader repeatedly experiences. Copied Consensus and final uploading are a strong alternative; living neurological programming, immunity and personal consequences govern most of the novel and its emotional center. Retain existing Rewired with moderate confidence and explicit copy/transcendence caveat. Not a claim the same self is never copied; this is selection by dominant whole story, not mechanism purity.

Finished SF Site review identifies personality-altering viruses as a fundamental change and only one part of a complex human puzzle. Culture Machine closely reads conditioning and subjective freedom. Vector's critical panel foregrounds Rolfa's treatment destroying her wanted identity and Milena's art restoring possibility. These support retention while repairing the false rationale. No new structure/gate/art or bibliographic values.
