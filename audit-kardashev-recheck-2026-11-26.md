Posthuman spacers: Space techno fiction centered on technologically transformed personhood and lived experience among the stars: manufactured or replaceable bodies,copied or integrated minds,and technologically extended or sustained lives. Infrastructure may enable that existence,but the person's identity,agency or survival is the governing inquiry.

Kardashev scaling: Space techno fiction centered on technology extended into civilization-scale systems beyond Earth: building,inhabiting,controlling or sustaining computational environments,stellar machinery and interstellar networks. It evokes the outward ambition of the Kardashev scale rather than requiring a numbered energy rating. Mere space travel,an advanced background empire or a server aboard a ship is not sufficient. The infrastructure and what lets civilization operate at that scale must be the subject.

The split does not rename the whole branch as all Kardashev-related SF. Existing root gates still hold; alien constructs,orbital-engineering puzzles or another dominant engine elsewhere do not automatically move here.

## All ten checked

Accelerando -> Kardashev. Planets consumed into stellar-powered Matrioshka computing,uploaded expeditions,networks and economic successors beyond human scale. Personal uploads are major,but the progression of computational civilization is the whole novel's organizing experience. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accelerando ; http://www.accelerando.org/fiction/faq.html

Freyaverse -> Kardashev. This changes from earlier assignment. Saturn's Children explores how robot replacements run a Solar-System civilization under physical travel constraints; Neptune's Brood moves this into interstellar expansion and its financing/communication infrastructure. Creator explicitly frames shared project as machine descendants making cosmic colonization possible. The latter novel's main subject is interstellar economics,not robot embodiment,according to the complete Strange Horizons review. First-book soul-chip identity and slavery remain a serious counterweight,but the whole two-book world is most distinctive for the machinery and institutions that let civilization function and grow beyond biological humanity's reach. No claim of explicit Type-I/IIrating or planet-energy capture. Sources freshly read: http://www.accelerando.org/fiction/faq.html ; https://www.locusmag.com/Features/2008/08/locus-magazines-russell-letson-reviews.html ; https://reactormag.com/review-saturns-children-by-charlie-stross/ ; https://www.benjeapes.com/index.php/writing/book-reviews/saturns-children-by-charles-stross/ ; https://strangehorizons.com/wordpress/non-fiction/reviews/neptunes-brood-by-charles-stross/

Web of Angels -> Kardashev. Galaxy-spanning and beyond-Milky-Way Bell network is the protagonist's subject of mastery and confrontation,not a backdrop telephone. Network reach does not prove a technical energy classification; membership follows the user's outward-infrastructure meaning. http://www.templetongate.net/web-of-angels.htm ; http://www.streettech.com/bcp/BCPgraf/Media/webofangels.htm

Ventus -> Kardashev. Whole three included novels connect near-future network economies and autonomous environments,ringworld/manifold infrastructure and Solar-System Archipelago,to nanotechnological planetary ecology and an interstellar AI history. Not a claim every installment has stellar megacomputers. Creator explicitly binds the continuity; Locus identifies constructed worlds and their organizing systems as the common inquiry. https://www.karl-schroeder.com/the-rewilding/lady-of-mazes ; https://locusmag.com/review/russell-letson-reviews-stealing-worlds-by-karl-schroeder/

Old Twentieth -> Posthuman. This changes from earlier assignment. Immortality and its failure,VR layers sustaining frozen people,and the survival of human experience on a five-ship800person voyage. Not a story of grand infrastructure expansion merely because it crosses interstellar distance. https://rambles.net/haldeman_old20th05.html ; https://cloggie.org/books/old-twentieth.html

Spin -> Posthuman. Strongest remaining overlap: collapsing quantum-transit network and the Drift frame interstellar survival. Across all three novels,Catherine Li's manufactured identity,memory and her connection to AI Cohen,then his scattered conscious copies and her effort to recover him,make personhood the recurring foreground. Not automatically Kardashev because the plot stakes span civilization; the first two have clone/AI identity and emancipation as well as espionage,not an outward-infrastructure development arc. Whole publisher accounts plus SFE read. https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/series/A62/the-spin-trilogy/ ; https://sf-encyclopedia.com/entry/moriarty_chris

Takeshi Kovacs -> Posthuman. Needlecasting across stars enables the society,but the three novels center the lives and power relations made possible by stacks,sleeves,duplicated and resurrected selves. Not about building or scaling the transmission infrastructure. https://www.richardkmorgan.com/series/takeshi-kovacs-novels/

Eight Worlds -> Posthuman. Four included novels foreground reconstructed,cloned,sex/body-changing people and their agency in AI-supported habitats. Ophiuchi's hotline and exile give infrastructure scale a foothold,but the whole-world through-line is identity metamorphosis and artificial life conditions,not technological outward scaling. Loose shared chronology caveat unchanged. https://sf-encyclopedia.com/entry/varley_john ; https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/series/8WO/eight-worlds/

Glasshouse -> Posthuman. Fabricated bodies,backup consent,memory worms and changed identity inside the experiment. Gate-linked interstellar civilization is the enabling background,not a scale-building subject. https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/294267/glasshouse-by-charles-stross/ ; https://strangehorizons.com/wordpress/non-fiction/reviews/glasshouse-by-charles-stross/

Beyond Enemies -> Posthuman. Engineered AI-host people,neural co-presence and the pair's agency after leaving service. No source-supported civilization-scale infrastructure subject. https://paulsemel.com/exclusive-interview-beyond-enemies-author-marisa-wolf/
