# Intake process

Ruling of 2026-10-09 (curator message phonemsg-01M4GD863CV2Z9QWVMDSNPBY4Y).

## No parked pile
- A book is never parked. Every book is walked root-down as soon as it comes up.
- If the walk finds an honest leaf, the book is placed there.
- If the walk finds no honest leaf, the book gets a seed proposal for a new leaf under a genuinely matching parent.
- If the honest leaf already holds nine fictions, the book comes as a split proposal.

## Proposals
- Proposals go out as soon as the book is walked. They are not batched.
- Only one decision is open with the curator at a time. The next proposal follows once the previous one is answered and, for splits, live and linked.
- Reason: image generation is limited to 50 per day, so the curator needs time to think over approvals early. The intake keeps running behind the open proposal.

## The only cross-off
- Non-fiction with no proof of speculative content is crossed off the list and the walk moves on.
- Also skipped as before: short works, collections, anthologies, novellas, omnibus entries and editions. Unpublished works are held until they publish.

## Unchanged rules
- Branches hold zero direct fictions. Splits keep the original node as an umbrella.
- A shared universe is one fiction (rule 11j).
- No leaf is opened without a book that needs it; empty leaves are not opened.

## Historical shelves versus futurist (curator, 2026-10-09)
Futurist is a frame of mind of the author: a future time of wonder that had not yet come at the time of writing. A book that celebrates a recognizable past period whose technology's golden age has clearly come and gone belongs on the historical shelves, whether it was written in the era or as a retro throwback; browsers of that period find both together (Rocket Riders sits in Rocketpunk beside modern interwar books). Do not apply this test from the space race onward: the verdict is still out on space, so space opera stays futurist whatever its vintage. The atomic age is the awkward edge; atomic-age books stay where they already fit.

## Portrait generation checklist (curator, 2026-10-10)
Every portrait prompt, new or edit, must carry: painterly oil style, 2:3 portrait, serious grounded realism, anatomically correct hands and arms, no text, not video-game look, and NO BOWS, NO ARROWS, NO QUIVERS anywhere in the frame, even in the background (curator, "make a note not to do pictures with bows in the future", 2026-10-10 3:17 PM). The generator cannot draw bows. Keep hands simple (no complex grips). Fix by fresh regeneration, not by editing a bad base image; edits faithfully preserve bad arms. Two variants A/B per new subgenre or umbrella, picked by bare letter; the pick is wired under a new file number.
