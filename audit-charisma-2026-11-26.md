# Charisma present-day ancestor correction, 26 November 2026

Old Copied minds mechanism was reasonable at the leaf but never licensed skipping Futurist. Tor's distributed publisher copy explicitly calls this a present-day science-fiction thriller; contemporary SFRevu independently says contemporary America, existing technology plus supposition. Strange Horizons' finished review locates the same contemporary Los Angeles/Vancouver human experiment. No factual future setting is established by the mere novelty of personality imprinting.

Root-down: Speculative fiction > Science fiction > Modern day SF > Soft science fiction > Social science fiction > Psychological SF. Deliberate behavioral conditioning and implanted personality template are the speculative science, not magic or an invented fantasy realm. Recognizable writer-present America and existing-tech extrapolation, not a period setting, future society, spacefaring world or temporally unplaceable reality. Narrative flashbacks/jumps recount a dead patron; not actual time travel.

At Modern day, no alien encounter/invasion or apocalypse, no designed genetic kind/cloning/nanomedicine. Technothriller is an authentic review label and a serious rival, but none of its existing children fits the engine: not cryptographic/information warfare, covert enhanced-operative espionage, or a high-tech system emergency with engineering/logistic detail. Modern social dystopia also loses to the actual experimental premise rather than society-as-such. Soft SF > Social science rather than pseudoscientific psi or pulp superhero/creature adventure: the central experiment is conditioning/psychological development. Within Social science, individual intelligence, personality imprinting, ambition and latent violence make Psychological SF exact; the 1500-child program has social consequences but the subject is the remade individual mind, not a group society designed as the experiment. No seed necessary and no gate widened. Confidence high on temporal failure, moderate-high on home.
