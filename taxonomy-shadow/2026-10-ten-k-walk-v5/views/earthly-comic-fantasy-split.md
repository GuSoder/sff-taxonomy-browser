Not Screwball fantasy (the fantastic agent is a god or myth, not a device or a haunting scrambling a love life); not Comic noir (no gumshoe frame). ## Comic noir  (2 cards)

Earthly comic fantasy told as a detective story: a gumshoe working cases through the marvelous, where the hardboiled frame is the joke. Not Noir fantasy (comedy governs, not mood); not Screwball fantasy or Mythic farce (the frame is the case, not romance or gods).
