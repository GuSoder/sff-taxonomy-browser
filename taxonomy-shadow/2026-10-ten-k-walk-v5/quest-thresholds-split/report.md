## Candidate axes rejected
- Rescue versus war: nearly every series has both, overstates armies in Dreamland and underplays divine combat in Ishtar.
- Threatened world versus threatened person as exclusive event checklist: fails because many personal stories have wide effects. Use governing whole-fiction stakes, with explicit precedence instead.
- Mythic versus secular: inherited gods in Ishtar, Wolfe, Wynne and Lewis cut across the actual adventure pattern; Dreamland would become a leftover.
- Artefact versus built doorway: loses the thematic mission axis and creates hard-to-defend mechanism cases.
- Historical versus contemporary: setting dates do not govern the adventures and it leaves an uneven pile.
- Portal champions: near association with active fantasy game Portals & Champions, and champion overpromises martial heroes. Portal pursuits: a minor game/app phrase, and realm saviors fits poorly with the companion label. No formal trademark clearance claimed.

## Proposed definitions and overlap rule
Realm-saving portals: Anchored-threshold adventures in which crossing draws the protagonists into defending, freeing or preserving an otherworld's people or political order. Personal rescues and return-home plots may launch the story, but the whole fiction becomes a public struggle for the realm.
Personal quest portals: Anchored-threshold adventures organized around finding or rescuing particular people, reuniting a family or lovers, escaping pursuers or surviving a hostile otherworld. The wider realm may be affected, but a public campaign to save or liberate it does not govern the whole fiction.
Boundary: put a fiction in Realm-saving only when the public struggle is sustained and governs the whole fiction. Do not count every victory over a dangerous antagonist as world-saving. Game of Shadows and January are the clearest boundary comparison. Ishtar is martial but personal; Lion begins with a wife rescue but becomes sustained public war.
Dreamland is an inherited work-specific exception to clean ancestor mechanics, not a model for a new general dream-as-doorway rule. It is the main qualification on recommending this split.
