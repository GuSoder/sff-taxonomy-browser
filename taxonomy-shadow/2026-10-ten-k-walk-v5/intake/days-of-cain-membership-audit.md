## Whole novel and scope
One1997novel. Baen's2012ebook is a reissue, not another book or the first publication. The publisher's author catalogue lists three distinct Dunn novels but does not establish a shared universe with This Side of Judgment or Full Tide of Night. Contemporary Kirkus1997 and WSFA1998 criticism corroborate the first publication and standalone story. No connected published novel established.

Gaspar James serves the Moiety's temporal Extension, enforcing continuity. Alma Lewin, whom he trained, leads renegades who first try to assassinate Hitler and then liberate Auschwitz. Gaspar pursues them but confronts the moral cost of preserving atrocity. Prisoner Rebeka and SS officer Reber supply distinct camp and later guilt narratives. The attack temporarily succeeds before the sanctioned team reverses it. This ending is supported by a completed review, not claimed as a full primary novel read.

## Root-down walk
Speculative fiction > Science fiction: interstellar/posthuman civilization, optical implants, holo records, institutional transit stations, control AI and explicitly technological chronon/singularity transfer. The Moiety is mysterious advanced intelligence, not established literal supernatural divinity. Historical evil and religious imagery are not proof of magic. Root confidence high with modest uncertainty about final metaphysical interpretation.
Science fiction > Atemporal SF > Time travel fiction: literal intervention in historical eras. HistoricalSF/alternate-history and future-Earth surfaces lose the any-SF-time-travel rule.
Time travel fiction > Time machines: engineered transfer process, stations and AI control, rather than innate temporal power or natural slip. The legal primary opening itself names the control AI and station subversion; the later opening describes high-energy chronons striking the center's enclosing singularity. Not Mind over time, Time disorder, drug, ancestry-selected travel, repeated-lived loop, differential aging, unexplained door or keeper crossroads. Standing portals are institutionally controlled interfaces, not independent unexplained passages. Physical-science vocabulary is not enough for Hard time travel.
Time machines > Time travel agency: institutional ownership and operations are explicit, with monitors, intendants, novice researchers, security procedures, assignments, retirement and stations. No private inventor/homebrew apparatus.
Time travel agency > Classic time agencies: a1997continuation of the classic institutional temporal-police problem: preserve history versus compassionate intervention. Legal chapter2 follows a full bureaucratic mission briefing, prior suppression attempts, disciplinary/research hierarchy, routine recruitment, transit rules and Gaspar's professional loyalty. Moral inquiry is central, but the shelf already includes philosophical End of Eternity, historical-research Oxford and reflective No Enemy But Time. Classic is not restricted to shallow pulp adventure.
Thrillers remains a real rival because there is a pursuit, assassination and armed raid. However, those are subordinate to the classic continuity-enforcement dilemma and moral transformation, rather than the lab-infiltration/action-conspiracy register that seeded Thrillers. A contemporary completed WSFA review explicitly contrasts the novel with typical Timecop action; the completed Chiseler review explains why an action romp would misrepresent its focus. Post2010Revival fails the actual era/sensibility gate; emotional depth alone cannot erase that gate. Classification confidence moderate-high, not claimed free of register overlap.
