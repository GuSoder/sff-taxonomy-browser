Prehistoric definition: Fantasy set in Earth's imagined prehistory: hunter-gatherer peoples and lost magical civilizations, their gods, visions and struggle to survive. Not Lost world fiction (explorers find a surviving prehistoric world in a later era), not Viking fantasy or Celtic fantasy (historical-era cultures).

Period definition: Historical fantasy anchored to a documented era or Earth's imagined prehistory. Chronicles and recorded history supply the documented eras; the era is the stage, not a puzzle secretly explained or rewritten.
