### Side A - crossing for one loved person (7): proposed name MULTIVERSE LONGING
- In Universes (North 2024): PW: "a scientist named Raffi as they travel the multiverse in
  search of the woman they love... in search of a universe where they had the courage to speak
  to Britt at 13." (publishersweekly.com/9780063314870) CORE.
- The Beautiful Land (Averill 2013): PRH: Tak "explorer of parallel timelines... The only way
  to save her is for Tak to use the time travel device... to transport them both to an
  alternate timeline... to save Sam, he must save the entire world." (penguinrandomhouse.com
  /books/313316) CORE.
- The Possibilities (Goldstein-Love 2023): PW: "Jack disappears from his crib, setting Hannah
  on a race against the clock to find him before he's erased forever." (PW 9780593446485) CORE.
- Bridge (Beukes 2023): laurenbeukes.com: "she becomes convinced her mom is lost out there.
  ... she's not the only one hunting across time and space." NYT 2023-08-07 confirms the
  mother-hunt engine. BOUNDARY: Kirkus headline is "inhabit different versions of yourself" -
  otherselves texture is strong; dominant plot is the hunt for Jo.
- The Singular Life of Aria Patel (Ahmed 2025): Locus 2025-10-08: "Aria's desperation to get
  back in time to save her mother is very real and propels her determination to find out just
  what is at the center of her multiverse jumps." GENUINE BORDERLINE: every landing is another
  Aria's life and the ending is her choice among lives; the engine is the mother.
- The Adjacent (Priest 2013): PW: "this moderately ambitious tale of separated lovers... as
  versions of him and his wife try to find each other." Guardian 2013-06-26: opens with Tarent
  returning after his wife Melanie's killing; Locus (Wolfe 2014): characters doubled/tripled
  (Trent/Tarent/Torrance) across eras. Call: the crossing/adjacency serves separated lovers
  reaching for each other - longing, NOT self-reckoning (no one confronts a double; versions
  of the lovers seek each other). BOUNDARY: braided war/weapons meditation is heavy.
- The Quantum Empirica (Brandt, 3 vols - ALL VERIFIED, full URLs):
  bk1 Threader Origins (https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/612298/threader-origins-by-gerald-brandt/):
  "Pulled from his world by an experiment gone wrong... On an alternate Earth ravaged by war,
  Darwin is torn between the Qabal and SafeHaven, his only goal to find a way back home and
  stop the same fate from happening in his time line." AND, decisive: "In this strange new
  world, Darwin discovers what he could never find on his own: friends, family, love, a mother
  he lost years before, and a younger sister he never had." So bk1's crossing is accidental
  with a warn-home goal, but its payload is the family and love he finds across the worlds.
  (Interview: https://paulsemel.com/exclusive-interview-threader-origins-author-gerald-brandt/)
  bk2 Threader War (https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/612299/threader-war-by-gerald-brandt/):
  "the gate between worlds has opened again, and through it Darwin Lloyd hears the anguished
  screams of Teresa, the love he left behind. He returns to her world... Darwin needs to find
  Teresa, and reverse what he has done before the Qabal take away everything he loves about
  [the] world he has entered." The only CHOSEN crossing in the series is for Teresa.
  bk3 Threader God (https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/612300/threader-god-by-gerald-brandt/
  and https://astrapublishinghouse.com/product/threader-god-9780756416454/): Darwin, scarred,
  travels WITH Teresa seeking a life without Threads; SafeHaven/Forsyth hold the QPS; the Qabal
  build an army to retake it; "another brutal war looming"; his decision is about "his and
  Teresa's lives." No new crossing; the life with Teresa is the stake.
  SERIES CALL: dominant structure = love/found-family spine (Teresa + the mother/sister he
  finds), not war. The Qabal-vs-SafeHaven war is intra-world obstacle, all three books.
  WAR-LEAF TEST (exact defs): multiverse-war = "worlds make war: invasions, colonization
  campaigns and world-repair fought across parallel realities"; invasion = armies/fleets cross
  between worlds; espionage = agents work between worlds by stealth. QE fails all three: the
  worlds never fight each other (only two Earths are connected and they are at peace; every
  war is inside the alternate Earth), and no army or agent crosses to fight - only Darwin
  crosses, for love. MIRROR TEST: "political counterfactuals... mirror on our own history,
  not immersion in the altered one" - bk1's warn-home glimpse is technological, not political,
  and bks 2-3 are full immersion in the altered world; fails mirror. SELVES: no doubles, no
  self-reckoning; fails. Longing is the only child whose def the series positively meets.
  BOUNDARY kept honest: most war-flavored odyssey card; bk1 goal is warn-home, its emotional
  engine the found family; flagged to HIM in the mapping, not as an unresolved weakest-card.

### Side B - crossing confronting the self (5): proposed name MULTIVERSE SELVES
- Mr. Breakfast (Carroll 2023): PW: the Breakfast Tattoo "gives the bearer the ability to
  observe, and ultimately choose between, three different versions of his own life."
  (PW 9781685890889) CORE.
- The Need (Phillips 2019): Singapore Unbound (long plot review 2020-09-21): the intruder is
  Moll, Molly's double from another world; "Molly is made to confront her own humanity through
  the glitch"; the two Mollys reconcile - "it is a mother who ultimately saves herself"; Moll
  "seeks only to return to some semblance of routine". Defense: no one crosses to reach a
  loved one; the double's arrival forces Molly's self-reckoning (motherhood is the CONTENT of
  the reckoning, not its addressee). BOUNDARY: Moll's own motive is family-return.
- The Space Between Worlds (Johnson 2020): Locus (Bourke 2020-10-23): "Cara has a secret.
  She's not this world's Cara at all... taking the place of a Cara who died traversing to her
  world... Throughout, [it] is concerned with identity: who you are, where you come from, who
  you want to be." Wikipedia Themes: "Identity is a central thematic element." Defense: the
  dead-double eligibility is the entry mechanic; the ENGINE is a swapped double living her
  double's life plus doubles of boss/emperor - identity, not a fetch-quest for a person.
  BOUNDARY: relationships-across-versions texture (family, Dell) is real but not the engine.
- The Affirmation (Priest 1981): Wikipedia: two selves, each writing the other's world;
  identities merging; which world is real left ambiguous. CORE.
- Dante's Equation (Jensen 2003): Wikipedia: five travelers flung into separate soul-matched
  worlds (good/evil ratios 60-40...), each forcing the traveler's reckoning with self; they
  return changed. DISCLOSED GAP: crossings involuntary, no literal doubles; fits via what the
  journey serves (self-reckoning) - requires the def widening below.

  crossing need not be chosen - what sorts a book here is what the journey makes them face."
