# Karavans universe: normal intake
2026-10-29

Place Jennifer Roberson's three currently published Karavans novels in Hungry realms, one fiction, leaf4to5. The sentient predatory forest Alisanos engulfs human lands, traps the refugees and remakes their children. Gods/demons and a growing rebellion are substantial strands, but the recurring menace is this consuming enclosure and the family's survival/recovery within it. Godforsaken realms and Dark quest fantasy are real rivals, rejected on the whole published sequence rather than one jacket keyword. No new leaf or changed definition.

## Whole published fiction, every volume
1. Karavans: Audrun/Davyn and four children are displaced by the Hecari conquest. Divination says their fifth child must be born safely in Atalanda, and they join a caravan that must skirt Alisanos. Ensemble viewpoints include Shoia guides Rhuan/Brodhi, their demon keepers, courier Bethid and diviner Ilona. Most of the novel builds this social situation; at the climax the forest moves and swallows people rather than remaining a distant destination. Primary author/publisher and independent reviews agree. https://jenniferroberson.com/books/karavans ; https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/series/C35/karavans-series/ ; https://fantasyliterature.com/reviews/karavans/ ; https://www.ancestralstars.com/wp/sff-books/karavans-by-jennifer-roberson/
2. Deepwood: continues immediately after the engulfing. Rhuan/Audrun and several children are inside; Davyn/Ilona/Bethid/Brodhi remain outside. Audrun's unnaturally early birth produces a mature infant; children are scattered/changed, the newborn taken by a winged demon. Rescue/survival within a landscape of arbitrary magic is crosscut with loss of divinatory power, Shoia rites and the Hecari rebellion outside. The1000gods and their judgment are genuinely important, not ignored to force a hunger reading. But the family is inside because the land has claimed them, and its transforming captivity remains the source of dread. ## Root-down hard walk
Speculative fiction > Fantasy: living intentional landscape, random magic, divination, gods/demons and repeatedly resurrectable Shoia, no scientific engine. Fantasy > Fantasy realms: inventedSancorra/Atalanda/Hecari/Alisanos, not actualEarth or an Earth protagonist crossing elsewhere. A secondary world's crossing into its own strange region does not override root-setting gate. Contemporary haunted-wilds/portal rescue branches therefore fail above their lower resemblances.

Fantasy realms > Gothic realms: captivity, monstrous alteration, dark appetite and uncanny sentient woodland govern the published sequence more than martial adventure, heroic rise or court politics. Epic fantasy is a real higher rival because the world is richly invented and war/rebellion is present. The author's 'epic' marketing and reviewer 'adventure' are acknowledged, but no large kingdom succession/campaign is the family/forest spine. High fantasy's consequential journey is a closer resemblance: first they seek safe birth, then children/rescue. Yet Classic/Dark quest's inherited fellowship/ancient-dark/world-saving pattern is absent, and darkness is not a newly established inner objective or fated power ascent. This is a refugee family overtaken by a place that traps/remakes them, with external rebellion as counterpoint. No claim that hopeful characters or travel prohibit Gothic fantasy; menace and enclosure still govern. Not Sword and sorcery's intimate combat celebration, invented fairytale retelling/task grammar, counterpart-culture immersion, comic realm, or romance/cozy/horse-bond spine.

Gothic realms > Dark realms: the invented world's dread and enclosure, not etiquette, detective inquiry, machinery or an isolationist society protecting itself. Dark realms > Hungry realms: the place itself is sentient, predatory and expands to encompass humans. Existing definition includes a garden or world's order consuming those who enter; no widening is needed for a moving woodland. Characters are consumed/claimed and changed, not necessarily literally digested. The story is what is being eaten/absorbed and what survives. Primary publisher explicitly calls the forest predatory, and author insists Alisanos is a character, not just locale: https://fantasybookcritic.blogspot.com/2007/03/official-jennifer-roberson-website-well.html . Robert Thompson explicitly describes characters consumed by Alisanos and transformed within its grasp, independent of a fabricated physiological feeding claim.

Godforsaken realms is a moderate genuine wobble:1000gods, demons/keepers and Rhuan's divine ancestry do shape the setting and subplots, and the family faces divine judgment. But existing leaf asks whether a dark god/demonic power or its bargain supplies the governing dread. The primary/whole-book accounts consistently foreground the predatory land swallowing/remaking the family, not one fallen god's reign, demon contract or ruin. Demons present is not automatically Godforsaken, just as living forest present is not automatically Hungry. Here the forest's active appetite is the key distinction across all3. Haunted realms fails: no dead/ancestral haunting as main menace. No empty new Forest horror leaf required when this one already fits.
