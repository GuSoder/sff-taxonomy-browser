# Dragon Brigade world, whole-six walk
23 November 2026

## Verdict
Place one connected Dragon Brigade fiction, six published novels, in existing Geopolitical fantasy, two to three. No new genre, definition change, or split. The Dragon Corsairs is explicitly a later trilogy in the same world, not a second fiction. No current duplicate found in full index title/world scan.

## Scope and original years
Shadow Raiders2011; Storm Riders2013; The Seventh Sigil2014; Spymaster2017; Privateer2018; Kingmaker2019. Tor's2020Dragon Corsairs ebundle is not a seventh original. The2017 author news item calls its then-planned next book Oath Breaker; the actual published second title is Privateer, not another novel. Primary author2018news, publisher quartet/trilogy descriptions, contemporaneous PW/Kirkus reviews and ISFDB date records corroborate chronology. Shadow Raiders2012paperback is later than the publisher's2011ebook and contemporaneous2011review. No unverified forthcoming title is counted.

## Whole-fiction content
Shadow Raiders: Freya and Rosia's existing rivalry is destabilized by an apparently impossible magical object and a disappeared magic crafter; Stephano's hired adventurers, Freyan spymaster Henry and Church investigators converge on attacks. Everyday magical technology is real, but the discovery's ability to alter interstate power motivates the factions.

Storm Riders: rival empires have to cooperate against the Bottom Dwellers' contramagic, after five centuries of revenge for an island sunk by magical/religious policy. Freyan and Rosian power brokers still seek national advantage inside the common defense. Church suppression and familial secrets are important subplots.

The Seventh Sigil: Stephano and Henry represent their opposed nations in an alliance against a threat that can destroy the world's magical order. Knowledge deemed heretical is necessary to defeat it; commanders, clergy, captives, dragons and diplomats fill the ensemble.

Spymaster: war over, Freya's fiscal crisis and loss of access to crystalline fuel drive Henry's scheme to attract foreign dragons, while a pretender and a dragon criminal cabal destabilize the court. Kate's privateering and Philip's covert mission operate inside this national contest.

Privateer: tentative peace rests on a Rosian princess's engagement to a Freyan claimant; competing love, secret societies, Dalgren's rescue and Thomas's path to power threaten that arrangement. The acts cross the two governments and Bottom rather than remaining one court's closed spy game.

Kingmaker: a coup in Freya, a prisoner king, threatened war with Rosia and a third country's invasion scheme draw old enemies into new alliances. Restoring Thomas also prevents a wider interstate war. Completed independent reviews differ strongly on quality but agree that these political strands govern.

## Root-down walk
Speculative fiction > Fantasy: actual sigil magic, contramagic and blood magic, supernatural dragons and levitating continents. Not SF merely because a scientific discovery, refineries or dirigibles matter.

Fantasy > Fantasy realms: independently invented Aeronne, with invented countries resembling Europe. Primary publisher compares its gaseous sea with Earth's oceans, not establishing it as Earth. No positive Earth continuity or Earth crossing was found. Real-earth/Period and Portal gates therefore fail. The new Space fantasy is not a label for every magical skyship.

Fantasy realms > Epic fantasy: world-scale war, invented cosmic/magical system and recurring international political order. Sword and sorcery is a serious rival due to hired adventurers, airships and Kate's privateering, but the whole six are an ensemble saga of national power, not just the ship/crew's intimate adventure. Pirate fantasy also requires the sea as its adventure space, not an airy metaphor; the full first trilogy is not Kate's piracy. Technofantasy/Magitech has a genuine magical-infrastructure fit as furniture, but magical engines do not define the recurring narrative across succession, economy, church, invasion and peace. No positive steam-engine evidence supports Steampunk realms over its explicit steam priority; aesthetic steampunk tags are insufficient. The Hexologists precedent is different: its manufactured alchemical industrial fuel and its cost are the concrete investigation mechanism. Here magical infrastructure is the setting and tactical vulnerability, while the changing political order is the sustained prize.

Other realms children: no governing fairy-tale revision, menacing Gothic register, non-Western counterpart culture, comedy, courtship/cozy/horse bond. Romance is substantial in Corsairs but not the governing whole-six form.

Epic > Political fantasy: the Rosia/Freya balance, temporary alliance, postwar weakness and succession's effect on interstate peace unite the two trilogies. Heroic class tree does not contain the variable ensemble. High fantasy's consequential journey is not the recurring narrative; Hard fantasy's scientific understanding of rules is not the whole-six engine; invented divine cosmology is not sustained worship/godhood aboutness. Military fiction is a real first-trilogy rival, but the postwar three do not remain an army, campaign or one fighting unit. Dragons/airborne action alone cannot establish Aerial soldier fantasy's inside-the-unit gate.

Political > Geopolitical fantasy: positive cross-border power contest, not a map or backdrop. Nations' interests organize the plot and affect adventurers, royal lovers, clergy and commoners alike. It is not merely ruler-level governance: Henry and Cecile, privateer Kate, the Cadre, Church investigators and dragons all carry independent political agency. Court intrigue is a strong Corsairs straddle but no single royal house's legitimacy contest explains the earlier multinational common defense. Dynastic spy games/war/magic each take real local strands, not this full universe. Revolutionary fantasy lacks a sustained liberation/remaking engine across six; Bottom Dwellers' revenge does not make them the series's governing emancipatory movement. Election fantasy fails mass franchise. Magic politics has the strong Church/contramagic subplot, but public authority over magic does not organize the later fiscal/succession/alliance trilogy. Geopolitical's stated cross-border axis applies without widening.

## Confidence and caveats
Moderate-to-high positive whole-fiction fit. Magitech, Court intrigue and Military are substantial straddles, not dismissed as absent. Whole published content was checked through primary descriptions plus completed professional/reader criticism, not a cover-to-cover reading. The independently invented-world root is a supported setting finding, not a newly invented exception for an unverified Earth future. No need for a structural proposal.
