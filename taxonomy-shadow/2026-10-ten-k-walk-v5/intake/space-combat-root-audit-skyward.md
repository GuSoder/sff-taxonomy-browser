## Every novel
Skyward: Spensa, daughter of a supposedly cowardly dead pilot, trains to fly in the human enclave on Detritus while rebuilding M-Bot with her mechanic friend. Her skill, her father's actual fate, and her cytonic nature unfold within repeated combat against the Krell/Superiority. The academy is an opening formation, not the whole five-book engine.
Starsight: Spensa infiltrates the Superiority under an alien identity, finds its supposedly monstrous human enemy image a political construction, encounters Cuna and other species, and faces the planet-destroying delvers. Her psychic engagement changes a delver's view of life rather than killing all the opposition. Space espionage is a real volume-level engine but not the whole fiction.
Cytonic: Spensa enters the Nowhere, navigates the Path of Elders with M-Bot and Chet, works with and fights pirate factions, and learns the delvers' AI origin. Memories of dead wife, grief, self-deletion and replication explain Chet/delvers, not independent supernatural gods. The second portal supplies a distinct actual temporal element: an ancient cytonic woman feels Spensa's present questions and leaves answers in the portal, responding even to the changed circumstance of millions rather than one delver. Coppermindchapter24gives the concrete exchange; independently fetched SuperSummary explicitly calls it across-time communication. The first portal's passive fast-forwarded memory, long-lived inhabitants and FTL shortcuts do not independently establish this gate. Spensa's bodily location remains the present ruin while her consciousness makes the temporal contact. No claim she bodily journeys to ancientEarth or rewrites history. Chet joins Spensa's consciousness at the end and she returns to Detritus.
Defiant: Spensa's alliance confronts Winzik's interstellar regime and its delver weapon, raids for information/acclivity resources, and develops a resolution through individual minds and delvers rather than an ordinary naval fleet campaign. The ending frees interstellar travel from the old political order and sends exploration outward. Luna/OldEarthhistory grounds the universe in realEarth's future, not an independent secondary fantasy realm. Portal memories remain memories, not every use time travel.
Blightfall: after the war, Arturo and Sadie of Skyward Flight escort diplomats to an isolated human settlement, are ambushed, find themselves stranded between local political factions and a rogueAI, and fight their way through an arena/adventure plot. A romance thread and pilot-career anxiety accompany the action. Primary publisher, Kirkus and completed reviews agree it is a new Skyward-universe novel, not excluded novella content. Latest book does not erase the temporal event in the includedCytonic.
