# Lenhardt (MC06680-82) skipped, 27 November 2026

Badlands, Blood Oath and Sawbones are the Laura Elliston (Sawbones) trilogy: a historical western, Dr. Catherine Bennett fleeing a murder charge in 1871 and practising on the Texas and Colorado frontier. Reviews and the publisher blurb show no magic, no speculative science and no alternate history, so it sits outside the speculative-fiction taxonomy (same call as The Wasp Factory and A Man in the Zoo). The rows came from a Hachette keyword search, which is not genre-pure.
