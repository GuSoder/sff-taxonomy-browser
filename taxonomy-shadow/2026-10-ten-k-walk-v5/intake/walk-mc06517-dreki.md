## Whole novel
TwelveyearoldJohan leaves Minneapolis to stay with his82yearoldgrandfatherSiggy while hisparents travel. After a fall Siggy reveals a longhidden dragonDreki in the basement, hatched from an Icelandicegg he brought to America as an immigrant. The hatching on Johan'sbirthday links boy and creature. The need to return Dreki to Iceland before worseninghealth becomes urgent makes the familysecret into a journey. Magic helps hide thedragon during practicalairtravel, rather than a portal taking them to an inventedrealm.

In Iceland, Dreki begins growing again; Johan meets localgirlElva and explores his grandfather's homeland. Siggy develops pneumonia and is hospitalized, forcing Johan to take responsibility rather than rely on his familiar companion. Johan contacts hisparents and hismother fliesout. A storm accompanies the dragonrelease mission. SchoolLibraryJournal describes the return to Dreki'srightful ArcticOceanhome; Kirkus calls the conclusion wistful and satisfying. The full librarianreview explicitly reveals Siggy dies ofpneumonia. Thus aging/grief/legacy and the boy assuming responsibility persist through theending; this is not just a whimsical basementpet opening. No invented resurrection, dragonwar or supernaturalvillain.
