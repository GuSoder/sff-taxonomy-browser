## Actual root-down walk
Speculative fiction > Fantasy > Portal fantasy > Fantasy rifts > Multiverse fantasy > Mirror multiverse fantasy.
Literal dragon prophecy, deity and magical claw crossing, not a scientific transfer. Earth is included, explicitly actual1957 in the destination, so the Portal ancestor passes. Fantasy realms fails the Earth criterion. Real Earth > Historical > Alternate history > Alt diesel describes Part1 but misses the whole book's organized exchange and counterfactual second chances. Contemporary/Future Earth fail period. No hidden-history explanation of recorded history.
Portal children tested: not one-way Otherworld fantasy revealing a mythic realm, not Sword and planet or a transported book. The worlds are parallel counterparts with duplicate people and alternate outcomes, not unrelated realms joined only by a gateway. The Mirror definition explicitly takes parallel Americas and the traveler who meets another self; no invented minimum of three worlds is imposed. Borderland/Worldhopper therefore lose to Mirror. The first-half dragon-alt-history is a genuine moderate wobble and remains recorded, but no longer determines the whole novel's only shelf.
