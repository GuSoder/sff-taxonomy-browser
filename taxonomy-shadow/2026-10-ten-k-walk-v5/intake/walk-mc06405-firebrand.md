# MC06405 The Firebrand, 24 November 2026

## Verdict
The Firebrand, Marion Zimmer Bradley,1987. One standalone novel, Greek hero fantasy7->8. Do not join Avalon from shared author, goddess motifs or similar feminist-retelling method. No narrative identity/sequel bridge established by creator/catalog/completed criticism inspected. No media tie-in or short-form issue; original first-edition record is608pages.

## Root-down walk
Speculative fiction > Fantasy: Kassandra's prophetic Sight and curse, divine conflict and the surviving Kentaurs are treated as real. No technological explanation or final staged-hoax reversal in completed sources. Her survival after Troy changes a received myth, not a machine-mediated timeline. No SF/Atemporal shortcut from prophecy.

Fantasy > Real earth fantasy, not Fantasy realms: named Troy, Colchis, Mykenae and the Aegean legendary past. Not Portal fantasy: travel is within the ancient Earth and mythical peoples, not Earth/another-world exchange.

Real earth > Historical fantasy: the Trojan legendary past, not contemporary retelling transplanted to modern life and not Future. Historical > Mythic retelling: named Homeric and later Kassandra narratives are deliberately reshaped. The myth is the source, not just stage. Period fantasy/Ancient Greece is for historical Greece without mythic narrative as its source; Secret history's concealed explanation of documented history and Alternate history's divergent historical timeline are less exact. The feminist perspective is an argument about inherited myth, not evidence of an Arthurian story.

Mythic retelling > Classical myth fantasy > Greek myth fantasy > Greek hero fantasy. Kassandra and the Trojan human survivors are the viewpoint and main narrative; Apollo and the Serpent Queen's powers condition them, but the immortals' own society is not the story. Greek god fantasy has the broad phrase mortals whose fates they hold, a genuine overlap. Greek hero fantasy explicitly includes Troy and the houses of Atreus; mortal mythical figures and heroic cycles include a seer/princess, not only sword-wielding men. Roman myth fails despite Aeneas appearing: Troy/Kassandra governs, not Rome's founding. No other myth tradition fits. No romance, war, Amazon martial or priesthood subdivision overrides this inherited mythic-source route.

## Whole-ending evidence
Kirkus's completed1987 review follows Kassandra through Amazon fostering, Colchis initiation, Apollo service, Troy's fall and escape from the expected death at Klytemnestra's hands. She leaves with Zakynthos rather than being killed. Independent completed2012 review confirms post-Troy/Mykenae survival and the broadly female-centered revision, not just publisher setup. No claim of reading the complete primary novel. Confidence high for root/source/leaf, moderate on exact divine ontology because secondary completed reviews supply the ending.
