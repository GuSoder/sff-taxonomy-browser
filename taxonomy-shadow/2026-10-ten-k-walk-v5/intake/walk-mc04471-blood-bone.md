## Root-down hard walk
Speculative fiction > Fantasy: angels/demons/gods are literal actors, no scientific explanation. Fantasy > Real earth fantasy: actual contemporaryArizona, world we know, not invented realm or Earthprotagonist entering a secondaryworld. Real earth fantasy > Contemporary fantasy: author's first current-world series, modern bikes/parole/popculture, not a named past era nor a future remadeEarth. End-times fantasy is a genuine local thematic resemblance, but fails its Future fantasy parent. HistoricalBiblicalretelling/WeirdWest likewise fail era gate. Mythic characters from history are present-day manifestations rather than the actual past setting.

Contemporary fantasy > Urban fantasy: hidden supernatural systems working through modern life and demon-hunting local missions in a small city. Modern biblical myth is a genuine higher rival with angels/Heaven/EndDays, mixed with Celtic/Chinese/African/Greekbeings. But the series aboutness is the enlisted pair's ongoing protective work, not mainly biblical powers acting openly inside ordinarylife. An organizational mission distinguishes it from a general mythic-cosmology story. No claim that mythic pluralism rules out biblicalmyth by itself. Also reject LitRPG/systemasgame, magic realism's accepted marvels, wish/household/maturedabilitystory, or nonhuman-animal/cozy/comic engine.

Urban fantasy partition: Modern Weird West is a real close fit, not mere superficial cowboy keyword: presentFlagstaff/NorthernArizona, rodeo/ranchorigins, desertvolcanoes, secret monsters and regionalghoststories.
