## Proposed carve and reciprocal boundaries

Eco fantasy under Epic fantasy. Definition: Imaginary-world epic fantasy whose governing conflict is the interdependence of people and the living environment: understanding, exploiting, damaging or repairing the ecosystems that sustain them. Ecological systems and the consequences of living within them organize the whole fiction, rather than supplying scenery or a one-off obstacle.

Not Heroic: protagonist's magical/classpower is a tool, not story'sorganizingpoweridentity. Not High: ajourney may carrythestory,but ecologicalrelationship ratherthan inheritedquesttraditionoritsrevision governs. Not Political: regimes/resourcecontrol canmatter,but ecosysteminteractionratherthan struggleforofficeoroverthrow determineswhole. Not Hard: nature/constraints neednotexplicitrulesforspells; scientificrulemagic remains therewhenprimary. Not Mythological: nature spirits/gods mayexist,but experienceofdivinity/faith/inheritedmyth notspine. Not Military: environmentalcrisis not campaign/ranks. Not Pirate/S&S: seas/crews maycarryaction, but ecologicalorder ratherthan privateeringnavaladventureisaboutness. Not Gothic: scaryecologyisnot sufficient; dreadregistergoverningstillgoesGothic. Not RealEarth/Portal: ancestorforcesinventedworld only. Noexistinggatewidening.
