# Jon and Lobo, whole-fiction hard walk
24 November 2026

## Verdict
Place one connected five-novel Jon and Lobo fiction by Mark L. Van Name in existing Space caper,7to8fictions. Moderate confidence at the caper/combat/mystery boundary. Positive choice is the repeated covert rescue, infiltration, extraction and retrieval job, worked through planning, disguises, manipulation of local factions and counter-cons. Fighting is real, not erased, but is the operational hazard and fallback rather than a sustained military campaign. No new genre or definition change.

## Complete-story evidence
One Jump Ahead: Jon is hired to rescue a kidnapped girl amid two corporations' struggle over a jump gate. He is manipulated into delivering her to the wrong hands, then must undo the job while hunted and aided by Lobo and a former lover/mercenary contact. He acquires and repairs the AI assault vehicle in this novel. Publisher and completed omnibus reviews establish the rescue/double-cross structure, though fighting ends the confrontation.
Slanted Jack: a protection job for a con man's supposedly unusual boy becomes a multi-sided fraud involving a cult, gang boss, illegal arms and government. Jon uses information gained by talking to mundane machines and tries to outmaneuver the factions without a bloodbath. Completed reviews emphasize the triple-cross scheme and deliberately limited killing.
Overthrowing Heaven: a seemingly personal rescue is folded into a covert job to penetrate Dr. Wei's organization, find a planted mole and extract a scientist conducting experiments on abducted children. Jon poses as a member of the lab's security force and works through a courtesan/local helper. Lobo's own origin is involved. The climax does use real bullets, and the personal ending is painful. SFFWorld and Fantasy Book Critic completed reviews establish the full operational arc rather than only the initial abused-woman synopsis.
Children No More: the initial rebel-fort assault rescues child soldiers, but Jon stays after the contracted fighting is over to help their rehabilitation. Author interview explicitly calls this a deliberate refusal of the special-skills hero's usual arrival/fight/departure pattern. The continuing threat from the government is defused through a con with Slanted Jack. This is the least action-driven volume, and its ethical aftermath is a substantial qualification to any simple combat classification.
No Going Back: following a child-auction rescue, Jon takes an illegal job to retrieve artifacts from his quarantined home world, with a scientist's agenda and pursuing security forces complicating the operation. Publisher/distributor synopsis, completed reviews and Lobo's own interludes connect the retrieval to Jon's childhood secrets and deteriorating self-control. Reviewers describe a return to adventure after the fourth book, with consequential revelations at the end. No full-book mirror obtained; finer final-scene mechanics not invented.

## Root-down walk
Speculative fiction > Science fiction: nanotechnology, regenerative augmentation, AI machines, interstellar gates and genetically engineered creatures supply explicit speculative-science premises. Alleged precognition/psychic mutation belongs to the book's scientific speculative register; no verified literal god, spell or supernatural cosmology overrides it. The dragons on Heaven are bioengineered, not proof of magic.
Science fiction > Futurist SF: human-descended interstellar civilization in a distant future, explicitly spread from Earth. Not present-day or past-era SF. Jon's century-old memories are flashbacks, not time travel; a long life does not force Atemporal. Gates cross space, not verified alternate worlds/timelines. No invented time machine.
Futurist > Spacefaring fiction: recurring work across settled worlds, interstellar governments/corporations and a traveling man/AI vehicle partnership. Not Alien fiction: abandoned gate-makers are setting infrastructure, not first-contact/alien-mind investigation. Not Hard SF: technology aids operations rather than rigorous engineering constraints governing the reading experience. Future-of-Earth/posthuman/cyborg and psionics tempt through Jon's body and hidden origin, but the core five books repeatedly organize these abilities around jobs in interstellar civilization, not an Earth social order, species transition or scientific study of psi. Techno-fiction in space is likewise a real identity rival but not the dominant operational form.
Spacefaring > Space opera: mobile interstellar adventures, predatory corporations, covert operations and personal-scale action. Not military-science-fiction as primary: Jon is an ex-soldier/mercenary, but the active books are private protection, rescue, extraction and retrieval jobs, not a corps, regiment, command campaign or naval war. Space mercs requires a hired fighting unit's contracts/pay/loyalty and field war; one problem-solver and AI do not become a regiment by being paid. Super space soldier requires a ground-pounder's war remaking the soldier; Jon's childhood alteration is backstory and ethical pressure across non-campaign jobs. Space navy/academy/command and alien-war grunts fail their positive modes.
Space opera > New space opera:2007onward self-aware revival, humane and damaged super-agent against corporate/government power, explicit noir influences, technological identity and consequences. Classic pulp register is a tonal influence acknowledged, not actual vintage or an Evergreen economic grant.
New > Space adventure: danger and action in successive missions govern rather than elite politics, empire liberation, theology or a romance/found-family crew's ordinary life. The sympathetic Jon/Lobo friendship matters but does not make Spaceship drama the plot spine. Frontier worlds and guns do not establish a primarily western register. Political struggles are the jobs' surrounding forces, not institution-building or governing as the main action.
Space adventure > Space caper: the repeated unit of story is a job with a hidden agenda, solved by assembling information, infiltration, identities, plans and reversals. First rescue is undone by a corporate double-cross; second is explicitly a triple-cross con; third is a disguised entry/extraction; fourth turns away from the fight to outwit a government; fifth is illegal artifact retrieval. This is not a claim that each novel is a comic bank robbery or bloodless. The existing leaf explicitly includes covert jobs, not only heists.

## Rival leaves and honest wobble
Space combat is strongest: nine existing fictions, positive armor/war-machine/mercenary action, and several climactic fights. Reject based on content, not to avoid its tenth-card split. Completed reviews consistently show the actual task is saving/protecting/extracting, with Jon minimizing deaths and the fourth novel's real work beginning after the assault. The recurring counter-scheme is more specific than combat as physical danger. If text evidence showed the decisive experience to be battlefield engagements with schemes merely setup, this verdict would change and Space combat's tenth would trigger its split immediately.
Space chase > Space dash has bounties and pursuit but Jon repeatedly chooses to run an operation rather than flee as continuing life pattern. Space hunt > Interstellar manhunt has missing children/scientist targets, and Galactic treasure hunt fits the fifth book's artifacts, but a changing trail/quarry is not the series-wide rhythm: the adversaries/target are often known and protected, requiring a covert approach and reversal.
Space colonies > Space mystery > Space PI passes the off-Earth-settlement and unofficial-fixer conditions. The author's2011interview explicitly calls every book an SF mystery and names Hammett, Chandler and Travis McGee as major influences. This is substantial evidence, not ignored marketing. Yet the custom tree distinguishes solving a case from running the operation. These books' repeated core is not retrospectively identifying who committed a crime, diagnosing an unknown or unearthing a buried event; it is carrying out then correcting a dangerous protection/rescue/extraction job. The novel-three mole and hidden origins are true mysteries, but not enough to make PI the canonical whole-fiction home against the five operational arcs. Same reasoning prevents assigning Cybernoir operatives: that would require the Future-of-Earth techno/cyberpunk ancestor rather than using noir voice alone to bypass Spacefaring. No new global rule or other-member audit implied.
Dystopian corporations are setting pressures rather than a closed oppressive settlement as the story's subject. Lobo is a ship and person, not enough for AI/posthuman leaf when the work engine is adventure. No seed required, and Space caper7to8does not trigger a split.
