## Whole story and resolution
Maskelle, formerly Voice of the Adversary, returns from exile at the summons of the Celestial One. The annual sand mandala maintains the world; this Hundred Year Rite has unusual power. A dark storm keeps returning despite repairs. With swordsman Rian and a troupe of actors, she investigates corruption of the Wheel, a dead priest and an incapacitated replacement, uncanny attacks and a possessed puppet. Court officials and the newcomer Marada complicate inquiry. The threat is not merely local politics: opponents have constructed another Wheel and their ruined world overwrites the protagonists' reality. The apparent identity and reliability of the Adversary are themselves part of the mystery, connected to Maskelle's earlier disastrous vision.
The catastrophe actually happens rather than remaining a threatened climax. Maskelle, Rian and companions survive it and restore their world through the Rite; the Adversary is transformed into the human child Siri, whose upbringing Maskelle and Rian take responsibility for. The broad restoration and changed divine/human relationship are supported by completed professional reviews plus reader spoilers. The exact metaphysics of each final step are not independently reconstructed from primary novel text and are not invented here. No actual journey across historical eras or time loop is demonstrated by the sources. A hundred-year ritual is not time travel.

## Root-down hard walk
1. Speculative fiction: sand-image alteration physically changes the world, spirits act and possess, and a whole alien reality intrudes. These are actual events rather than a realist superstition account.
2. Fantasy rather than SF: creator and current publisher frame fantasy, independent Celestial Empire/Duvalpore and real magical priestly power. No Earth location, future Earth origin or scientific demystification established. Buddhist/Khmer/Tibetan resonances are cultural analogies, not evidence this is historical Earth.
3. Fabulation exception examined first, not ignored. The owner's Major Arcana exchange of29September concerns narrative causality revision, a comic/occult frame and people editorially changing their causal past. Wheel's sand image is real and changes geography/existence, but is a bounded sacred magic instrument in a conventional invented-world conspiracy, not narration, recording or an authored fiction crossing into life. Its people are solving an attack within a stable magical/divine ontology. Projected worlds is the strongest SF-side rival because an image makes a world, but there is no projection/replay/recording or foregrounded arbitrariness of storytelling. Retconned worlds also fails positive evidence of a changed causal past. Godgame tempts because of supernatural rival powers, but the book is not a fabulation of hidden reality as a game. The tree's Golden Key precedent demonstrates that reality-altering art can be symbolic magic without automatically being fabulation; it is a comparison, not a new user ruling. No lower keyword overrides root.
4. Fantasy realms, not Real earth or Portal: independent world; the overwritten city/world is not an Earth-to-otherworld crossing. No evidence for a historical-Earth temporal shelf.
5. Fantasy realms siblings: Gothic realms wins for uncanny conspiracy and investigation. Epic is a serious rival, with world-threatening stakes, invented mythology and divine service. But the whole-book narrative question is who is corrupting the Wheel, how, and how to stop them; the inquiry persists through the alien-city endgame rather than being a brief opening device. Sword and sorcery has action and rescue, not the governing combat/expedition story. Fairytale revision/grammar absent, far-shores culture not the central about-ness, comic mode absent, romance is a substantial strand but not coequal governing spine, cozy/horse absent.
6. Epic rivals in detail: Invented worship is the nearest because Maskelle's service, exile, apparent divine absence and renewed divine/human relation matter from start to finish. Invented godhood is weaker, no sustained divine point of view or experience of being a god. Mythlike past loses to on-page divine action; real-tradition Mythic power struggle fails because these are invented ancestors/gods, not a retelling of Buddhist deities. Katha is not an automatic Buddhism shelf, and Khmer/Southeast-Asian architecture is not established South-Asian cultural about-ness. Scripted hard magic has a genuine sand-inscription mechanism, but sources do not establish a rigorously learnable system with explicit costs as the governing reader contract: divine reliability, mystery and moral judgment dominate, with several ontology revelations left hazy. A magic ritual being precise does not alone pass Hard fantasy. Time magic fails no demonstrated temporal domain. Political intrigue is an obstacle and red herring, not the long-term power struggle that governs. Heroic mage class is less exact than official occult case. High quest is not the sustained journey form after reaching Duvalpore.
7. Gothic realms siblings: Fantasy mystery wins. Necromancy engineering not demonstrated; mandala ritual is not technological apparatus, so Technomancy loses. Dark realms has genuine dread, haunting and bodily possession, but these are clues/threats in a solved case rather than dread governing over investigation. Isolationist lacks isolation as world's premise, though the cast becomes trapped. Manners not the engine.
8. Fantasy mystery leaves: Detective fantasy because the priestess returns by order of the Celestial One to solve the institution's crisis, a formal religious public duty despite her exiled status. The case includes official temple deaths, magical sabotage and an institutional rite, not hired client work. Private-eye loses no hired gumshoe role; Ghost-sleuth loses no dead investigator/witness structure governing the solution. Institutional detective need not mean a badge: current definition includes official duty and a scholar bound to a trial.
