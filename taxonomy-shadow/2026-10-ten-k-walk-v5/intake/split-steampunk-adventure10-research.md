## Recommendation
Split by the kind of setting the adventure inhabits: a recognizable historical Earth, however altered, versus a fantasy-world setting with its own geography/civilization rather than Earth history as its frame.7/3, one invariant axis across all eligible volumes.

Naming unresolved. Parent rejected Steamcapade and Adventurbine: neither reads cold as a genre. No best pair is recommended. Ordinary Earth steampunk adventures / Realm steampunk adventures reads clearly but violates current NAMES no-three-word-stack rule; Steampunk Earth / Steampunk realms repeats parent first word and drops adventure, also forbidden. No exemption invented. Existing word Vernean is attested but narrows expectation to Verne/scientific-voyage pastiche, unsupported across crime/sorcery-heavy cluster, so rejected. Parent asked Gustav for a plain word evoking steam machinery plus adventure at19:26. No Approve block, no split authorization. Intake holds at10pending naming answer.

Definition draft, Earth: Steampunk character adventures in a recognizable historical Earth: its countries, cities, historical people and cultures remain the setting frame even when technology, geography or cosmology diverges radically. Journeys may leave Earth for other planets. Not steam-era adventures whose operating setting is a fantasy world with its own geography rather than Earth history.
Definition draft, realms: Steampunk character adventures in a fantasy-world setting with its own lands, cultures and powers, rather than recognizable Earth history as the operating frame. No claim about an unrevealed cosmological origin is required. Not adventures that retain recognizable historical Earth even when its technology or physical rules are radically altered.

- Cinder Spires: setting presentation established, physical-world origin unestablished. Definition must use setting frame, not metaphysical Earth/non-Earth certainty.

- Space Machine visits Mars, but anchored Victorian Earth. No mistaken Earth-only travel restriction.
- Mystery vs quest: unstable in Gideon/Newbury/Baskerville/Dagger; cannot advertise invariant plot labels.
- City vs sky: city/surface/airship travel cross most series.
