# The Great Undead War - whole-fiction walk, 2026-11-06

Place one fiction with three published novels in Alt diesel fantasy (8 -> 9). No gate changes or existing moves. Moderate-to-high wobble: the 2026 LitRPG spinoff imports a modern soldier into alternate 1918, while the older pair is a pulp military rescue story in an extended Great War. This is not three novels with an identical engine.

## Scope and every novel
- By the Blood of Heroes (2012): Burke's team must recover American ace Freeman from the undead Red Baron. Corpse gas is the public scientific explanation of zombies; the occult blood-relative assassination threat against the president is separate magic evidence. Reviews from Alternate History Weekly Update and Nameless Zine independently describe it. Tesla gadgets, clockwork arm and dirigibles are genuine steampunk machinery.
- On Her Majesty's Behalf (2014): direct continuation within weeks; rescue British royals from zombie London, with undead former squadmate opposing Burke. Nameless Zine describes mystical objects beneath the British Museum, explicitly not used here, so this is supporting evidence and not a claim of a demonstrated spell. Harper's own page confirms December 2014, correcting misleading 2013 search labels.
- Operation Shambling Tide (2026): creator expressly says it is set in his Great Undead War universe; current series page lists it; February release announcement and completed 300-page review establish publication. Solomon Drake, born 1992, is wounded in Afghanistan and wakes in 1918 with stats and XP. His modern knowledge and engineered response to zombie control collars govern the mission. Completed review identifies Madman Burke and Graves, and explicitly says the transfer/HUD cause is unresolved at the ending. Do not claim a literal computer simulation, time machine, divine summoning or return trip.
The Sharp End #0.5 is a short prequel, not an added novel. Author references to a planned main trilogy or older five-book plan are not publication evidence. No unpublished main third volume invented.

## Root-down walk
Speculative fiction -> Fantasy: not merely an assumed fantasy classification from zombies. Corpse gas by itself could be SF. The occult long-distance blood assassination threat and museum's mystical artifacts support actual occult worldbuilding beyond the chemical premise. Both are less overt than the zombie war and that uncertainty remains visible.
Fantasy -> Real earth fantasy: altered Europe, France, London and historical personages, not an independent secondary world. Fantasy realms fails at its setting gate, even though action-forward Technofantasy, Steampunk realms or Technomancy would describe parts of the texture. Comedy and Heart fantasy do not govern this fiction.
Portal fantasy is a serious alternate route for Operation, including the creator's release wording 'Portal fantasy without isekai power creep'. The whole shared fiction is not organized around world exchange: the original pair is about soldiers native to the alternate history; the spinoff adds one unexplained transfer. No anchored threshold or transporting book; no mythic hidden realm, planet journey or leaking world. A system of many counterpart worlds is not established, so Mirror multiverse is not a safe inference merely because a reviewer calls the destination an alternate universe.
Real earth -> Historical fantasy: most action across all three novels is in the altered Great War. Contemporary fails despite Afghanistan in the spinoff's opening; Future fails despite the originals being set after their historical war endpoint.
Historical -> Alternate history fantasy: German corpse gas publicly changes the war from 1917, prolonging it into 1921, followed by zombie London and the Red Baron's seizure of the Kaiser position. This is open historical divergence, not secret magical truth behind documented events. Secret history fails; Mythic retelling fails despite a Greek-myth twist. Period fantasy -> Industrial age -> Electrified -> World wars -> WWI fantasy is the closest rival, but its documented 1914-18 stage is less honest for a war deliberately continued and rewritten into 1921.
Alternate history children: Alt crusade, Ming, spice route, Renaissance, flintlock and swashbuckler all fail era/place gates. Alt diesel matches machine-age twentieth-century Europe and an openly rewritten war with occult power alongside machinery. 'Diesel' is an era label here, not a claim that these steam-led gadgets run on diesel engines. No definition widened.
SF counterwalk: Timeslips -> Time strays -> Timestranded would describe Drake's involuntary era crossing if the whole fiction passed the SF root. LitRPG would describe its stats, but cyberpunk ancestor is unproved and two novels have no game engine. Owner's exact September 23 rule says 'when in sci fi, anything time travel goes Atemporal' (phonemsg-01M36M8K4DB3B8Y8T1GAQ6ARF0). Fantasy root comes first. Bill and Slow Gods exceptions are not generalized. Here the crossing is substantial, not dismissed as a blip; it is recorded as the fiction's main straddle.
