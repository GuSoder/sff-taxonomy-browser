# Brooks form revalidation, MC06586-588
25 November 2026

All three rows excluded, not held for a new genre. No blanket graphic-novel exclusion is asserted.

- MC06586 The Harlem Hellfighters (2014): author description is the historical story of the369thinfantry inWWI and racial discrimination, not an identified speculative revision. Non-speculative historical fiction excluded at intake root. Graphic presentation is not the reason.
- MC06587 The Zombie Survival Guide (2003): mock survival handbook, not a novel. Publisher describes practical-format advice on zombie physiology, defense and surviving terrain. Speculative subject matter is real, but the standing novel/form gate excludes the guide format, not zombies or mock documents in general.
- MC06588 The Zombie Survival Guide: Recorded Attacks (2009): publisher markets it as a graphic novel. Independent comics review identifies twelve separate short comic accounts/anecdotes across history rather than one sustained novel. Exclude as collected short pieces under the standing collections gate, with publisher terminology conflict retained. Do not infer that every graphic novel is excluded.
