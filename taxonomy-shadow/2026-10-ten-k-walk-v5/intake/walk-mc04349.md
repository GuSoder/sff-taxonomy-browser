# MC04349 Zero World,2026-10-28

Walk: Speculative fiction > Science fiction > Atemporal SF > Parallel-world fiction > Multiverse war > Multiverse espionage. Engine: cybernetically enhanced assassin Peter Caswell crosses a rift to the Earth twin Gartien to pursue Alice Vale, who is transferring advanced knowledge into its North-South conflict; local southern spy Melni targets the same scientist for technological secrets. Their covert missions and pursuit remain the plot engine through Caswell's memory reset and the final operation (publisher; Bookreporter; SFFWorld; Kelly Jensen). Rejected Fantasy: technological implant and science-fiction wormhole, not magic. Rejected Historical/Modern-day/Futurist SF as the organizing shelf: the operative crosses parallel realities, rather than remaining in one era of one Earth; not time travel. Rejected Multiverse mirror: differences between Earths supply setting, but covert intervention rather than comparison drives events. Rejected Multiverse odyssey: not a personal-longing or self-discovery crossing. Rejected Multiverse invasion: two spies and technological interference, not armies crossing openly. Wobble low-moderate: covert geopolitical competition rather than declared inter-world war, but the leaf explicitly includes stealth and sabotage.
