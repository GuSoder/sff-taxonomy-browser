# Marr walk, 27 November 2026

MC06683, MC06686, MC06687 excluded short fiction; MC06684-85 placed.

Root-down: Speculative fiction > Fantasy > Real earth fantasy > Contemporary fantasy > Magical coming-of-age > Magic school. Queue MC06685 (Remedial Magic, 2024) and MC06684 (Reluctant Witch, 2025), the two books of Marr's A Course in Magic. FictionDB, readingorderlist and the author's site list exactly these two; the author describes them as a pair of fantasy novels in a new world, and more books are only 'planned', so nothing else to itemise. Ellie, a small-town Pennsylvania librarian, discovers she is a witch and must attend magical community college in Crenshaw to learn the craft and save her new world; Reluctant Witch continues in the same magical land and college community. The craft is taught in a school, so Magic school; the students are adults, as with Strange Familiars and The Incandescent already in the leaf. Rejected: Romantasy (the romance is real but the school and its instruction drive the plot, and the setting is a hidden land reached from contemporary Earth), Occult order (no initiatory secret society). Standing scope: both are novels (336-page paperback), so they are in. The queue's other Marr rows, Of Roses and Kings (8,097-word Tor.com novelette, ISFDB SHORTFICTION), The Maiden Thief (Tor.com novelette) and Two Lines (2011 short story), are short fiction and excluded, as are her collections and the Wicked Lovely, Graveminder and Blackwell Pages universes, which are not in the queue except as short fiction.
