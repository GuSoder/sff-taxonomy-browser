# Spectre War whole-fiction walk, 23 November 2026

Verdict: existing Space combat. One connected fiction, three published Margaret Fortune novels: Nova (2015), Archangel (2017), Iolanthe (2022). No new leaf or definition change. Space combat becomes nine after the directly encountered Dire Earth duplicate consolidation, not ten. The source ledger records the substantial evidence limits.

## Scope
The author's current home/books pages and both publisher series pages explicitly list three novels. Five was a projected sequence, not five published novels. Iolanthe2025 in the queue is the audiobook; the publisher distinguishes the22February2022 ebook. SFE's Archangel2015 is contradicted by primary2017 and contemporary reviews. The unrelated Delany Nova is not a duplicate. Whole shared-world rule keeps these three together.

## Root and time gates
Speculative fiction > Science fiction > Futurist SF > Spacefaring fiction > Space opera > New space opera > Space adventure > Space combat.

Science fiction, not Fantasy: Lia is an engineered human weapon with implanted memories; later books depict alien reproduction, detection and anti-alien weapons. The authorized Iolanthe sample directly explains the Spectres as incorporeal aliens breeding in human hosts, not literal dead spirits. It also gives the rainforest a terraforming origin. Shar is described as psychic in this scientific space-opera world; no read source establishes actual magic, gods, otherworld portals or supernatural haunting. The spectral vocabulary alone is not a Fantasy verdict. If later complete-text evidence established literal magic, the root would need review.

Futurist passes the interstellar human polities, stations and colonized planets. Historical SF fails a recognizable past-period aesthetic; Modern day SF fails the writer-present Earth setting; Atemporal fails a governing time-travel, parallel-world or unstable-reality frame. Memory implantation and countdown clocks are not time travel. Spacefaring is a sustained setting and conflict, not incidental travel. Hard SF loses because rigorous scientific constraint/problem-solving is not the reading engine. Future of Earth fiction loses its Earth-society center; this is not the present-day alien invasion shelf. Alien fiction, especially Alien horror, is a real rival: host possession and fatal gestation create dread. But the whole sequence follows human protagonists' discovery and armed response to a war, rather than alien mind/culture, contact, cosmic history, megastructure exploration or horror as its overriding purpose. No claim that alien horror requires Earth.

## Spacefaring sibling test
Military SF is the strongest rival. Archangel explicitly follows Guardian Michael in evacuation, weapons field tests, sabotage and battle. Its completed reviewers independently describe the changed military feel. Ground-pounders > Alien-war grunts would be the best military leaf for that novel, but the whole fiction is not consistently the foot soldiers' mud: Nova follows a memory-bearing human bomb living among civilians, Iolanthe a school exile and ragtag civilian resistance. Ground-pounders' Super space soldier also overweights Lia's engineered body and ignores the rest; Space marine corps and Space mercs lack a governing corps/hired unit. Space Navy fails recurring ship-crew naval mode; Space Academy fails military formation as series engine (Teal's school is civilian and disrupted); Space Command fails command viewpoint as governing mode. Leaders' interludes do not make this a command series.

Space colonies > Space station thrillers is a strong first-book rival and Archangel's saboteur thread supports it. But the whole series leaves the contained settlement terrorist threat for military field operations and a planetary invasion/resistance. Space mystery's investigator/case, Space colony politics' civic power struggle and Colony life's lived settlement are likewise not the whole engine. Future history in space fails a sustained civilizational chronicle; Generation starships fails multigenerational ship society; Techno-fiction in space overweights implanted identity technology, which does not govern the other two novels; Space dystopias overweights secret projects rather than a closed oppressive social order; Comic space fiction fails governing comedy. No sibling is used as a remainder bucket.

## Space opera and adventure
The interstellar war/adventure tradition passes Space opera. Classic space opera is not selected by vintage nostalgia or commercial immortality; this is a modern character-led revival, not Evergreen's enduring commercial benchmark or the earlier pulp traditions. New space opera's Political branch loses because oligarch/admiral/scientist power plays support the weapon-and-survival conflict rather than relations among powers as primary field of action; Theological loses because the Spectre cult is not a search for sacred order; Space western lacks a governing frontier-lawless register. Space soap opera is a substantial Nova straddle: Jim Hines describes personal growth/relationships across roughly three quarters, and Jessica Strider independently confirms romance/identity/mystery. That cannot be minimized. But Archangel explicitly drops romance for military action, and Iolanthe's published premise is invasion, survival and resistance rather than a continuing romance or relationship drama as the entire trilogy's engine. Space adventure is the least-distorting whole-fiction home, confidence moderate rather than absolute.

Adventure's Space caper fails a continuing crew-run scheme/heist/con; a planted bomb or saboteur is not automatically that engine. Space chase is a serious rival because Nova has a ticking clock and Iolanthe initially flees into jungle, but neither a continuing fugitive dash nor hunter's trail governs the whole published sequence. The later books face the enemy through field weapons and resistance. Space combat explicitly includes mixed soldiers and other fighters in war machines/battlefields, not only institutional infantry. It fits the whole conflict better than Alien-war grunts while preserving the substantial Nova wobble. Existing mixed-mode Skyward/Moonstorm and improvised-crew action membership supports, but does not substitute for, that definition test. No gate is widened.
