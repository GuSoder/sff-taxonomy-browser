## Glorious Angels: retain, primary contradiction resolved
One2015published novel remains the scope. A2018planned sequel Hell's Ditch is not counted as a published novel: current author book page still describes a manuscript, current ISFDB bibliography and SFE do not list it as published. This is no claim that it was never written or privately shared. The author called it a continuation about the same city, retroengineering and first contact, so if published it would fold into this fiction rather than a second card.

A finished review argues that the technology amounts to literal magic. That was investigated, not accepted as a root verdict. In the30March2015Big Issue North interview, Robson directly explains that characters think they have magic but do not, it is fantastic technology whose manufacturing origin is forgotten, and archaeological reverseengineering drives their discoveries. Her publisher-hosted final-edit account independently describes the same backwards discovery society. This primary ontology statement is more specific than a review's complaint about implausible technology, and no direct supernatural counterexample was established. Bloodline engineering and biological abilities are not made magic merely by their improbability. Retain SF > Atemporal > Science fantasy > Ozymandian for old technological works under a fantasy surface. Alien contact, sexual politics and matriarchy are substantial rival emphases; none explains the lost-work causal engine as well. No claim of rigorous hard science. High confidence primary explanation, moderate fine leaf.
