## Root-down walk
Speculative fiction > Science fiction: physically modeled artifacts, astronomical exploration, machines/organic intelligences, cyborg implants and cosmology. Not Fantasy: Highers are advanced alien agents, esty engineered spacetime, not literal sorcery. Weird/poetic passages do not overthrow physical mechanism.
SF > Atemporal: early near-future/human galactic future strongly fits Futurist, and Hard alien correctly describes lower-level biological/mechanical encounter. But root explicitly says ANY science-fiction time-travel as premise or element sorts Atemporal, so actual final-volume wormhole to remote future controls whole shared fiction. Modern/Historical do not fit whole, narrative near-present strands do not override. Never alter Hard alien definition to keep the card or split around known root conflict.
Atemporal > Time travel fiction: positive actual transfer through time. Not reincarnation (no repeated soul lives), fabulation (not narrative unreliability primary), New Weird (not irresolvable scientific/supernatural ontology), Science fantasy (physical timeline usable; any-time gate specific), Parallel worlds (review alternateuniverse wording serious rival, but verified trip to physical cosmos heat-death future supplies temporal boundary; not alternate-Earth counterpart/history-governing multiverse).
Time travel fiction > Hard time travel: rigorously physical blackhole/esty/wormhole machinery central to late journey, author theoretical physicist, publisher explicit well-grounded scientific mechanism and cosmic-string/blackhole engineering across arc. Already-existing Thousand Earths accepted analogous relativistic future expeditions; no new gate invented. Not saying this is time-travel-agency missions merely because Highers are a species: story is not an organization owning apparatus and assigning temporal operations, and transfer is battle consequence. No personal inventor's device for homebrew leaf. Time distortion strong rival: esty distorted rates/refuge as well as ordinary relativistic longevity, but positive relocation to remote heat-death future and scientifically-developed cosmological navigation outweigh unequal ongoing rates among contemporaneous regions as sole governing operation. Time crossroads strong refuge/hub surface, but no host/keepers running a waystation/trade among eras as story engine. Time door requires standing UNEXPLAINED passage simply there; esty constructed and mechanism cosmological, fails. Timeslips require unexplained/natural carrying shift, not engineered spacetime refuge and physical wormhole dynamics. Time travel disorders/tonics fail bodycondition/substance triggers. Mind over time fails only-consciousness: bodies/families move; ancestral time travel fails kinship determining target century despite father/son plot. No reset loop. Hard time travel positive fit, not nearest leftover.
