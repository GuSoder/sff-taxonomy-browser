## CALL for review

Recommend split-with-umbrella by what the epidemic leaves survivors living among: **people remade by infection** versus **a society remade by epidemic loss**.5/5. This is a reader-facing plague-mode axis, not natural/artificial origin, creature morality, prognosis, or whether the ending finally cures the condition.

A: Aftermath survival in which a continuing population of infection-transformed people defines the changed world: vampires, feral humans or bodily remade survivors. Includes the viewpoint survivors themselves, intelligent successor societies and sympathetic infected people. Does not require monsters as enemies or irreversible changes forever.

B: Aftermath survival in which epidemic deaths, illness, food loss and damaged institutions define the changed world; people endure or rebuild amid those losses. Not a promise of normal bodies, a cured disease, empty Earth, or successful reconstruction. A transient symptom, chronic injury or a single immune person does not by itself establish cellA.

Working labels to workshop, NOT accepted names: Transformed plague aftermath / Shattered plague aftermath (3words each; both carry parent plague/aftermath context). These are descriptive new labels, not claimed established genres. Neither name means the other cell lacks change or destruction. Owner coins final names.

### B5: society changed by epidemic loss

1. **The Death of Grass** (1956): plant virus kills grasses/food crops, so famine and social violence drive Custance's family's passage to sanctuary. Infected humans are not the changed population. **Real boundary**: not human infection; parent already admits pandemic plant disease through its accepted placement. https://deathofgrass.com/ (rights-holder press description).
2. **Earth Abides** (1949): Ish returns after mass mortality, gathers a small community, tries to preserve knowledge, and ages as new generations become a preindustrial tribe. Further disease kills children; no altered host population. Whole life, not just opening, checked https://www.blackgate.com/2020/01/23/things-are-as-they-are-george-r-stewarts-earth-abides/ . Root timing caveat: opens in author's present, but main extent is succeeding decades/living aftermath; not collapse-in-progress. Publisher fetch https://www.harpercollins.com/products/earth-abides-george-r-stewart lacked substantive description, so not used as plot evidence.
3. **Station Eleven** (2014): flu kills most people, touring actors and survivor communities live years later among the lost world's artifacts; prophet and coercion are human social dangers, not infected transformation. Flashbacks do not change the principal aftermath engine. https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/239452/station-eleven-by-emily-st-john-mandel/ .
4. **How High We Go in the Dark** (2022): **genuine boundary, explicitly tested**. Arctic plague turns organs into other organs; comatose survivors share an uncanny space; people later wake, have chronic symptoms or virus-caused cancer. It is NOT valid to say nobody changes or nobody survives infection. However, the full linked narrative concerns terminal care, grief, death industries, recovery, climate repair and a starship, not life among a continuing mutant host population. Talking pig is deliberately grown organ-transplant research, not an infected successor human. Late alien world-builder reveal explains the origin but does not make the contemporary sick population a new host society. https://www.sequoianagamatsu.com/how-high-we-go-in-the-dark ; https://locusmag.com/review/paul-di-filippo-reviews-how-high-we-go-in-the-dark-by-sequoia-nagamatsu/ ; complete chapter-by-chapter synopsis https://www.supersummary.com/how-high-we-go-in-the-dark/summary/ ; final reveal https://www.supersummary.com/how-high-we-go-in-the-dark/chapter-14-epilogue-summary/ . Do not classify by ending alone or label it biologically unchanged.
5. **Child Zero** (2022): four years of antibiotic resistance/mass deaths plus bioterror leave broken public health and coercive biological security. Detective Gibson pursues a massacre and a healthy boy whose immunity offers hope. Working institutions and the crime thriller are real; this is NOT abandoned-earth reconstruction. The accepted parent means enduring the living aftermath, not total disappearance of government. Mateo's unusual healing/immune status is an individual exception, not a widespread changed-host population. https://www.mulhollandbooks.com/titles/chris-holm/child-zero/9780316295123/ ; https://www.criminalelement.com/book-review-child-zero-by-chris-holm/ ; https://bolobooks.com/2022/05/child-zero-the-bolo-books-review/ .

## Root-down check retained

Speculative > Science fiction (disease/parasite/engineered or alien plague premises, not supernatural magic) > Futurist SF > Future of Earth fiction > Dystopian futures > Post-holocaust survival > Post-pandemic survival. This is the existing living-aftermath branch, not Modern day SF > Apocalyptic fiction > Pandemic's catastrophe-in-progress. Future societies misses the governing damage; Totalitarian/social dystopia are real competitors in Child Zero but not the outbreak-damaged survival engine; Ruined Earth requires ruins as later cultural substrate, not survivors of a named epidemic; Dying earth has moribund-sun scope. Post-nuclear wrong cause; Terminal wastelands mixed/unspecified cause, wrong here. Darwin alien mystery is substantial but Earth survivors' continuing emergency remains whole-trilogy engine. High's starship and cosmic coda do not turn the whole fiction into a voyage/alien-history story. Wilder's island scope and Child's functional institutions are real parent-edge cases already in their accepted placement rationales, not facts to conceal or definitions to silently widen.

## Rejected axes

- Zombies versus ordinary people: excludes Legend's living society, Wilder's viewpoint mutants and Passage's sympathetic hybrids; falsely implies pathogen must be biological and zombie rules universal.
- Alteration vs no alteration: fails High's organ/body changes and chronic survivors.
- Enemy transformed people vs survivors: fails Wilder and Legend; prior4/6 suggestion withdrawn.
- Cure/rebuild vs permanent mutation: fails final Passage recovery and explained Darwin cause; endings do not govern whole-fiction classification.
- Solitary vs community: Legend ends in community and most fictions mix both; no invariant.
- Collapse complete vs unfolding: Grass/Wilder/Child remain at the living crisis edge, Passage spans both; unsuitable split within accepted parent.
- Natural vs engineered: High late alien origin and Darwin's explained design expose spoiler/cause dependence rather than reader aftermath expectation.
