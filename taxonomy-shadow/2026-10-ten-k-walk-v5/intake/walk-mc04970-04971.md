# Amenkor universe, MC04970-04971, 2026-11-06

Place the whole nine-novel shared universe in existing Channeled hard magic, 8 to 9. No definition change, seed or split. Three trilogies, one fiction: Throne of Amenkor (The Skewed Throne2006, The Cracked Throne2006, The Vacant Throne2008); Well of Sorrows (Well of Sorrows2010, Leaves of Flame2012, Breath of Heaven2016); Ley (Shattering the Ley2014, Threading the Needle2016, Reaping the Aurora2017). Queue2015 for Well/Leaves describes the digital reissues, not originals. The first two Well novels originally used Benjamin Tate, the same author; current print reissues credit Joshua Palmatier. One author, no duplicate attribution as two people.

## Scope evidence and limits

The author's2017 finished-Ley interview explicitly says all three previous series were connected, in the same world at different times. His2012 guest essay independently places Amenkor and Well on the same mapped world and ties both through the White Fire and an unpublished ancestral novel. The unpublished novel is not a tenth volume. A current agent bibliography lists all three completed trilogies. Crystal Cities is not silently included: the author says his next series is a new world, new characters, different from the preceding three. Current bibliography names Crystal Cities separately. No later published Amenkor-world sequel found. Anthologies edited by the author, Ur-Bar, collections and omnibuses are not extra novels in this fiction.

## Every trilogy's own engine

Amenkor: White Fire twice causes madness and ruin. Varis's danger-sensitive river sight helps her survive the slums, become an assassin and overthrow the Mistress. In Cracked she controls the soul-filled throne while managing famine, treachery and an impending Chorl invasion. Its founding souls and accumulated rulers form a magical reservoir, with costs and constraints on mastery. In Vacant, the throne's power has been sacrificed against the invasion; Varis attempts to heal Erick, learns the Chorl's linked magic, seeks the sister throne in Venitte and faces political distrust and another attack. The past is told through a founding soul's memories and visions; do not turn that into literal bodily time travel. Politics and statecraft are substantive, but the magical river/throne and how to wield/control/share its power are structural throughout.

Well: Colin's refugee caravan encounters rival peoples and life-eating shadows. Drinking Lifeblood changes his nature, lifespan and powers, including stopping/slowing time with an addictive personal cost. Decades pass in the narrative, not automatically time travel. Leaves takes place a century after the peace he brokered: three of five magics mastered, protective Trees gifted to the three races, wells reopened by the enemy, and the Source controlling the network becomes the central danger. Breath starts with the Autumn Tree fallen, the Source activated, a wounded Colin and armies of Wraiths. He must surrender himself to the Lifeblood to stop Walter and the Wraiths, at the cost of his humanity. This is a multi-magic network with learnable consequences and personal transformation, not just the first book's frontier journey. Third-volume publisher synopsis and primary completed-trilogy scope verified; no substantial independent finished Breath review located, a material thinner point preserved rather than claiming the novel was read.

Ley: author describes natural magical energy connecting nodes/rock formations, augmented by Wielders and routed to heat, light, transport and build cities. Its abuse shatters the network. Shattering follows Kara's training, Allan's suppression ability, baronial monopoly and terrorists until catastrophe. Threading follows refugees' survival, rebuilding attempts and rival gangs/cults; frozen-in-time distortion shards and their healing are real consequences of the magical network. Reaping follows the Needle, Kara's attempt to heal Tumbor with all Wielders, Morrell's healing of people/structures/crystals, attacks and internal rebellion; it resolves the technical/magical-system crisis as well as political conflict. Full primary descriptions of all three, author explanation of their world-specific engine, Kirkus and SFRevu finished reviews read. Not inferred from the Well trilogy's continuity.

## Root-down walk

Speculative fiction > Fantasy > Fantasy realms > Epic fantasy > Hard fantasy > Hard magic > Channeled hard magic.

Actual Lifeblood, souls in thrones, White Fire, Wraiths and ley are presented as magic. A reviewer calls Ley science fictional, and Kirkus says its magic obeys scientifically testable principles; neither makes this SF rather than fantasy. The author explicitly says it is an invented fantasy world with a modern feel. Geographic analogies to colonial America or New York do not make it Earth. Neither Real earth fantasy nor Portal fantasy is supported. Immortality, time slowing and in-world distortions remain magic-side and are tested below, not routed to Atemporal SF.

Fantasy realms siblings: Epic positively carries multiple civilizations, centuries, large geographies, magic networks and world-scale consequences. Sword and Sorcery's intimate action celebration underdescribes the system and civic stakes. Fairytale has no controlling revision or invented fairy-tale grammar; Far shores is not primarily counterpart-culture exploration. Comic fails governing joke; Heart has relationships but not romantic/community/horse bond as spine. Gothic realms is strong in Amenkor's haunted throne, White Fire madness and Well's wraith forest; however whole-fiction action focuses on mastering, balancing and repairing power through civic and world-scale conflict, not dread/enclosure. Technomancy is a genuine Ley rival because it powers cities, but the Gothic necromantic-machinery gate would overread only Amenkor's soul throne and the horrific distortions. The explicit quasi-natural force laws and bodily limits provide a cleaner whole-universe Hard fantasy fit than menace machinery.

Epic siblings: Heroic classes are individually present (Varis assassin/mage, Colin transformed caster, Kara Wielder, Allan guard), but no one class describes all protagonists or their common problem as well as the magical systems. High fantasy's fellowship/marked-one journeys govern only parts; its quest tradition is not the whole shared-universe engine. Political fantasy is the strongest rival: Varis's statecraft, racial empires/peace and the baron's monopoly/terrorists. Yet no one political child (court legitimacy, statecraft or organized overthrow) governs all three, while managing and paying for the same world's magic networks does. Military has wars but not rank/unit/campaign as whole spine. Mythological has invented spirits but not their mythic-being narrative. Hard fantasy is positively grounded in source-tapped force, magic's flows and networks, their stabilization, costs and bodily consequences, rather than inferred merely because the author teaches mathematics.

Hard fantasy children: Time magic is tested seriously. Colin's stop/slow powers and Ley's frozen distortion shards are load-bearing in their respective narratives. But loops, rewritten pasts and journeys through history do not organize the entire nine-novel fiction. They are domains/effects within wider Lifeblood/ley/river systems, not the whole fiction's principal magical domain. Amenkor's ancestral visions/memory are not assumed historical traversal. Magic-side time does not have SF's any-element override. Hard magic is the more inclusive positive mechanism, not a way to disregard temporal consequences. The whole-fiction judgment is moderate, and a verified history-rewrite engine across the trilogy would change the comparison. No such evidence found.

Hard magic children: Channeled is positive for tapping Lifeblood, the magical river and ley force through human bodies, with change, exhaustion, madness or loss of humanity as limits/costs, and collective/channel-amplifying systems. Bonded is a serious Amenkor rival because the throne holds souls, but the whole universe's source is not chiefly a personal pact or spirit bound to each caster. Colin's Lifeblood and Kara's ley are not such bonds. Scripted fails written/coded sigils or commands as common operative mechanism; mathematical author biography is not evidence of spell formulae. Existing Channeled leaf accepts ninth fiction. No need to invent an infrastructure-magic seed or change a gate.

Confidence moderate-high for Hard fantasy/Channeled, with meaningful political, time-domain and thin Breath-review caveats retained. No full novels read. Evidence covers every book's official engine plus independent completed reviews in each of Amenkor and Ley, two Well finished reviews and official final-volume synopsis. One universe is never split by trilogy or volume to get cleaner shelves.
