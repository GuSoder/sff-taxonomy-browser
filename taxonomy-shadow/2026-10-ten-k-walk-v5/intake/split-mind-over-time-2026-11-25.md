## Approved execution
Approved by owner phonemsg-01M89XE549Y7HC3P5TD1XR69MG (25 November2026,18:53 CET), on proposal phonemsg-01M89MKSDPXMEHKQ6KRF5XJ425. Original proposal and attributed owner approval inspected in supplied conversation context.

slaughterhouse-five: Billy Pilgrim experiences the war, postwar life and alien captivity out of order within his own life. The mind's nonsequential lived moments are the approved model, not a willed expedition to another historical society or a recurring conversation with a separately anchored past mind. Trauma/alien ambiguity remains explicit, not silently solved.

flux: Bo, Brandon and Blue are stages of one man's overlapping lived experience. The employer induces dreaming while he is connected to machines, and he makes active choices, so neither innate-versus-technical nor passive-versus-active defines this split. Owner26October explicitly approved Flux's technological trigger in this consciousness-experience leaf. The defining form is his fractured life across moments, not primarily contacting another era's person or conducting a historical mission.

elysium-brissett: The first novel's reconstructed computer memory alone is not time travel. Whole-fiction sequel Destroyer of Light supplies Cate/Cora's concurrent experience of past, present and future, confirmed by the author interview. This extended temporal consciousness, rather than a single historical visit, controls. The AI can enter human experience and contact others, a real overlap, but the defining Here and Now of simultaneous time is stronger than treating it as a temporal telephone. No claim of bodily transport or historical revision.

stamping-butterflies: Moz, Zaq and Tris share dreams and mental knowledge across widely separated eras through the Library. These linked minds are the access frame. The ending's historical revision and the offered childhood loop are real overlaps with journeys and loops, not suppressed, but neither replaces the cross-era mental connection that joins the whole novel. The exact ending claims rest on retained earlier research, while current completed reviews confirm interwoven eras and Library-mediated mental dialogue; no new full-text mirror used.

ler-trilogy: Whole three-novel fiction retained. The Day of the Klesh's Skazenach permits Cretus to look from the ancient past and bring his mind into present-day Meure, a contact between separately anchored people, not merely a stored personality being replayed. Oracle mediation and embodiment are real caveats, but cross-era mental inhabitation is the most specific function. Earlier anthropology and later unknown-entity language do not undo the already-recorded Earth-derived SF root.

darkover-late-novels: Whole29-novel fiction retained. Damon's timesearch through the Overworld contacts Varzil and other Keepers across time in The Forbidden Tower; independent completed reviews corroborate this specific psychic encounter. Shadow Matrix also sends bodies into Varzil's time, so it genuinely overlaps Psychic time journeys. Assignment follows the distinctive recurring cross-era psychic link, not an invented claim that bodily visits never occur. Prior Earth-colony, laran and genuine magic/technology-ambiguity root reasoning retained.

the-skyward-series: The ancient woman in Cytonic feels Spensa's present questions and responds through the portal memory. This is cross-era mental dialogue between two people, corroborated by two summaries, rather than generic timeless FTL or passive ancient playback. Spensa stays physically at the ruin. Whole5-novel fiction retained, not a Cytonic-only card. A memory focus is not itself a ridden time machine or a door she bodily crosses into history.

time-and-again: Si Morley's trained self-hypnosis and historical immersion take him bodily into1882and1912, where he pursues romance, investigation and historical intervention. Government training is not a passenger apparatus. A directed mental journey into a historical setting, not merely simultaneous awareness or a dialogue across eras. Owner26October specifically approved this mechanism in Mind over time.

elleander-morning: At death Elleander wills consciousness back into her young body in1907and acts to kill Hitler, reshaping the history her granddaughter investigates. The earlier-body return genuinely touches a life-spanning-self reading, but the concrete willed visit and historical action are the stronger frame than living multiple moments concurrently. Not ordinary reincarnation, and no assertion of perpetual repeated loops.

the-timedivers: Whole two-novel fiction retains inherited, trained mental navigation through time: concentration brings travelers bodily into historical settings for missions and political action. The Temporal Guard employs the faculty rather than supplying its necessary passenger apparatus. This is a directed temporal visit, not an intrinsic pathological time disorder, concurrent self-experience or primarily a cross-era mental conversation.
