## Every novel's own engine
Pure: nine years after nuclear Detonations, Pressia with doll-head fused to her hand hides from militia recruitment outside the Dome; unscarred Partridge escapes the genetic-conditioning regime to find his mother. Pressia, Partridge, Bradwell with birds fused to his back, Lyda and El Capitan encounter wasteland factions and discover the Dome's sinister role. The fusions are bomb-associated nanotechnology, not magic, and their inherited divided survival condition drives the story.
Fuse: Pressia follows her mother's Cygnus clue/Black Box toward a cure for the Wretches' fusings. Dome attacks/hostages force Partridge back inside to confront his father. Separate journeys, friendship and El Capitan's reconciliation with fused brother Helmud matter alongside the mystery of what the bombs did. The fusion condition is still active plot, not decorative scars carried over from first volume.
Burn: Partridge takes his dead father's place as Dome leader and struggles to dismantle the order, then succumbs to its power and an imposed marriage/virtual refuge. Pressia's group returns from Ireland with bacterium capable of bringing down the Dome and hope of healing the Wretches. Final confrontation puts physical humanity and the protected/unprotected divide at stake. Completed final reviews disagree sharply on its ending; both confirm these mechanisms. No claim of universal healing/happy ending or resolved fate for every character.
