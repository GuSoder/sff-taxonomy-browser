## Whole scope
The Wild Boys1971 continues in Port of Saints, nominally1973 but actually issued1975, revised1980, which SFE includes in the Red Night sequence with Cities1981, Place1983limited/1984trade, Western1987. Literary Encyclopedia explicitly links Port to Wild. Starck1992 identifies common characters/mythology across these five and Exterminator!, tracing Audrey through Cities, renamed Kim in Place, dead Kim into Western. NYRB independently identifies the Audrey/Kim continuity. Exterminator! is a collection despite some editions subtitled novel and is excluded from count. Other Burroughs books are not folded merely because of cameos, themes or authorial persona. Five is supported novel scope, not an assertion that every other Burroughs text exists in a separate universe.

## Positive existing-leaf fit
Fantasy > Real earth fantasy > Contemporary fantasy > Supernatural fantasy > Undying.
- Wild: characters including Audrey/John and wild boys retain consciousness after death, per Skerl discussed in UC Press; actual car pulled through time invalidates former Futurist ancestor independently.
- Port: immunity-to-death training, shaman spells, bodily transformation. The supernatural is operative, not inferred from author's private beliefs.
- Cities: successful sex magic and dead Jerry's possession of Jim's body, with interchangeable identities and eras.
- Place: Kim revives Joe the Dead, who kills him. Time travel ranges across Earth history and Venus, not one historical era.
- Western: dead Kim persists into an Egyptian-inflected afterlife quest. This is a culmination of repeated efforts to escape death, not the only death-related book.

The current Undying definition takes unnatural deathlessness outside a bargain, including necromantic return, preservation or imposed eternal life. Its six existing members span sought immortality (Blood Call), corpse return (Ward, Make Me a Monster, White Trash Zombie), imposed preservation (Tide), and immortal/reincarnating lovers (Hundred Loves). This whole joins them without widening the gate. Confidence moderate: political rebellion, sex/linguistic control and experimental collage are substantial straddles. Literal death escape recurs across the whole, supported by Cultural Logic and LARB independent criticism. The leaf does not require every page or protagonist to be immortal.

Portal requires governing Earth/otherworld exchange through an anchored threshold, text, rift or otherworld discovery, not merely unstable time or narration. Historical/Future fantasy select individual settings rather than the mutable whole; Contemporary explicitly allows Earth-rooted fantasy outside any past era. Contemporary mythic fails ordinary present-day inherited/invented myth frame; magical coming of age fails adult identity formation; domestic fails household site; dark horror/torment fails governing menace and specific tormentor gates; urban fails city-institutions engine; magic realism fails quiet acceptance inside daily social reality; folktale/comic fails governing tale/comic machinery.

Undying positively catches the recurring survival across death rather than choosing only an ending. Vampire, werewolf, ghost, dragon and haunted house frames fail. Time magic fails rigorous-magic/epic ancestors. Weird West selects Place only. Modern Egyptian myth selects Western only.
