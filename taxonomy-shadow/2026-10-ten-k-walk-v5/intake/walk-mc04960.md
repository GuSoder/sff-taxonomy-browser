BirdBox's present strand is five years after unseen creatures broke civilization: Malorie and two four-year-olds leave their isolated house blindfolded for a twenty-mile river journey to a blind school. Flashbacks follow her pregnancy, housemates' resource/trust problems and breakdown. Sight causes murderous/suicidal madness, not a diagnosis of infectious disease. ## Root contradiction and choice

SFE explicitly calls this NearFuture AlienInvasion. Strong SF alternate, not ignored. But primary author/publisher repeatedly say no explanation/origin; Malerman calls the central horror infinity personified in Hellnotes, Kirkus and Syfy, not an extraterrestrial species or native organism. PW interview's bracketed description calls them supernatural entities: that bracket is editorial, not Malerman's verbatim word. PRH reprints WashingtonPost's review calling Malorie a supernatural thriller; own author's page quotes PW's supernatural-thriller review. Taken with literal mind-breaking unknowable entities and no biological/technological explanation, Fantasy is the stronger root. This is a literary taxonomy judgment with moderate SF wobble, not proof of cosmological ontology. Do not assert literalGod, Lovecraftdeity, hell, demon or known creature origin. Infinity comparison is the author's creative premise, not an established species fact.

SF losing walk: Historical fails any privileged past-era/fork; modern-day apocalypse and unknown-catastrophe genuinely fit first-book collapse strand but whole fiction principally follows survivors five through seventeen years after, not catastrophe unfolding. Futurist -> FutureEarth -> Postholocaust positively fits survival but its cause leaves require disease/nuclear/alien/Earth-native infestation, none established by primary descriptions. Terminalwastelands requires devastated landscape as governing condition, not simply civilization gone and unknown cause. These books hide the landscape behind blindfolds and keep creatures/communities/family rules central. RuinedEarth's farther-future inheritance is wrong at years, not centuries. ModernSoft -> Pulp -> Creaturefeature strongest register rival (Kirkus uses that label), yet natural/atomic monster spectacle and adventure do not explain this cosmic supernatural unknowability. Pseudoscience's evidenced cryptid/psi claims, Bioengineering, Technothriller, social dystopia, first contact, alien-war/occupation engines all fail. Psychological science is not the cause of real creatures. Atemporal fails time travel/parallel worlds/reincarnation; narrative flashbacks aren't travel. Sciencefantasy requires science beneath fantasy surfaces; no engine demonstrated. NewWeird/HomeWeird cross-mode ecological/grotesque material world is a possibility by association, but no need to invent a cross-genre science component when a direct fantasy aftermath gate already fits.

## Fantasy full root-down walk

Fantasy -> RealEarth: Michigan and remains of our world, not autonomous FantasyRealms or a crossing to another world. Portal fails literal inter-world journey; river/train travel on Earth is not a portal. RealEarth's Historical fails era/myth-retelling; Contemporary is substantial first-book backstory and ordinary technology but wholeduology lives in years of a changed civilization, with ownpublisher calling near-future apocalypse. FutureFantasy positive: ourEarth after a supernatural catastrophe, not scientific extrapolation. Literal dates not invented.

Contemporary losing routes: MythicFantasy fails identified tradition; MagicalComingOfAge fails newly developing personal magic as plot; DomesticFantasy fails home/family as sustaining magical site rather than dangerous shelters abandoned for survival; UrbanFantasy's recurring case/service/vigilante or public magical civic engine fails, even though people and surviving communities are present. SupernaturalFantasy's named vampire/werewolf/ghost/haunted-house/deathlessness/deal/afterlife categories fail actual entity identification; house shelters aren't haunted engines. MagicRealism fails the marvelous accepted as ordinary; terror and survival are explicit. Folktale/comic/animal fail tale/comic register or animal society. DarkFantasy is real horror-mode rival but place branches lack literalHell, cursedground or folkrite; torment branches require ancientworshippedgods, Satan, demons, ancientprehuman/shapeshifter, vengeance or trusted-roleimpostor, none attested. Unknown worldwide entities must not be squeezed into a named lineage. Horror mood doesn't override positive future aftermath.

FutureFantasy's four children: Post-apocalypticFantasy positive because old civilization fell and remnants live in the resulting world, contending with monsters not explained by science. EndTimes fails biblical angel/demon apocalypse; infinity/God-face simile isn't Revelation. VampireFuture and PsiFuture require never-fallenEarth and respective beings/socialorders, both wrong. Post-apocalypticFantasy's existing gate explicitly includes fall and remnant societies; it does not demand centuries, rediscovered magic, or rubble landscape. This is an honest existing home with supernatural-vs-SF uncertainty disclosed, not a new cosmic leaf and not a definition widening.
