## Each novel's own engine
Master of Sorrows: Annev, born without a hand and secretly magical, trains at Chaenbalu's anti-magic warrior-thief academy while Sodar secretly teaches him forbidden power. Tests, prejudice, divided loyalties and hidden identity culminate in the destruction of his school. This is not the promised fully formed Dark Lord yet. Author explicitly cautions the villain pitch belongs to the full arc, not the first volume alone.
Master Artificer: survivors enter Luqura; Annev seeks control of his own power and removal of the cursed artifact fused to his body. Fyn's criminal-guild struggle, Myjun's assassin training and magic mask, Kenton's altered body and Vault magic are separate interlocking arcs, not retreads of the academy. Completed reviews describe lengthy on-page magic lessons, ancestry restrictions, distinct domains, bodily consequences and Annev's increasingly cruel choices. Series axis remains discovering and using a consistent elemental magic system while the central character takes his darker path. Not an Academy series based only on book1.

## Root-down walk
Speculative fiction > Fantasy: genuine gods, magic, elemental beings, enchanted artifacts, not scientific or technological rationalization. Fantasy > Fantasy realms: author explicitly locates the whole series in invented medieval Luquatra, not Earth, historical Earth, future Earth or Earth-to-otherworld crossing. Fantasy realms > Epic fantasy: invented pantheon, deep history, expanding cities/cultures, supernatural factions and world-shaping prophecy. Sword-and-sorcery's episodic action is not the governing scale; no fairy-tale retelling, Gothic literary school, comic premise, counterpart-culture organizing axis, romance/cozy/horse bond spine.
Epic > Hard fantasy: explicit magic rules govern what the characters can attempt and what it costs. Author says he builds systems like game rules and calls his system akin to Sanderson hard magic; book2 completed reviews independently describe detailed lessons and restrictions as central, not decorative lore. This beats Heroic's single RPG class because multiple powers and POVs become the engine; not solely the reincarnation of one deity or religious service making Cleric, not Grey Mage by moral ambiguity or Black Mage merely because eventual villain. Quest fantasy is the strongest narrative competitor, especially its revisionist branch, but book1 mainly four days at the school and book2 urban learning, criminal conflict and pursuit of answers do not make a consequential road journey the governing form. Political intrigue is a strand, not institutional maneuvering as primary genre; no campaign/unit warfare; invented mythology is substrate, not the gods' own narrated lives or deep-time chronicle.
Hard fantasy > Hard magic: learnable elemental rules and costs, not time manipulation's paradox/loop engine.
Hard magic > Channeled hard magic: power is elemental essence manipulated through the caster with bodily limits and consequences. Primary author identifies three physical elements skywater/earthblood/lightfire and metaphysical mind/body/spirit, with younger gods' powers as subsets. Completed reviews corroborate ancestry-linked access, practical lessons, metal/flesh mutation and Kenton's aqlumera fusion. Community technical reference supplies more detail, explicitly lower confidence than author/reviews: Darite glyph-work consumes oxygen/fluid and dehydrates the caster; Ilumite magic channels lumen through the body with heat/fever risk; Terran spells alter the caster's blood/bones/skin. The whole system runs through elemental material and bodies, not one single written or bonded medium. Scripted is the closest sibling because Darite glyphs matter, but writing is only one family, not the whole-fiction delivery mechanism; no primary bond with a sentient elemental being governing terms and limits for Bonded. Magical prosthetics and artifacts are not proof of bound spirits. Channeled does not imply every spell lacks a glyph or artifact, just as existing mixed-system whole fictions here are not all one casting gesture.

## Wobble and evidence limits
Broad aboutness choice, not a claim that every scene operates through one spell type. The system deliberately includes soft mystery as well as explicit rules, and book1 withholds much of it. Book2's independent reviews and author interview show the rules take center stage. Fandom mechanical details are supporting reader evidence, not sole proof of the classification. Full texts not read; no unsupported claim about exact eventual god identity, future-book mechanics or published ending. No seed needed to force one protagonist into an unproven narrow school.
