# Blood on the Moon: intake decision
2026-10-29

One fiction, two published novels: Blood on the Moon (2011) and Blood Crave (2012), Jennifer Knight. Place in Vampire hunt, 4 to5 cards. Werewolf fantasy is a genuine rival, not dismissed merely because the heroine is human. No structural change or definition widening.

## Whole-fiction evidence
1. Blood on the Moon: contemporary Colorado State freshman Faith enters a concealed vampire/werewolf conflict. Vampire Vincent targets her to hurt his former friend Lucas, now a werewolf; Lucas protects her, their romance develops, Faith's mental influence over his wolf form matters at the climactic battle, and Derek is bitten. Kirkus verifies that causal chain, not just the blurb. Independent reviewer likewise describes Vincent attacking Faith to provoke Lucas and the protection/romance arc. https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/jennifer-knight/blood-moon-knight/ ; http://unabridgedbookshelf.blogspot.com/2012/09/book-review-blood-on-moon-by-jennifer.html
2. Blood Crave: the vampire kidnapping aftermath becomes resistance to a vampire uprising; Derek's vampire bite and Lucas's answering bite yield a half-wolf, half-vampire hybrid. Faith's empathic ability and influence on wolves become the means of persuading and mobilizing the packmaster against that threat. Primary publisher explicitly describes the uprising and preparing the pack for battle, supported by two full reader reviews. https://www.hachette.co.uk/titles/jennifer-knight/blood-crave/9780762441181/ ; https://rhapsodyinbooks.wordpress.com/2012/11/20/review-of-blood-crave-by-jennifer-knight/ ; http://readinglark.blogspot.com/2012/12/book-review-blood-crave.html . Reading Lark's heading mistakenly calls this #3, while its body and independent series bibliographies establish #2.

## Root-down walk
Speculative fiction > Fantasy: actual supernatural werewolf transformation, vampires and empathic influence, no grounded scientific engine. Real earth fantasy: Colorado State and recognizable modern America; no crossing from Earth to another world, no independent fantasy universe. Contemporary fantasy: college/campus setting contemporary to 2011, no historical era or future setting.

Supernatural fantasy rather than Urban: hidden supernatural species organize the conflicts and relationships, not city infrastructure, a living/shadow city, a publicly acknowledged supernatural civic order, fae, police/PI cases, a state agency, modern frontier survival or a Gothic institution/community. A campus locale alone is not Occult institutions. Romance is genuine, but the Fantasy realms Heart branch fails the Earth gate. Magical coming of age is a rival strand in Faith's discovery of empathy, but it is not training as a magic practitioner. Dark fantasy also catches danger and pursuit, yet no cursed place, demonic, satanic, primordial species, elder god or revenge-spirit cause fits those children; the named supernatural creatures have their own matching branch. Not ordinary/unexplained accepted marvels of magic realism, domestic marvel, mythic retelling or folktale.

Supernatural fantasy > Vampire fantasy > Vampire hunt: both novels follow human Faith confronting a vampire predatory threat, first personal targeting/kidnapping and then collective resistance to an uprising. The definition expressly includes resistance, not just professional hunters. Not Vampire worlds: no vampire-society/lineage protagonist chronicle dominates. Not Vampire fringe: hybrid Derek is a consequence and complication within the continuing conflict, not a formal/ambiguous vampire experiment organizing both novels. Werewolf fantasy is the serious rival: Lucas's changing body, wolf-control and pack politics are central and Derek's hybridization adds a transmission/identity thread. Nevertheless the invariant organizing action across both is Faith's survival and resistance to vampires through wolf allies, while human/wolf identity struggle is not the controlling engine in either. This is a weighted whole-fiction judgment, not a claim the books lack wolves. Paranormal fantasy's empty definition must not become a dumping ground for any mixed cast; named predator/resistance supplies the more specific fit. Ghosts, Undying, Evil deals and Surreal afterlife fail their subjects.
