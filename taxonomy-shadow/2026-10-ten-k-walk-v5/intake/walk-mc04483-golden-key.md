Scripted: Grijalva painted iconography and formula-like symbolism alter reality, while the Shagara prequel traces protective charms/symbols into Qamar's codified magical language and book. Art is symbolic inscription here, not any painting automatically qualifying as script. No bound-being pact or external force drawn through the body makes Bonded/Channeled a better mechanism. The two-book invariant is discovering, inheriting and misusing the symbolic magic rather than a continuing contest for one ruling line. ## Whole content
Golden Key: Sario learns forbidden painted magic, imprisons Saavedra and moves through new bodies over centuries; second act ducal marriage/mistress intrigue, third art/female agency and constitutional revolt. Symbolic art/inheritance/control persists despite changing political periods. SF Site explicitly analyzes rigor of inherited Gift and time dilation; not merely label inference. Gothic/romantic evidence in FlamesRising is retained as counterevidence.
Diviner: Azzad survives clan massacre, Shagara healing/protection preserves family; Alessid marries into Shagara and instrumentalizes their magic in state-building; Qamar inherits fatal talent, studies symbols and creates magical legacy associated with Grijalva descendants. No claim that every chapter shows the same painting method or that the prequel is a procedural laboratory novel. Broad symbolic power is continuous, different media develop.
