Dead-spirit viewpoint, celestial beings, divine limits; royal/alien conflict description. Full visible chapter captured privately in /tmp/guardians-prh-excerpt.txt.
- PRH series: https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/series/SOG/star-of-the-guardians/ . Primary publisher, quartet descriptions.

- Rising Shadow, Knights: https://www.risingshadow.net/book/4551-the-knights-of-the-black-earth . Catalog/publisher description. Earth-supremacy project and Xris plot.
- Rising Shadow, Robot Blues: https://www.risingshadow.net/book/4552-robot-blues . Catalog/publisher description. Explicit shared-world wording, robotic-artifact plot.

- Mythcreants523, Space Fantasy: https://mythcreants.com/blog/podcasts/523-space-fantasy/ . Genre discussion, 16 February2025. Attested phrase, multi-planet civilization, acknowledged overlap with science fantasy.
