The discovery of Enelda Drake is the beginning of an investigation, not the sole goal of an episodic wilderness quest. They return to Scarfell and work the missingchildren mystery, aided by Lady Iveta's knowledge of the castle. Poor children vanished before officials cared; a noble hostage's loss threatens war.
