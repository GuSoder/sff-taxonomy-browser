- The Mysterious Island (1874-1875 serial/parts,1875completedbook; use1875): Civil War escapees become island settlers through chemistry, engineering and cooperative labor. Positive continuity is unusually strong: primary Island names Aronnax's published account, has Nemo explicitly recall the earlier visitors, concludes his life, redeems Ayrton, and brings back Robert Grant/Duncan. ## Root walk
Speculative fiction -> Science fiction. Nautilus electric engineering and technological intervention explain seeming mysteries, no magic. Castaways nonspeculative in isolation remains a constituent of whole shared fiction rather than split out.
Science fiction -> Historical SF: nineteenth-century Earth now reads historical. Not Modern-day. Not Futurist: even if technology was futuristic when written, modern reader's world is historic. Not Atemporal: no true time travel; stable Earth chronologies with editorial contradictions, not ontological timelessness.
Historical siblings: Proto SF wins, written in own era before genre formation. Not Period SF, a modern author re-creating period aesthetics. Not Hidden history SF, which is a poorer fit than the explicit own-era proto form. Not Alternate history: no divergence structures history.
Proto siblings: Scientific adventure wins. Current gate requires a nineteenth-century technological marvel plus fantastic voyage. Whole fiction includes the paradigmatic Nautilus trip; Island extends scientific mastery, wonder and rescue, with technical creation and electric technology resolving seeming supernatural incidents. Castaways adds a geographical voyage, not independent SF on its own. Lost world fiction loses: Atlantis is an episode, Lincoln/Tabor existing islands are not surviving prehistoric/ancient hidden civilizations; no whole-series lost-world discovery engine. Fantastic voyage loses its current pre-novelistic Lucian/Gulliver gate: these are nineteenth-century novels, and technology/cognition drive the shared fiction. Gothic SF loses: Nemo is enigmatic and violent but mystery/terror/Promethean monstrosity do not outweigh knowledge, exploration and survival. No rule widened for this placement.
