## Definitions proposed, not enacted
- Cybernoir sleuths: Cyberpunk noir driven by an investigation into a killing, disappearance or abduction. Professional or reluctant sleuths follow leads through corrupt technological power to find a person or expose the crime. Invasive technology is both evidence and danger. A paid rescue belongs here when solving the case governs the whole story, rather than a sequence of operational assignments.
- Cybernoir operatives: Cyberpunk noir driven by dangerous field assignments carried out by assassins, mercenaries or corporate and government agents. Protection, infiltration and counterterror operations expose betrayals by their handlers, while invasive technology makes the operatives useful and expendable. The mission and its compromised execution govern, rather than a primarily retrospective case or a continuing life on the run.
- Cybernoir fugitives: Cyberpunk noir driven by an outsider's continuing struggle to evade, escape or turn against the high-tech powers hunting and using them. Identities, bodies and loyalties are expendable as pursuit and survival carry the story beyond any single case or assignment. Counterattack can change the system without making institution-building the governing experience.

## Why this distribution
The concepts emerge from actual verbs shared by the novels: investigate/trace/find; protect/infiltrate/execute; evade/escape/survive. It is not technology-type slicing, which would overlap body engineering, neural interfaces, networks, clones and AI throughout most of the ten. Nor is it hired versus ordinary people: Kobo is employed, Idolon has workers, and most of the operatives become hunted. A two-way case-versus-mission split strands Shockwave Rider and distorts the eight-novel Cates arc. A job-versus-survival split dumps every detective into jobs and blurs the point of the cases. The 4/4/2 structure preserves those distinctions without a residual bucket.

All ten still pass the existing root path: speculative mechanisms, scientific rather than literal magical engines, extrapolated Earth futures, techno power shaping lives, invasive cyberpunk society, then hardboiled crime/conspiracy/survival. Existing Postcyberpunk requires absence of classic noir alienation, not merely having friends or a salary. Metaverse/Neural describe governing virtual experience, not any interface. Biotech alone does not force Altered flesh when a crime or operative thriller governs. No existing ancestor definition needs widening.
