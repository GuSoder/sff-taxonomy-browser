## Root-down walk

Speculative fiction > Science fiction: the read sources describe a future African-diaspora civilization, AI, biotechnology, space vessels and alien encounters. Spirituality, ancestral respect and naming ritual are not by themselves proof of literal magic. The author's description of the Orun Gate as an artificial wormhole and the final-volume review's account of ancestral intelligence support an SF frame. Agranoff's loose description of light fantastical elements does not identify a magical mechanism that would override the SF evidence. Fantasy, including portal fantasy, is not established.

Science fiction > Atemporal SF > Time travel fiction: the original Rebel space opera placement describes the political reading engine well, but does not survive the owner's any-included-novel-time-travel rule. SKJAM's completed review of Sweep of Stars explicitly confirms accidental background time travel. Agranoff's first-novel review and later sequel review explain that a displaced crew used modern technology to rebuild in the past, hiding on the Moon and creating the Dreaming City. Thus this is actual pastward displacement, not the figurative language of the finale blurb and not merely generations passing aboard a spaceship. Historical, Modern-day and Futurist SF lose at this earlier temporal gate. Owner clarification phonemsg-01M850W64E1MFDAN0BQD84RRP9 excludes short works from the test, but these events are reported in the included first novel.

Within Atemporal SF, this is not chiefly a parallel-world cosmology, fabulation, New Weird, reincarnation or science-fantasy setting. Literal movement through time supplies the more specific parent.

Time travel fiction > Timeslips: the verified event is an accidental group displacement whose detailed physical mechanism remains unexplained in the sources read. No source establishes a deliberately operated time apparatus. The author's artificial Orun Gate is a spatial wormhole, not proof that it caused the earlier accident or that a temporal institution owns a machine. Time machines and its agency/homebrew descendants therefore lack their positive apparatus test. Hard time travel lacks a rigorously developed mechanism central to the fiction. Ancestral time travel is not established by African ancestry or ancestral AI: no source shows kinship determining temporal destination. Mind over time, Time travel disorders and Time tonics lack altered-state, bodily-condition and substance causes. Time loops lacks repeated reliving; Time distortion is not genuine pastward travel; Time door lacks a standing passage used at will; Time crossroads lacks a timeless hub.

Timeslips > Time strays > Timetribe: the relevant displaced unit is a crew who create a community in their new era. The consequences of their building the Dreaming City support community displacement rather than a solitary stranded traveler. Time bleeds has no traveling people; Time tangles requires intertwined uncontrolled crossings to drive the whole fiction; Timetide requires recurrent unscheduled out-and-back travel. None is established. The detailed founding event makes Timetribe the honest existing leaf, 7 to 8, even though the subsequent three-novel story remains a broad political space opera. This applies owner phonemsg-01M6XKB91QD65FFYNC4FDMM2H4: absent an overarching matching genre, a defining concept in a subset of the included books can support the fiction's shelf.

## Whole-trilogy and rival checks

Book 1 establishes the lunar community, attacks from Original Earth, leadership crisis, Cypher trouble and an alien conflict. Book 2 continues the same people, institutions and crises, with captives on Earth and stranded personnel beyond the gate. Book 3 resolves these strands and follows a crashed Cypher community through generations. Generational elapsed time in book 3 is not independently counted as time travel. Political community-building is a strong common theme, but cannot bypass the prior temporal rule. No extra fiction card, new genre, portrait, definition change or structural approval is needed.
