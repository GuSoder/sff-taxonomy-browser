# Daughter from the Dark, MC06384, 23 November 2026

Place the one eligible novel in Magic family fantasy, seven to eight fictions. Strong domestic-parent fit, moderate fine-leaf fit: the magical child is not proven biological kin, and the book is an uneasy, at times cruel, formation of family rather than a warm domestic fantasy. No definition widened. The existing leaf includes found household/coven bonds, not only hereditary witch bloodlines. One novel, selected English edition 2020, original publication 2006.

## Actual-tree walk

Speculative fiction > Fantasy > Real earth fantasy > Contemporary fantasy > Domestic fantasy > Magic family fantasy.

Literal supernatural agency is established by the transformed protective bear, otherworldly music and documents/reality altered around Alyona, even though the precise cosmology and her account of her origins remain ambiguous. Do not translate that ambiguity into scientific aliens, a proven demon, a Christian angel, or a mundane scam. The novel grows out of modern Russian urban life, radio, clubs, an apartment and a working DJ. It is not an independently invented Fantasy realm, historical period or speculative future.

Aspirin takes in a strange child whom he cannot get rid of. Her search for her brother, supernatural music, teddy-bear protector and uncertain identity disrupt his household and his shallow routines. Their changing ties, parental responsibility, dependence and eventual attachment govern the narrative. Kirkus explicitly describes his transformation toward love and parenthood. Publishers Weekly, quoted on the current publisher page, identifies found family at the heart of the novel. Detailed independent reviews, including unfavorable completed-reader accounts, agree that the main substance is Aspirin's relationship with Alyona, not a developed otherworld adventure. Magic lives in the people and their relationships, not an inherited house, library, land or pact. Therefore Magic family rather than Magic house.

## Sibling and ancestor audit

- Dark fantasy/Torment is the strongest rival. Alyona's coerced fatherhood and the teddy bear create genuine unease. Impostor torment specifically requires a nonhuman intruder using false intimate belonging to control and prey on people. Control is present, but a predatory false-family mechanism governing the full book is not established: the relationship develops toward care and responsibility, the bear protects the child, and her brother search is not exposed as a feeding fraud. Do not import Bedfellow's predation just because papers are fabricated. No ancient god, demon, Satan, primordial race or vengeance entity is established for the other torment siblings. Horror fantasy's place-bound menace is not the engine.
- Portal fantasy is a real secondary overlap: Alyona says she and her brother came from a perfect other plane and plan to return. But the other place remains uncertain and largely undescribed; the novel's work is the changed modern household and creative relationship, not crossing and exploring a system of worlds. No anchored door or text crossing is established. World bleeds concerns a world-leak whose effects are the engine, not merely otherworldly people developing a domestic relationship. No hidden world is found or reconciled, and no multiworld/planetary journey is established. Remain Earth-rooted Contemporary.
- Supernatural fantasy's Paranormal leaf is broad but less exact than the central household bond. No established ghost, vampire, dragon, werewolf, unnatural-life-extension project, soul bargain or afterlife journey. Calling her like a fallen angel is a critic's simile, not verified biblical identity.
- Contemporary mythic is a real Piper allusion, but the inherited being does not organize the eligible novel as a sustained retelling. The supernatural child and adult's domestic relationship does. Do not let an excluded short work set the whole novel's shelf.
- Magical coming-of-age does not fit an adult DJ's moral growth as an age label, and the child does not undertake a school or ancestry/awakened-magic plot. Violin lessons do not make an academy of magic the governing institution.
- Urban fantasy is a setting overlap, but no investigator/service caseload, hidden magical people, supernaturally organized city, Gothic haunting, Western frontier or weird-science weapon engine. Magic realism loses because the uncanny is actively contested and feared, not ordinarily accepted. Folktale humor and animal-tale structure do not govern.
- No literal role-playing fantasy, fairy-tale revision in an independent realm, SF demystification, historical myth retelling or technological premise established. No new music genre needed because the existing domestic leaf honestly holds the novel's governing relationship.

## Scope and date reconciliation

Current publisher calls this a stand-alone novel. Russian bibliographic cycle Krysolov/Pied Piper lists two works: The Burned Tower (1998, short novella) and Alyona and Aspirin (2006, the original title of Daughter from the Dark). Reader commentary describes the link as one recurrent Piper crossing otherwise separate settings, without continuing plot. This is not evidence for merging Vita Nostra, whose connections are thematic reader speculation only.

Under the novel-only intake, The Burned Tower is excluded as short fiction. It is not added to this card and does not drive this included novel's temporal placement. Creator site legally hosts an excerpt of Burned Tower: fetch ends mid-narrative, so do not claim to have read its full ending. Whether the Piper connection counts as a strict shared universe rather than a cameo does not change eligible scope today: one eligible novel either way. Preserve the link in the record so a later eligible novel prompts a fresh scope check. Do not silently assert that every Dyachenko book shares a world.

FantLab establishes original 2006 publication, with Ukrainian translation appearing some months before the Russian edition; selected English Harper Voyager print edition is 11 February 2020. Record original_publication_year 2006 separately, fiction first_published 2006, selected edition year 2020. The English title/cover is not an edition of Vita Nostra.
