## Each novel's own engine
Shadow and Bone2012: orphan soldier/cartographer Alina reveals SunSummoner power in a Fold attack; Darkling takes her to palace for training. Discovering and controlling her magic, manipulation by magical/military elite, amplifiers and threatened use of her power define coming-of-age plot. Not simply subsequent king's dynastic story.
Siege and Storm2013: Darkling pursues amplifiers and Alina's power to take throne; privateer Sturmhond/Nikolai's claim, Alina's Grisha army command, saintly authority and romantic ties compete. Kirkus explicitly changes genre emphasis to political thriller.
Ruin and Rising2014: Alina leaves Apparat's zealot protection with surviving Grisha, seeks Nikolai and third amplifier/firebird against Darkling and divided Ravka. Kirkus calls this epic quest, not uniform court plot. Power's costs and capacity to control it remain causal.
Six of Crows2015: disabled crime prodigy Kaz assembles six outcasts to extract a foreign chemist for merchant reward. Grisha-hunter Matthias, Heartrender Nina and escaped slave Inej contribute distinct motives. Heist engine and found-family are real, not falsely a royal court. Technology develops alongside coveted and despised magic; jurda parem discovery makes control of Grisha power the stakes of international commerce and state violence.
Crooked Kingdom2016: doublecrossed crew rescues Inej and schemes revenge against merchant princes; Kuwei is hunted by powers seeking secrets of jurda parem. Nina's addiction/changed powers, experiments, market and state competition over the drug connect personal heist payoff to Grisha world's balance. Author explicitly a war for revenge/redemption deciding Grishaworld. Caper strong for these2 only.
King of Scars2019: Nikolai's state survival, debt/diplomacy/marriage, pretenders and internal monstrous curse; Zoya and Grisha powers; Nina's rescue/recruitment and altered ability uncover poisoned waters/secretfactory. Court and dynastic stakes substantial, not universal over earlier5.
Rule of Wolves2021: Fjerda invasion, Nikolai parentage/diplomacy, Zoya becoming weapon her country needs, Nina undercover manipulating royals and attitudes toward Grisha, Darkling/blight; completed Kirkus identifies warfare arms race. Government control/exploitation of magical people and weaponizable powers brings strands together.

## Actual root walk
Speculative -> Fantasy: SmallScience named scientific in-world but supernatural affinity, saints, shadowcreation and transformation; author calls it a magic system guided by molecularchemistry, not science-fiction explanation. FantasyRealms: Ravka/Kerch/Fjerda/Shu invented despite recognizable real-world analogues, not historical/contemporaryEarth, no Earth-crossingPortal frame. Realm branches: Epic passes cross-national wars, magical order and realized world's power institutions. Sword&Sorcery/Caper strongest Crows2 rival, but not whole7 intimate heist/combat adventure. Fairytale no deliberate fairy-tale grammar or inherited-tale revision. FarShores fails counterpart-cultural aboutness as governing whole, not awarded for Russian dress. Comic fails governing parody/absurdity. Heart fantasy strong romantic/found-family strands, but whole power/control conflict not a single bond spine, no cozy/horse. Gothic fear/monstrouscurse strong Scars but not menace/enclosure governing whole.
Epic: Political positive across7 contests over power, supernatural people, state/military/merchant exploitation and resistance. HighFantasy governing journey fails whole (Ruin is quest but Crows/Kings aren't uniformquest). HeroicRPGclass fails multi-power nonmagical/magicalensemble and political institutions; not Army/Soldier military whole. Mythological saints and later divineencounters are real but not sustained divineidentity/worship/mythpast as whole subject. HardFantasy is serious rival: author explicitly molecularchemistry rules and consequences. It is not dismissed as soft. Yet those rules are load-bearing instruments in struggles over people/power and weaponization, not the whole narrative's governing inquiry. Channeled leaf specifically draws force from world through body; SmallScience manipulates existingmatter through affinity, so no mechanical equivalence invented to reach thatleaf. Bonded/Scripted not wholeall7 spiritbond or writtenpower. No Hard gate widened.
Political: Court/Dynastic governs Scars2 and some trilogy, but Crows2 criminalcrew/merchantstates and trilogy's changingrise/quest don't pass insidercourtwhole; existing courtspy/statecraft rungs cannot be chosen just because spies/kings exist. Revolutionary contains rebellion/rescue/persecutedclass but no single overthrow/messianic/openrevolt/insidesubversion/outlawnetwork/emancipation engine throughout7; Election no massfranchise. MagicPolitics positively covers public authority/control of supernaturalpower: Alina's recruitment/forcedamplifierbond/weaponization, drug/chemist race and Grisha commodification in Crows, state protection/persecution/experimentation/armsrace in Scars. Definition not widened. Wholegenre1to2; Dynastic8to7. Main title now Grishaverse, originalIDkept as stable canonical identifier, old title recorded.
