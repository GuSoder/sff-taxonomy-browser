Required Channeled hard magic frozen-member research. Existing two novels retained: Silvercloak2025 and Turncloak2026. Third novel is being written in the author's September2026 interview and is not added.

Speculative fiction > Fantasy > Fantasy realms > Epic fantasy > Hard fantasy > Time magic.

Invented Atherin, mages, spells, wands, prophecy and magical time-rewriting; not scientific time travel or actual Earth. No fairy-tale source, heritage school, culture-aboutness or governing comedy. Epic fantasy is positive: a continent-spanning struggle in an invented world with consequences for its political order and timeline. The author calls it expansive epic fantasy and expressly rejects romantasy because the central romance is secondary to the main storyline. Character desire and grief remain important, not dismissed.

The undercover detective plot governs the opening, but cannot alone describe the whole two-novel fiction: book1's final choices and book2's central quest are to rewrite lives through Timeweaving. Neither PI-by-trade nor one gang's ordinary police investigation is the sustained engine. Dynastic legitimacy, forbidden necromancy and villainous choices are important alternatives; the characters' efforts to alter past deaths through time are the actual domain driving them.

Hard fantasy: learnable, bounded magic, finite personal reserves, particular wand requirements and material to acquire. Time magic rather than Channeled: pleasure/pain describes how power is fueled, but not what the central power DOES. The author's full book1 spoiler recap explicitly has Saffron reverse minutes in the kingpin confrontation, freeze the final battle, kill Levan, rewind and choose him, then spend her last reserves reversing seconds to save Auria. The climax's alternatives are actual rewritten timelines, not prophecy or hypothetical regrets. She ends by deciding to reverse decades, to save her parents. Turncloak's publisher makes the quest to unmake time central; two completed reader reviews describe the shared goal of reversing21years, undoing parents' deaths, its cost, grief and dangerous consequences. Third POV detective and other magic types do not negate that sustained whole-fiction engine.
