# The Book of Joby, whole-fiction hard walk
24 November 2026

## Verdict
Place standalone The Book of Joby by Mark J. Ferrari (2007) in existing Satanic torment,4to5fictions. No structural or definition change. Moderate-high placement confidence; Modern biblical myth and Arthur returned are genuine rivals, not dismissed as absent.

## Corpus and date
One complete novel. The author's September2007interview explicitly discusses fitting the standalone into one volume and calls his next project unrelated. No sequel established by the current author site or bibliography. The ISFDB title-level2004date conflicts with Tor2007and is not silently adopted: the same interview explains an announced May2004release at a different publisher was canceled about six weeks before publication. The author's biography confirms Tor2007; NYPL catalogs a2007first edition; ISFDB's hardcover publication record itself gives August2007and first printing of first edition. Use2007, not the canceled schedule or2012mass-market edition.

## Story evidence
Primary author synopsis: Lucifer and the Creator wager the continued existence of creation upon California-born Joby Peterson. Lucifer systematically erodes his childhood hope, relationships and adult life; angels struggle with obedience and intervention. Joby eventually finds refuge in an unusual seaside community, but the destructive forces follow him there and turn that refuge against him. The author interview identifies the story's sustained concerns as undeserved suffering, anger, free will and kinds of justice, and explains that the divine figures act as a container and chorus around the human drama.

Kirkus and Publishers Weekly corroborate the repeated personal harassment, ruined studies, work and love, with escalating tragedies intended to make Joby renounce God. Black Gate gives the Arthurian machinery: Joby, Ben and Laura are Arthur, Lancelot and Guinevere reincarnated; grandfather Merlin hides the Grail at Taubolt. Lucifer orders the destruction of both Joby and the refuge when he discovers them. Fantasy Book Critic's completed review describes a resolved one-volume story, active God/Lucifer encounters in everyday venues, and the Arthurian figures integrated into the Heaven-versus-Hell conflict. No full-book text was obtained; specific final mechanics beyond these accounts are not invented.

## Root-down walk
Speculative fiction: genuine God, Lucifer, angels, reincarnation and Grail magic, not secular social suffering alone.
Fantasy rather than Science fiction: no naturalistic, technological or staged explanation for the supernatural; the author explicitly calls the divine material fantasy. Godgame under SF/Fabulation has a tempting literal wager but fails the SF ancestor. A game word is not sufficient to cross roots.
Fantasy > Real earth fantasy: ordinary California and a fictional Northern California coastal town within our Earth, not an independently invented world. Fantasy realms fails Earth. Portal fantasy fails as governing engine: a magically protected town and angelic/diabolical realms do not establish a plot organized around exchange with another world.
Real earth > Contemporary fantasy: modern California across one human lifetime, explicitly contemporary in author biography and reviews. Historical fantasy fails: this is neither Job in the ancient world nor Arthur in his own time. Biblical fantasy/Mythic retelling is a real thematic connection but not the actual temporal setting. Future fantasy, biblical future End-times and SF apocalypse fail: the threatened destruction is a stake of the present-day wager, not a future civilization or life after an apocalypse.
Contemporary > Dark fantasy: this is a sustained personalized supernatural persecution. Neither family/house magic nor the discovery of a young magician's abilities explains the adult half of the book. Urban fantasy fails a governing city/supernatural-institution mechanism; magic realism fails the extraordinary contest and suffering rather than ordinary communal acceptance; folktale/comic and animal branches fail the human persecution plot. Supernatural fantasy is broad enough in subject, but its evil-deals leaves would require the protagonist's bargain or temptation mechanism: the defining wager here is imposed by God and Lucifer, not freely entered by Joby. Immortality, ghost, vampire, werewolf, dragon and afterlife journey mechanisms fail.
Dark > Torment fantasy rather than Horror fantasy: Lucifer's agents pursue Joby through life and into his sanctuary. The menace is not a cursed place that catches whoever enters. Taubolt is a defensive refuge attacked because Joby and the Grail are there.
Torment > Satanic torment: Satan/Lucifer and his minions specifically torment a chosen human to break him. Not Elder god torment, primordial species, generic attaching demon, vengeful dead or intimate impostor: the source of the pursuit is explicitly Lucifer's wager, not any of those causes.

## Strong rival routes and wobble
Modern biblical myth passes all its ancestors and its leaf: the Creator, Lucifer and angels act in present-day life, including ordinary restaurants and bars. This was the initial leaning. It loses to the more specific enacted mechanism: not merely biblical powers manifesting today, but a decades-long campaign against one victim, continuing even when he changes place. Satanic torment names exactly what drives the human plot; biblical cosmology supplies the frame and stakes. God is also active, so this is not a claim that biblical myth is absent, nor a new reciprocal priority rule for other members.
Arthur returned also passes Contemporary mythic fantasy > Modern European myth > Modern Celtic myth. Arthurian reincarnation and the actual Grail/Merlin are substantial, not metaphors. But their roles are Joby's identity, the recurrence of a love triangle, and the defense of a sanctuary in the contest imposed by Lucifer. The novel is not primarily a restored Arthurian Britain, new Round Table quest or Grail hunt. The author describes Job's predicament as the framework and the Arthurian material as character clothing/personal mythic idiom. This supports the torment choice without pretending the overlap is clean.
Biblical fantasy under Historical fails its ancestor despite the modern Job adaptation. Godgame fails its SF ancestor despite a literal cosmic wager. No seed needed when an existing leaf names the positive dominant mechanism honestly. No auditing or moving other leaf members under this decision.
