## Root-down walk
Speculative fiction is established by real ritual magic, reincarnation, supernatural intervention and the Atlantean priesthood, not unverified supernatural advertising alone. Fantasy, not SF: the completed occult criticism and historical creator accounts describe genuine spiritual powers and reincarnation, not a technological or psychological explanation. Do not import SF's Reincarnation fiction merely because the mechanism word matches.

Fantasy > Real earth fantasy, not Fantasy realms: the named Britain, Roman and Arthurian history and modern American settings are our Earth; Atlantis is that Earth's imagined prehistory. Not Portal fantasy: other planes and magical states occur, but the whole reading engine is recurrent souls and a continuing Earth-rooted tradition, not Earth/otherworld exchange. The modern occult novels alone have Gothic/occult machinery, but whole-world scope controls.

Real earth > Historical fantasy, not Contemporary or Future: the substantial ancient-to-Arthurian half cannot honestly be called present-day. Seven modern occult novels do not constitute a single framing narration enclosing all historical books. The world's substantial long arc begins in Atlantis and continues through Britain to modern America. Future has no substantial future-era narrative.

Historical > Period fantasy > Long chronicle fantasy. No single-era band is honest: imagined prehistory, Bronze Age Britain, Roman Britain, Arthurian Britain and contemporary America. The priestly tradition, recurring souls, initiation, abuse of occult power and spiritual atonement persist through the sequence. Industrial chronicles requires an industrial-age start; Electric chronicles requires the substantial span after1900. Neither is true. Antique times alone excludes the modern books.

Historical alternatives tested: Mythic retelling/Arthur reimagined fits Mists and the Arthurian part, not the Atlantis and seven modern occult novels. Other myth-root children do not supply a universal reincarnation shelf. Secret history/Occult cabals has real occult organizations and history overlays, but hidden explanation of documented history is not the whole-world engine, and Atlantis's events are not that sort of historical concealment. Alternate history fails an openly divergent historical timeline as defining spine. Not a choice made to preserve an old placement or fill a leaf.

Contemporary counterfactual: Occult orders is a strong fit for the modern seven, Gothic occult horror is relevant to their menace, and modern mythic powers are relevant. Each fails the substantial ancient and historical books. No supernatural-kind branch overrides its contemporary ancestor merely because reincarnation is supernatural. Historical Arthur reimagined likewise fails the modern books and most pre-Arthurian material.

## Definition wobble and precedent
Long chronicle's own sentence says Renaissance to modern. Its accepted Numinous World tenant explicitly begins with Troy/Egypt/Alexander and continues through Napoleon, using the same reincarnating-soul device. Industrial chronicles' sibling exclusion assigns substantial spans beginning before industrialization to Long chronicle. Vampire Chronicles also has antiquity-to-modern reach. Applying that already-used span gate is an ordinary whole-fiction placement, not a silent definition widening. No gate text is changed. Confidence moderate because this is a broad connected world, not one continuously narrated novel; the original-world grouping is mandatory.
