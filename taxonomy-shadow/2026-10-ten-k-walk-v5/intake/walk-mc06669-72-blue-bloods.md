# Blue Bloods universe whole-fiction walk, 27 November 2026

Queue MC06669-72 plus the 13 unqueued titles found by the whole-fiction audit, one fiction, 17 books, Vampire dynasty fantasy.

Root-down: Speculative fiction > Fantasy > Real earth fantasy > Contemporary fantasy > Supernatural fantasy > Vampire fantasy > Vampire world > Oldfangs fantasy > Vampire dynasty fantasy. One shared universe is one fiction, so all seventeen titles of ISFDB's Blue Bloods Universe (https://isfdb.org/cgi-bin/pe.cgi?32742=) are itemised: the nine-book core (Blue Bloods 2006 to After Death 2023; Wikipedia counts seven in the original run, then After Life 2022 and After Death 2023 as a new duology on ISFDB and the author's site), the companions Bloody Valentine and Keys to the Repository (2010), the werewolf spin-off Wolf Pact (2012, issued in four parts, itemised as one book), the Beauchamp Family witch trilogy (Witches of East End 2011, Serpent's Kiss 2012, Winds of Salem 2013) and the adult spin-off The New Blue Bloods Coven (Vampires of Manhattan 2014, White Nights 2017). Left out as non-books: the Blue Bloods Books I-III omnibus (2014), the four Wolf Pact parts, the short story Shelter Island (2007) and the graphic novel. Content decides the leaf. The Blue Bloods are an old-money Manhattan society of vampires, secretly angels reborn across the centuries, bound in houses and families (Van Alen, Force, Llewellyn, Duchesne) with elders, heirs, bondings, feuds and succession, and the stories are told from inside those houses (Schuyler, Mimi, Bliss, Jack). So the vampires long have been what they are (Oldfangs), and the houses, not a senate, drive the plot (Dynasty over Court: the Conclave and the Committee are a backdrop, not the engine). Magic is literal throughout (vampire, angel and witch powers are real), so Fantasy; the supernatural is hidden in contemporary New York and the Hamptons, so not Publicly paranormal or Fangs out. Alternatives tested: Vampire court fantasy (politics is not the engine); Newfangs fantasy (Schuyler's awakening opens book 1 of seventeen and the arc is bigger than the turning); Magic family fantasy (fits only the Beauchamp trilogy and would split the shared universe); Paranormal neighbours (the world is secret). Soft spot for the curator: the leaf definition says houses are made and adopted by the bite, never born, while Blue Bloods are born angels who awaken at fifteen and carry bloodlines. The house-from-inside engine is what the leaf is about and no other leaf fits better, so it is placed here; the clause may want a small wording change (for example 'made and adopted by the bite, or inherited through blood'), which is the curator's to approve.
