## Contradictions and limits
Iolanthe2025 is audio, original2022. Archangel2015 in SFE is contradicted by2017 primary/review evidence. Five books were projected, three are currently published/listed. Marketing treats Spectres as incorporeal aliens; primary sample directly confirms alien reproduction and technological response, not literal ghosts. No completed independent Iolanthe review or final ending text recovered. Do not silently convert the opening sample into a full read.
