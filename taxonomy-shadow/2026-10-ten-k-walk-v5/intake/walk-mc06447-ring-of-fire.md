## Shared continuity, not merely branding
The authorized current magazine describes the Assiti as alien artists whose discarded shards displace pieces of Earth. Its first issue describes five events sharing the original timeline: Grantville, Time Spike, Queen of the Sea, The Crossing and Peterbilt. The publisher's Alexander Inheritance blurb explicitly makes Grantville and the prison earlier catastrophes in the same sequence. Time Spike and The Crossing also carry an investigation and government-coverup thread on original Earth. Different destination histories are therefore branches of one connected multiverse, not independent works linked only by a repeated device. An earlier author-bibliography description saying only the mechanism is shared is weaker than these direct continuity facts. Keep all five shard threads on one fiction.

## Full walk
Speculative fiction -> Science fiction: actual temporal/spatial displacements, attributed to alien phenomena. Historical beliefs in witchcraft and gods are not proof of literal magic. Fantasy's Earth, invented-realm and portal branches do not override this SF mechanism.

Science fiction -> Atemporal SF. Historical SF fails as the canonical route because actual time travel in the included novels overrides the historical-destination surface. Modern day SF fails because the transported people leave modernity; Futurist SF fails because the main lives are in past eras. This repairs the later Age of sail duplicate without changing that leaf's gate.

Atemporal: Fabulation fails, no foregrounded arbitrary storytelling; New Weird fails, not grotesque cross-mode reality; Science fantasy fails, not fantasy-surfaced scientific worldbuilding; Parallel world fiction is secondary to explicit travel across eras; Reincarnation fails, no recurring soul. Time travel fiction passes.

Time travel: Time machines fails, the travelers do not operate a device or institution; Hard time travel fails, alien-art displacement is not rigorously worked physical engineering; Ancestral fails, kinship does not determine the transfers; Time loops fails, not repeated reliving; Mind over time fails, whole bodies and places move; Time travel disorders fails, no bodily condition; Time distortion fails, not unequal clock rates; Time crossroads fails, no outside-time hub; Time tonics fails, no administered substance; Time door fails, no standing passage entered at will. Timeslips passes: the external catastrophic event carries the people.

Timeslips: Time bleeds fails, people actually move; Time tangles fails as the defining engine, because the main communities settle and live in destination eras rather than form an interdependent repeated-crossing knot. Time strays passes.

Time strays: Timestranded fails, not a lone traveler; Timetide fails, not recurring uncontrolled departures and returns; Timetribe passes, towns, prisons, ships and smaller groups are transplanted together and build communities. This is also the specific owner-approved home. Timetribe remains7 fictions; Age of sail alterations drops7to6. Neither reaches ten.
