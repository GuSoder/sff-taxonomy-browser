# Artamon whole fiction repair, 6 November 2026

Seven positively connected novels become one fiction in approved Magic politics, replacing Tears of Artamon4 plus The Alchymist's Legacy2 plus Songspinners. Two existing cards become one; two novels added. Main title retained with expanded scope stated. No Moths to a Flame or The Lost Child folded: comparative chronology and general other-novels comment are insufficient primary identity proof for those two. The secondary overview suggests geographical/theological links, recorded as unconfirmed, not used to merge.

## Every work's own engine
Lord of Snow and Shadows2003: painter Gavril inherits Azhkendir's throne and a dragon daemon needing innocent blood. Eugene's rationalizing empire and guns confront inherited supernatural sovereignty. Dynastic magic strong for this book.
Prisoner of the Iron Tower2004: Gavril expels daemon and is imprisoned; Eugene seeks its power, women sustain rebellion and a spirit quest, rubies reunite imperial sovereignty. Prior2005 was paperback year.
Children of the Serpent Gate2005: imperial human conflict meets escaped daemons and their children; Kiukiu's spirit-singing, hosts' corruption and the Francian anti-magic order bring mythic and state conflicts together. Completed SFFWorld review says daemons increasingly overtake human events, a real mythological rival acknowledged.
Tracing the Shadow2008: inquisition burns magi, apprentice Rieuk blackmailed into supernatural crime, orphan Klervie/Celestine guarded by a spirit becomes a celebrated singer seeking justice. Completed Booksforabuck explicitly says Celestine's growth is main narrative and wars/assassinations/political intrigue hidden undertones, so not ruler-level statecraft.
Flight into Darkness2009: Rieuk must restore guardian Azilis between living/dead while she protects Celestine; inquisition hunts singer's forbidden power, dragon daemons threaten world. Author interview explicitly sets it through/beyond original trilogy from enemy viewpoint. Completed Klausner review corroborates these own stakes, not merely metadata.
Songspinners1996: Orial's forbidden musical gift allows transcribing persecuted composer Khassian's seditious opera; religious agents pursue them, her musical telepathy and lost Lifhendil heritage risk sanity. Author explicitly says few years after Flight with returning characters. Author/Baen primary and Susie Williamson completed review used. Not a dynasty contest or a conventional bard adventure whole.
The Arkhel Conundrum2019: foreign extraction awakens winter deity, clan hatreds and fragile imperial peace return; Gavril/SpiritSinger Kiukiu seek Eugene, distracted by flying-machine contest, while disappeared magus and daughter Larisa's inherited daemon power complicate control. Author explicitly book4 and October2019 print publication. 2020 review date is not first publication.

## Full actual root walk
Speculative -> Fantasy: literal spirits, daemons, telepathy and supernatural ancestry, not scientifically explained SF. Fantasy realms: invented Rossiya/Francia/Tourmalise, not real Earth despite eighteenth-century counterpart vocabulary, no Earth-crossing portal frame. Seven realm branches: Epic passes cross-state politics, cosmology and intertwined magic systems. Sword and sorcery no intimate combat/heist adventure governing whole; Fairy-tale revision no inherited tale or deliberate invented fairy-tale grammar; Far shores fails non-Western counterpart-cultural aboutness; Comic fails governing parody/absurdity; Heart fails romance/cozy/horse bond as spine. Gothic substantial blood appetite, persecution and daemon menace, but whole moves through public authority, religious/magical order and political conflicts, not sustained dread/enclosure; no manners or mystery caseload engine.
Epic children: Political passes struggles over public authority and supernatural power across rulers, states, religious institutions and persecuted people. Heroic class-tree fails ensemble whole (Songspinners musical heroine doesn't make all seven bard adventures); High fantasy fails governing consequential journey over whole; Military fails army/ranks as lens; Hard fantasy fails scientific learnable rules as organizing subject (author says raw magic rational enlightenment cannot explain/contain); Mythological strong rival in Children/Flight/Arkhel, but the whole includes Songspinners' musical telepathy/ancestry rather than a divine-order relationship; not sustained experience of divinity or inherited mythlike past, not real-tradition mythic beings' own war.
Political existing children: Court intrigue/main4 strong, but Legacy/Songspinners are not governed by an insider contest, dynasty or ruler-level statecraft. Court spies and dynastic weapon ladder cannot pass their parent merely because agents/kings appear. Revolutionary real liberation/religious persecution in Legacy/Songspinners but not one mass-uprising/sacred leader/imperial resistance/inside-subversion/rescue/emancipation movement governing main4. Election no mass franchise. Existing whole-tree gates do not fit without stretching.
Approved fourth Political child Magic politics: governing contest is public authority over supernatural power, who may wield it and who suppresses or controls it. Not merely magicians engaging in politics. Court intrigue wins when ruling house/court is contest; Revolutionary when overthrow/liberation is the governing movement; Election mass franchise. Political definition unchanged. Parent is level4 with only3 immediate children but structurally broad, disclosed before approval. Ceiling at approval ONE fiction, not Grishaverse bundled into decision.
