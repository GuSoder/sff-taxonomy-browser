# The Hexologists: whole published fiction re-walk
6 November 2026. MC04976-04977.

## Scope and decision
Two published novels: The Hexologists (26 September 2023), The Hexologists: A Tangle of Time (9 September 2025). First-year2024 in the inherited card was wrong. Publisher lists book3 A Tempest of Titans for1June2027: not published, not counted. Separate world from Books of Babel: author's creation account says he started with a new planet and left the Tower behind. Move existing whole2 from Cozy craft fantasy to existing Magitech,1->2. No new genre or definition change. The detective-story engine versus magic-industrial worldbuilding remains the substantial wobble. No duplicate card or fiction/book-count change.

## Actual content of each novel
Book1: married private investigators Isolde and Warren Wilby investigate a royal heir/blackmail mystery after the king asks to be baked into a cake. Attacks, forbidden magic, alchemical industrial power and forty-year secrets matter to the investigation. They visit a factory and confront an industrialist. Alchemy actually manufactures electrahol by processing organic material through an infernal dimension; that fuel operates vehicles, industrial machines and electric lighting. The process and its cost to the landscape are described, not merely hinted by a Victorian jacket. Strange Horizons independently confirms alchemy powers the industrial nation and its technological development. Isolde's hexegy, unlike alchemy's infrastructure, is a small and unreliable practical art; writing symbols alone does not establish Sanderson-style hard magic.
Book2: an artist's death and a resetting crime scene lead Isolde through a murder investigation as time is rewritten and magic's rules break. Her portalmanteau becomes inaccessible. The mystery threatens the world rather than merely her workshop. Completed reviews confirm the separate time-rewriting engine, the same quasi-Victorian magic/technology world and its polluted industrial city. The Reading Reality reviewer describes sulfurous magic AND technology and the same modernizing culture disparaging hexegy. Do not pretend this is a mechanical time machine: actual time magic is positive, but load-bearing only in this installment and not the whole two-book fiction. The ending restores a viable course through the time tangle; her missing father's story continues.

## Root-down walk
Speculative fiction > Fantasy > Fantasy realms > Sword and Sorcery > Technofantasy > Magitech.

Actual magic, beings and impossible spaces establish Fantasy over a rational SF account. The world is newly invented, as the author explains, not Victorian London despite its influences; no Real Earth Historical/Period shortcut. Portalmanteau dimensions are travel within an invented-world fiction, not the Earth-to-otherworld framing required by Portal.

At Fantasy realms, action/adventure and magical technology provide an existing positive home. Each book carries dangerous investigation, fights and magical objects; the explicit Technofantasy gate accepts technology central to worldbuilding OR story, not solely a machinery-centered plot. Alchemical infrastructure is central to this world's industrial transformation, transport and first-book secrets. It persists as the world of book2 even though the second case's own mechanism is temporal magic. This is stronger than assigning the whole series a workshop/home comfort engine it does not have. Do not claim every case is about electrahol or every gadget is machinery.

Rivals: Epic High quests fail the episodic city investigations. Heroic caster classes describe Isolde but understate the partnership and modern magical problem-solving; no single dark/healing art or defined nature art is the whole fiction's aboutness. Hard/Scripted magic is not justified by chalk sigils alone: Strange Horizons calls hexegy capricious and frequently failing; no fully explicit learnable rule/cost system was verified. Time magic is strong book2 only. Political power drives first-book secrets, not the whole paired-case fiction. Military ranks/campaigns and mythological worship/divinity are not the organizing engine. Fairytale revision/grammar is not a sourced whole-series engine. Far Shores has no non-Western cultural counterpart as its subject. Comic language and absurd incidents are substantial but the plots solve mysteries rather than principally parody a story or satirize an absurd society. Horse bond absent.

Heart and Cozy require positive comfort/community/craft-as-story, not a default for married people or a warm voice. Their affectionate marriage, Warren's food and cheerful resilience matter; both praise and reader disagreement about “cozy” are preserved. But the present Cozy craft definition says the craft/work/small community IS the story. Royal conspiracies and a reality-unraveling murder case are not merely working a comforting trade. Romantasy also fails: this is an already secure marriage, not romantic development coequal with the mysteries. Cozy peeps would likewise mistake the supportive couple for the case-driven narrative.

Gothic realms > Fantasy mystery > Private-eye fantasy was checked seriously: uncanny crimes, monsters and occult systems are essential, and the pair are hired investigators. However the parent gate requires Gothic menace as register and the leaf invokes hardboiled voice. Strange Horizons explicitly says they shrug off an eldritch fight, return to tea and the novel never darkens; the author identifies The Thin Man rather than Marlowe as his model. The second remains witty/madcap despite terrible threats. Monster furniture is not enough to widen Gothic menace or smuggle a general non-Gothic mystery leaf into it. No gate rewritten.

At Technofantasy, the alchemical process is not necromancy-as-engineering, so existing Technomancy fails. No evidence establishes steam as the defining mechanism, so Steampunk's priority is not invoked from vague gaslamp styling. Diesel is not the source. Magitech is positive: actual magic produces the fuel running engineered transport, machines and illumination central to the world's modernization. It has one existing fiction, now two. This is a worldbuilding-led fit with a detective-story wobble, not a claim that Magitech means any magical relic.
