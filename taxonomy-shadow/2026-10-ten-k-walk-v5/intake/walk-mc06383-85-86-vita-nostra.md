## Root-down walk and whole-arc evidence

Speculative fiction > Fantasy > Real earth fantasy > Contemporary fantasy > Magical coming-of-age > Magic school.

Sasha's powers become linguistic and ontological: students literally become Words in the Great Speech that constitutes matter. The title of the Institute of Special Technologies, quantum metaphors and scholarly exercises do not establish an ultimately scientific explanation. Publisher copy calls it magic and the creator calls the original modern literary fantasy. This is Fantasy with philosophical/scientific analogy, not SF.

The opening grows out of recognizable modern Eastern-European everyday life, seaside holiday, secondary-school completion, family and ordinary institutions. Planes, recorded audio coursework, phones and text messages recur. The magical Torpa Institute is not proof of an independently invented universe. Reality is later rewritten, but the fiction remains rooted in the same modern world, not a tour of distinct worlds with Earth exchange as its governing mechanism. Real earth fantasy is therefore stronger than Portal or Fantasy realms. No historical period or future social order is the governing setting. Reality splits and long magical punishments do not establish a whole-fiction crossing between historical, modern and futuristic periods. Retain Contemporary rather than inventing an Atemporal fantasy branch or importing an SF gate.

Book one takes Sasha from high school into a terrifying curriculum whose exercises transform her into a reality-constituting Word. The second continues her third year, redoing the consequences of her earlier exam; relationships and a split self push her toward remaking a world without Fear. In the third she is the provost trying to train the next generation, including her younger brother and the twin sons of her former lover. The students' failure to master the curriculum now threatens the fabric of reality. She has become the sort of coercive teacher she once resisted. The author explicitly says she wanted to see the Institute's academic process from the faculty side, drawing on her own student-to-teacher experience. Independent full-trilogy reviews call this student, graduate and educator progression the sustained metaphor. Magic school is therefore not a first-volume-only shortcut.

Magical coming-of-age remains the whole-fiction parent through the students' metamorphosis into adult and nonhuman identity; the third volume's new young students renew that trajectory even after Sasha herself has become a teacher. Instruction, assignments, exams, failure and pedagogical coercion organize the magic. Birthright or stumbled-into Found magic do not. The inherited shards in volume three are selected and developed through the Institute, not an independent ancestry-claiming engine. Body swap is not the plot.

Contemporary siblings audited: terror/torment is substantial, but comes through the pedagogy rather than replacing it; domestic relations and romance matter but do not govern the fiction; no sustained urban occult investigation or professional supernatural practice; marvelous events are not accepted as ordinary, excluding Magic realism; no inherited myth-beings or folk-tale grammar. The magical cosmology and being more-than-human overlap with supernatural fantasy, but the academic transformation is more exact. Institutional abuse and Sasha's rebellion do not become an invented-world political revolution. Rewriting reality and time are important, but not sufficient to move an Earth-rooted school fiction under invented-realm Hard fantasy/Time magic.

## Scope and dates

The 2025 creator interviews and publisher endpoint call these three connected novels the completed Vita Nostra trilogy: Vita Nostra, Assassin of Reality, School of Shards. The older Metamorphosis label is an associative cycle: the creator explicitly says its novels do not share characters or plot. Digital and other thematic-cycle titles are not added to this fiction. Daughter from the Dark is not merged on thematic resemblance or common authorship.

Creator original-book page establishes Vita Nostra 2007; original-language bibliographic records establish Assassin 2021 and Shards 2024. English editions are 2018/2023/2025. The main book-list years describe the selected English edition, with original_publication_year recorded per volume and 2007 as the fiction's first publication. Do not call the 2023 and 2025 translations the original publication dates. The third title retains the coauthor credit requested by Marina in the 2025 interview, despite being written after Sergey's death.
