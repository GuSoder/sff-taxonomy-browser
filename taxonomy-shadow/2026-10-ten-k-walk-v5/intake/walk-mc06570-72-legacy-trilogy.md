# The Legacy Trilogy whole-fiction walk, 25 November 2026

## Scope and evidence
Three original novels by Matthew Ward: Legacy of Ash2019, Legacy of Steel2020, Legacy of Light2021. The author's official books page and publisher trilogy lists agree. The US2020release of Ash is not its2019original. Soulfire takes place in a completely new world, not one shared-universe card. The Game and The Tribute are same-world short stories, explicitly identified as such by the author; not imported under the novel-only scope. No omnibus or bonus short fiction added.

Creator says the trilogy follows a new generation grappling with inherited mistakes, freedom and damaging personal choices; all three are one connected saga. Author recaps establish the human TressianRepublic/HadariEmpire war, council coup, Southshiresrebellion, Malatriant's enthralment and return, and later intervention by Raven, Jack and Ashana. Steel expands to the war of gods and the threatened Reckoning, which the cast averts while mortal war continues. Light moves five years onward: Melanna protects her throne while LordProtectorViktor seeks forbidden power to restore losses, becomes coercive, and the final war resolves the friendship and state arcs. Completed Light criticism stresses the multiple fronts, rulers, armies, family burdens, moral attrition and final loss/hope. Not classified solely from opening blurb, midpoint military spectacle or one god scene.

## Root-down walk
Speculative fiction passes actual magic, spirits, gods and resurrection. Fantasy wins over SF: no naturalized explanation for those powers. All three Fantasy children considered. Fantasy realms passes invented Aradane, Tressia and Hadari. Real earth fantasy fails no historical/contemporary/futureEarthsetting. Portal fantasy fails Earthcontact: Otherworld and god-realms occur inside an invented cosmology, not travel from ourEarth. Existing individual fiction authority permits placement, not a new gate.

Fantasy realms children: Epic fantasy wins sustained multi-viewpoint worldbuilding, wars, states and gods. Sword and sorcery fits some fighters but its intimate action-forward adventure is not the whole tapestry. Fairytale fantasy fails inherited tale/grammar. Gothic realms is a serious rival for Malatriant, enthralled dead, shadows and mist, but dread/enclosure/investigation/necromantic machinery do not govern the complete trilogy over national war. Far shores fails a culture-aboutness premise. Comic fantasy fails governing comedy. Heart fantasy fails governing romance/cozycommunity/horsebond; loves and friendships carry the war but do not replace it.

All Epic children considered: Military fantasy wins the recurring war, armies, sieges and multi-front campaigns across all three. Political fantasy is the strongest rival: council coup, regional resistance, Melanna's succession and Viktor's authoritarian statecraft are substantial. Its Court intrigue would narrow to court-level governance or succession, Revolutionary to one overthrow/liberation engine, Election to massfranchise, Magic politics to public regulation of supernatural power, Geopolitical to interstate powerrelations. Those describe real threads, but the whole is the broad war tapestry including fieldcombat, civilian costs, undead/demonic interventions and competing rulers. Heroic fantasy fails a governing singlehero/class invitation across the ensemble. Quest fantasy fails journey-as-governingnarrative despite searches and journeys. Hard fantasy fails rules-of-magic-as-centralexperience. Mythological fantasy/Inventedgods is serious because gods act and meddle, but the author's trilogy and completed criticism retain the political/military generationstory rather than sustained divineexperience or worship as the whole genre.

Military children: Army fantasy wins wholewar and multiple polities/fronts. Soldier fantasy fails one-unit-internalexperience. Army children: World-at-war fantasy wins ensemble tapestry. Mud-and-blood fails primarily ranks viewpoint; Command-tent is a serious rival with Viktor/Melannastrategy but omits the many civilian, court, divine, ghost and refugee threads.

World-at-war children: War of humanity wins the TressianRepublic/HadariEmpire and internal human struggle, even with major supernatural powers alongside. War of immortals is the strongest leaf rival: Jack/Raven field revenants and demons, Ashana sponsors the invasion, Rosa becomes divine, and the Reckoning is an explicit danger. The intervening gods do not make the overarching trilogy a war principally of deathless races/empires: mortal polities and their leaders' choices frame its entry and culmination, as with the existing Warofhumanity definition that expressly allows inhuman threads. War on humanity fails a governing enemy seeking the extinction of mankind. Eldritch invasions fails governing externalinvaderincursion; local god-realms and demons do not by themselves establish a whole-fiction outside-worldinvasion. This is a qualified whole-fiction judgment, not denial of the divine war.

Placement: Speculative fiction > Fantasy > Fantasy realms > Epic fantasy > Military fantasy > Army fantasy > World-at-war fantasy > War of humanity,7to8fictions. No new node or definition widened, no cap reached.
