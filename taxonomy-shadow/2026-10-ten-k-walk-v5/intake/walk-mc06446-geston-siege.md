# The Siege of Wonder, whole-fiction walk
24 November 2026

## Verdict
Place standalone The Siege of Wonder (1976) in existing Borderland multiverse fantasy,8to9fictions. Moderate confidence on its dimensional geography, high on literal magic and Earth. No new leaf, definition edit or invented collapse cause. This completes the remaining eligible novel in MC06446's2009omnibus; the earlier connected pair is already placed separately. The Day Star is not added.

## Corpus
Creator introduction says the first two novels are loosely sequel-related and share Havengore, but Siege is a later return to the themes, not a designed trilogy. David Drake's separate introduction calls Siege probably standalone. Same omnibus and thematic cycle do not establish shared continuity. SFE dates the original1976; publisher2011 is ebook reissue. ISFDB and Fantastic Fiction call it third in Wars, a real grouping conflict retained, not used to overrule creator's more specific continuity account.

## Complete narrative and sources
Baen says transdimensional magic-users invaded Earth800years before. Hachette synopsis gives Aden's Special Office spy mission to place an electronic transmitter in a wizard's unicorn, then his divided loyalties and attempt to undo the mission. Gerald Jonas's contemporary1976NYT review gives a physical border, a spy's long stay in magical territory, a sorceress and a unicorn, and the scientists' growing ability to disassemble magical creations by understanding their parallel spectra. This physical border does not itself prove a portal, and was initially a serious obstacle to that inference.

A completed2005SFFWorld review, preserved in a2006Internet Archive snapshot, adds specific geography: magical takeover of parts of Earth including North America, humans living in those countries, bomber passage through changing realities, soul-built city gate and undead cavalry against hovertanks. A completed2011Goodreads review explicitly describes wizards fleeing to other dimensions as the scientists advance, a sorceress's timeless garden and an agent's crossing into that enclave, followed by the Special Office's attack to free him. TVTropes explicitly locates wizard kingdoms in dimensions untouchable by technology, though its mistaken novella label lowers bibliographic confidence. These are community accounts, not three independent primary confirmations. In combination with the primary publisher's transdimensional origin, they support a continuing border of different realities imposed on Earth, rather than a conventional geopolitical frontier only. I do not invent a single hinged doorway, portal apparatus or mapped cosmology.

Completed2018StrangerThanSF review supplies the end: Etridge's unauthorized offensive penetrates the magical countries and destroys the Holy City; Aden is caught there after the sorceress enclave is attacked, and confronts Etridge. The technological side wins rather than the novel simply ending at the unicorn mission. The scientist/magician war and erosion of the magical territories govern the whole fiction. Not a realm-hopping itinerary or one isolated vision.

## Root-down hard walk
Speculative fiction: literal impossible agency and altered realities.
Fantasy rather than Science fiction: author explicitly describes the two sides as science and magic, both real and literal. Scientists measuring magic in spectra does not erase the independent author statement. The actual unicorn, created beings, stolen souls and suspended-time enclave corroborate genuine magic; no proven technological fraud resolves all of them. Preserve the normal SF marketing/critical labels as a custom-tree root conflict. No pseudoscience exemption invented.
Fantasy > Portal fantasy: Earth is explicit. The old transdimensional invasion alone would not suffice. The present war still operates through magic countries described as dimensional domains, altered realities and supernatural enclaves. Aden goes from scientific home territory into that magical domain; its forces occupy and transform Earth, while the opposing incursion into the Holy City concludes the story. Exchange across this standing border is the governing engine, not decorative prehistory. The NYT physical-border phrase and SFFWorld's occupied Earth are compatible with a borderland; neither requires a separate spherical planet.
Portal > Fantasy rifts: no book/text carrier; no one anchored whimsical or nightmare door governs. Distinct realities meet through an extended border and dimensional domains. Not Whimsical doors, Eldritch doors or Transporting tomes.
Fantasy rifts > Multiverse fantasy: the wizard kingdoms and their retreats include other dimensions and sealed temporal realms, a system rather than one Faerie world simply revealed. Community evidence is the moderate-confidence step; no mirror Earth counterparts established. Otherworld fantasy's one hidden mythic world is a real rival, but the multiple dimensional kingdoms/retreats favor Multiverse.
Multiverse > Gateway multiverse: distinct realms in contact, not counterpart Earths.
Gateway > Borderland multiverse: one scientific Earth is home ground, and magical countries press on it through an enduring frontier. Aden's espionage, allegiance and counter-offensive are bound to that standing border. Not Worldhopper: no changing itinerary across many unrelated realms.

## Rival leaves and boundaries
Real earth fantasy > Future fantasy is the strongest rival ancestor: future Earth, centuries-old war and civilization in decay. Its new Dying Earth fantasy leaf is not a convenience bucket: the novel's positive engine is a particular ongoing contest between Earth and invading dimensional magic, not terminal world-age or exhausted sun. Do not merge it with the Geston pair merely because themes recur. Post-apocalyptic > Post Magicalypse cannot be inferred from invasion alone: no sufficiently established civilization-ending ancient fall in this separate continuity, despite James Davis Nicoll's broad post-apocalyptic label. Fantasy after Disaster lacks a verified secular collapse cause. Biblical End-times, future-city vampire return, psychic social order and sustained space civilization fail. Fantasy realms fails Earth; New Weird, science-fantasy and technological war shelves on SF fail literal-supernatural root. A literary allegory about rationalism is not Books in Books, narrative-editing or demonstrated metafiction.

## Confidence and what would change this
Moderate placement confidence, specifically the multiverse ancestor. The physical-border review is not suppressed; an authorized text establishing all magical countries as ordinary transformed Earth land with no continuing dimensional territories would move the choice back to Future fantasy and require an honest terminus decision. Conversely, no need to prove every crossing uses a glowing doorway: existing Borderland expressly allows border strips between mundane and magic. The decision uses positive present-story evidence, not shallow-seed avoidance. No audit of existing leaf members or new structural permissions claimed.
