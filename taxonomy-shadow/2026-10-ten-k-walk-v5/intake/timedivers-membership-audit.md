## Each novel's content
Timediver's Dawn: Query's technological Westron empire has conquered Eastron, whose persecuted witches have hereditary spatial powers. Sammis survives persecution and the catastrophic Frost Giant attack, joins surviving ConFed forces under Odin Thor and develops his own temporal ability. Rebuilding and fighting the aliens bring questions of coercion, survival and founding the future Guard. Public opening shows a witch vanishing and a pursued boy spontaneously moving a third of a world, before any Guard machine. Completed readers explain the genetic inheritance and full societal conflict.
The Timegod: the much later immortal Loki develops unusually strong timediving, trains for and joins the Temporal Guard, and learns its ostensibly protective missions depend on stealing others' technology and suppressing their development. Heimdall's hostility, a failed rescue, punishment and manipulation by the older founders lead him to change his parasitic society's path. Completed detailed review supplies the ending; no full-novel primary read claimed. Author2024explanation explicitly places Sammis/Baldur/Wryan on Terra as the source of Norse myths, rather than proving actual supernatural Norse deities.

## Full root-down walk
Speculative fiction > Science fiction: spacefaring humanlike aliens, engineered equipment, energy scarcity, inherited powers and the causal origin of human myths. Witch/god terminology is not proof of literal supernatural magic. Creator and publisher explicitly identify this two-novel line as SF. The root remains moderate confidence given metaphysical features and Recluce marketing hint; no independent invented-world-only fantasy origin or literal sorcery is established.
SF > Atemporal SF > Time travel fiction: actual journeys and interventions through history override futurist space opera, military conflict, resource ecology and social inquiry surfaces under the owner's any-SF-time-travel rule. No parallel-world-first escape from actual time movement.
Time machines fails before the ownership test. Inherited divers perceive paths, concentrate and cross in mind. Locator bracelets, equipment halls, energy weapons and support technology are not a built causal time machine. The prior rationale jumped directly from Guard institution to machine owner. Institutional employment cannot substitute for apparatus. Natural ability exists in children and civilians and survives the removal of the Guard.
Mind over time positively fits the altered perceptual/mental operation, trained attention and deliberate navigation of temporal paths. The owner's original26October approval phonemsg-01M5W59JNCEDB4ZZY1ZFV939ME, responding to phonemsg-01M5W58B497R1KFMPJAWA8T6HG, explicitly widened this leaf to perception or movement among moments through consciousness. That full exchange was personally recovered. Bodily transport does not by itself defeat a consciousness-driven mechanism.
Time travel disorders is the strongest rival because the aptitude is inherited and biological. It is not depicted as a medical disorder, involuntary sickness, or pathological condition; it is a species-normal faculty developed through attention and training. The biological substrate alone does not put all psychic temporal skills in Disorders. Existing Mind members also possess intrinsic trained powers. This is a moderate-confidence boundary, not an expanded definition.
Hard time travel fails rigorous physical mechanism despite constraints; ancestry does not select the destination (Ancestral); there is no repeatedly lived span (Loops), swallowed tonic, standing unexplained door, hub-and-keeper meeting engine, or dominant unequal clock-rate scenario (Distortion). Timeslips fails a carrying natural event; these are personal acts of attention, not a shift that happens to passive travelers. No seed needed.
