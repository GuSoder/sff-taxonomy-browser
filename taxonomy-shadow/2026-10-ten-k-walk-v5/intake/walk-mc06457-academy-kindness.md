# Academy of Kindness,24November2026

## Decision
Place The Academy of Kindness, anchored by Daughter of Crows (2026), in Paladin hero,3to4. One currently published novel. This is a close class judgment, not a claim that Rue resembles a conventional chivalric knight. Her trained killing and supernatural power are joined in a sacred martial office: a Kindness, made an avatar of the Furies and bound to enforce their ancient justice. The existing Paladin definition selects that combination, rather than costume, weapon or moral goodness.

## Corpus and continuity
PRH records Daughter of Crows, Mark Lawrence, Ace,24March2026. ISFDB confirms2026. HarperReach lists Daughter of Ruin for25March2027, so excluded as future at this24November2026 checkpoint. The fiction is the Academy of Kindness, not a standalone plus a future series duplicate. No creator-confirmed shared continuity with other Lawrence worlds was established. One completed reviewer speculates about Abeth but explicitly retracts certainty; borrowing Earth mythology or recognizing recurring author motifs is not proof of common universe. Preserve that uncertainty, do not merge unrelated cards on speculation.

## Full walk
- Speculative fiction > Fantasy: real necromancy, the Morrigan, raised dead, magical elixirs and altered memory. No source establishes these as demystified technology. Nonlinear childhood, school and old-age strands are flashbacks, not time travel.
- Fantasy > Fantasy realms: the story presently establishes Gog and Magog and invented polities, not contemporary/historical Earth. Author says its mixed Earth mythology has reasons to emerge across the trilogy; do not invent that unrevealed explanation. Portal fantasy fails the Earth-crossing gate. Contemporary Earth Magic school fails its setting ancestor and cannot swallow the mature revenge frame.
- Fantasy realms > Epic fantasy: long journeys, manufactured wars, imperial reach, an old magical kinship, afterlife cosmology and the divine law behind the Academy give the heroic adventure its realized world. Sword and sorcery is a real action-forward rival, but intimate battle celebration alone misses the interwoven life history, sacred institution and cosmic frame. Heart fantasy fails bond-as-spine despite strong friendship. Comic/fairy-tale/counterpart-culture routes lack their engines.
- Gothic realms > Godforsaken realms is the strongest setting rival: ruined families, corpse magic, divine manipulation and repeated returns from death create sustained dread. Nevertheless the governing action is Rue's pursuit of retribution and protection using the office and powers that made her, not exploring a whole world's domination by a dread power. Do not select it merely because the cover and prose are dark. Its9cards do not decide the outcome.
- Epic > Heroic fantasy: political conspiracy and war generate targets, but the novel is not a campaign from the ranks or a court/succession narrative. High fantasy's consequential journey is less specific than the protagonist's acquired power. Mythological fantasy is genuine context, but not principally mythic beings' own personal struggle or a mythology chronicle. Hard fantasy fails: author explicitly prefers magical ambiguity to rule-system foregrounding.
- Heroic > Half-caster: Rue combines learned killing, physical improvisation and assassin training with her own actively wielded necromancy/memory powers. She is neither a no-magic martial rogue nor a pure priest/mage whose weapons are irrelevant.
- Half-caster > Paladin hero: the sacred office is the discriminant. Publisher describes graduates as avatars of vengeance bound to the oldest laws; author says the Academy imposes the Furies' justice without rank privilege and aligns those Furies with a triple goddess. Fantasy Hive's completed review identifies divine power of a dead Fury filling the Kindnesses. This is an armed religious agent, not merely a stealth professional who happens to receive a blessing. Rue's old-age revenge reactivates that same law and charge after retirement.

## Closest class rivals and wobble
Arcane trickster heroes is a serious rival, not a rejected occupation stereotype. Rue's assassin methods and inherited memory gift fit its positive blade-plus-magic core. But its definition excludes sacred office; here divine empowerment and the sacred retributive office are structurally foregrounded across training and old age. Paladin's definition does not require churchly goodness, heavy armour, royal service or a visible sword. Spellsword heroes likewise yields to sacred office and assassin method;9cards there is irrelevant. Sworn assassins has excellent oath/bond semantics but fails its Martial ancestor because Rue genuinely works magic herself. Cleric hero fits divine power but loses the joined martial-office kit. Necromancer describes a major tool, yet its Black Mages parent excludes god/order power and it would reduce this armed avatar to one spell art. Warlock hero lacks established demonic power as primary source.

Wobble: medium. Retired Rue's personal memory gift and independent quest make Arcane trickster plausible; source evidence for divine power joined to the Kindness office is what settles the present novel. Later trilogy revelations may justify a source-grounded rewalk. No definition or existing membership changed to manufacture fit.
