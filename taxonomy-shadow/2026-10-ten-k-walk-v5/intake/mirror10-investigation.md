# Mirror multiverse split investigation
6 November 2026

## Decision
Keep Mirror multiverse fantasy as umbrella, propose Looking-glass selves (6) and Looking-glass worlds (4). No split executed. The axis is what the counterpart relation makes the story about, not the number of worlds, travel mechanism or scale of stakes.

## Distribution and definitions
- Looking-glass selves: living as, confronting or returning as another version of oneself across realities is the governing relation. Apprentice Adept; Slaves of Sleep; Twice Lived; Burn; The Dark World; Moorcock Multiverse.
- Looking-glass worlds: counterpart places and their differences, powers and relationships govern the crossing story. His Dark Materials; The Chronicles of Amber; Shades of Magic; Last Exit.

Both have world threats and personal lives. They are positive engine definitions, not small versus epic stakes. Six/four is honest and balanced; forcing Moorcock across for five/five would make the result less true. Moorcock has many world-order plots, but Champion incarnations and the recurrent Cornelius/von Bek/Glogauer identities are the most specific binding of its 68 novels, already the reason the whole wins Mirror over Worldhopper. Adept genuinely straddles: later generations merge worlds and resist invasion, but Stile/Blue, Mach/Bane and Nepe/Flach keep the counterpart-self relation central. Amber also has Pattern ghosts and doubles; the fourteen-book whole is about Shadow realities, their making and their political/cosmic relations, not one person living alternate lives.

## Root-down review
Speculative fiction > Fantasy > Portal fantasy > Fantasy rifts > Multiverse fantasy > Mirror multiverse fantasy. All ten have actual or unexplained fantastic crossings, not an exclusively scientific engine. Each system includes actual Earth. Adept Earth is expressly present in Split Infinity through the imported plants/horses, parents returning there, Earth music and Stile never having visited it. Current approved Portal definition accepts a system that includes Earth; it does not require every novel to stage Earth scenes. No ancestor widened. Multiple counterpart realities, not one hidden country, organize each whole fiction. The existing whole-fiction matrix below records individual contrasts.

## Naming
Looking-glass is an existing English compound: mirror literally, and through Carroll an alternate fantasy world. It fuses both parent hints without stacking Mirror multiverse <axis>. Selves/worlds names the new axis. Neither pair is claimed as an established genre. Exact usage caveat: Looking glass selves is a sociology chapter and echoes Cooley; Looking-Glass world is Carroll setting. No confirmed exact trademark clash found in the checked search, not legal clearance. Weakest point is that Looking-glass selves can be read as social self-image outside fantasy. Runners-up: Doppelganger lives (loses multiverse hint and suggests impostors); Mirror selves/worlds (drops multiverse hint); Looking-glass lives (exact Felice Picano novel title).

## Amber per-novel engines
### Nine Princes in Amber (1970)
Earth amnesia leads Corwin through Shadow to Amber, a failed throne assault, blinding, regeneration and escape through Dworkin drawing. His curse opens the Black Road.

### The Guns of Avalon (1972)
Corwin seeks Avalon diamonds and powder that will work in Amber; the Black Road and Chaos invasion turn his throne bid into a world crisis.

### Sign of the Unicorn (1975)
Caine murder frames Corwin; Brand rescue, family testimony and the true Pattern reveal an attack on the shared cosmology.

### The Hand of Oberon (1976)
Brand blood-damages the Pattern; Corwin and Benedict stop his use of the Jewel. Ganelon is revealed as Oberon.

### The Courts of Chaos (1978)
Oberon repairs the Pattern while Corwin creates another; Brand falls, Random becomes king and diverts the reality-destroying storm. Amber survives.

### Trumps of Doom (1985)
Merlin investigates annual Earth assassination attempts, Julia and Luke while his Ghostwheel computer can search Shadows. Actual sorcery and Shadow travel coexist with computing.

### Blood of Amber (1986)
Jasra and the Keep of Four Worlds, a tyiga body-hopper and Jurt complicate Merlin investigation and family loyalties.

### Sign of Chaos (1987)
Luke drug-generated Wonderland becomes real Shadow; Mask, Julia and the Keep lead into Chaos succession politics.

### Knight of Shadows (1989)
Pattern and Logrus ghosts press Merlin to choose a side. He repairs a Broken Pattern, keeps Jurt ghost alive, and sees the Jewel implanted in Coral. Real counterparts exist, but their function is the competing world-making powers.

### Prince of Chaos (1991)
Dara and Mandor manipulate Merlin succession, Corwin is rescued, and Merlin claims power without accepting their control. Shadow cosmology and Pattern/Logrus remain the shared engine.

### The Dawn of Amber (2002)
Oberon learns his family belongs to Chaos as his home is threatened; Dworkin leads him through worlds and reveals his inherited role.

### Chaos and Amber (2003)
Oberon enters Chaos family intrigue, faces threats from relatives, and learns the forces that will make Amber possible.

### To Rule in Amber (2004)
Oberon works with Dworkin toward a new Pattern and a new kingdom while war threatens their escape from Chaos.

### Shadows of Amber (2005)
New Amber faces Chaos forces, a monster and treachery; Oberon must defend the reality that his family has made.
