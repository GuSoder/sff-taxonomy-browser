Each volume's own engine:
1. Mazes: Kinders fever kills the Eminence and disproportionately threatens the inbred Grobal nobility. Tagaret enters competition for Heir to rescue his mother and marry Della; brother Nekantor manipulates the contest and servant relationships. Seven caste cultures, purity rules and sociological consequences drive the succession story. Technology: electriclights,maglev,palm-scanners,medicalcare. Hereditaryrank is not evidence of genetic engineering; author explains inbreeding and social control.
2. Transgressions: thirteen years later, Della/Tagaret seek reform and reproductive survival in Selimna; Nekantor schemes to become Eminence and control younger Adon. Arissen officer Melin adds surface expedition and policing perspective; Pyaras's Fall down the caste hierarchy becomes an actual plot turning point. Caste boundaries and government legitimacy, not just bookone'sfever, organize booktwo.
