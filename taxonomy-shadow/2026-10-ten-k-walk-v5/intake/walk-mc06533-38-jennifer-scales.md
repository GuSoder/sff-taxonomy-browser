# Jennifer Scales: whole-fiction intake
24 November2026

## Verdict
One six-novel **Jennifer Scales** fiction by **MaryJanice Davidson and Anthony Alongi**, to existing **Contemporary dragons**,2to3. No new structure or portrait. The common world is modern Earth's hidden dragon shapeshifters, beaststalker enemies, mixed lineages and fights over coexistence. This persists when adolescent Jennifer's viewpoint gives way to family histories, siege survival and finally adult police/FBI investigation. Wildblood is an honest opening-book rival, not the strongest promise for all six. Whole-fiction rule permits a famous defining concept, not every book requiring a juvenile discovery plot. Both authors'2010interview explicitly calls it a Jennifer Scales universe in which Jennifer may play a minor role, anticipating the last book.

## Actual root-down walk
- **Speculative fiction > Fantasy**, not Science fiction: weredragon transformation, magic sanctuary, sorcery and timeline-rewriting spells, magical containment and creature lineages. Scientific labels such as genetics do not demystify these mechanisms. No reason to apply SF-only Atemporal rule to fantasy history revision.
- **Fantasy > Real earth fantasy**, not independently invented Fantasy realms. Contemporary Minnesota/Winoka, humans, modern schools, law enforcement and US agencies anchor the world. **Portal fantasy** is a rival because arachnid dimensions and altered history occur, but the fiction's primary site and identity are Earth-rooted shapeshifters; no sustained Earth-to-independent-world journey organizes the six novels. Real earth's current gate expressly includes dimensions without automatically leaving its branch.
- **Real earth > Contemporary fantasy**, not Historical or Future. Modern school/family/civic institutions, cars, hospitals and federal investigation, no purposeful past-era portrait or future society. Third book's magically overwritten history is a major episode, not a historical-era setting requirement. The final book's old twenty-year case is not proof that the whole novel is futurist; ordinary US institutions and dragon-secret investigation remain.
- **Contemporary > Supernatural fantasy**: dragons/werachnids, their identities, transformations and coexistence are continuing subject. **Magical coming-of-age > Birthright > Wildblood** genuinely fits initial discovery and her inherited nonhuman nature; Mageblood/Witchblood fail craft specifics, Magic school fails normal high school, Found magic fails heredity. Wildblood loses to creature-world theme across family prehistory, adult conflict, war and sixth-book investigators. **Domestic > Magic family** is genuine due mixed parents/siblings, but repeated wars, refuges and non-family polity extend beyond household ties as primary site. Magic house fails inherited place. **Urban fantasy** is real secondary register, but neither the city-as-being nor fae courts nor occult protection service governs. Occult mystery/cops would overweight final volume. Publicly paranormal/Paranormal aftermath becomes relevant when secrecy collapses late, not an assumed settled-public-supernatural society across the saga. **Dark fantasy** fits increasing menace but is not a stable whole-fiction haunting/torment register. **Folktale > Earthly comic** would promise comedy the fifth/sixth deny; **Fables** would mistake transforming humans for animal-tale tradition. Contemporary mythic fails a particular inherited myth system; magical realism fails explicitly named, contested supernatural order.
- **Supernatural > Contemporary dragons**, not vampire, ghost, haunted house, werewolf, unnatural deathlessness, evil bargain or afterlife subgenres. The actual leaf specifically names modern dragon shapeshifters, septs and politics. Broad Paranormal fantasy is less specific. Magical morphing dragon families organize the larger tale even when were-arachnids and human authorities dominate particular conflicts. Contemporary dragons currently2; placing one fiction makes3, no split.

## Confidence and limits
High corpus/date/coauthor confidence. Moderate-to-high whole-fiction home. Complete novel texts not read; completed criticism and reader accounts used beside primary series and jackets. Evangelina's exact final twist and third book's restoration mechanics remain unverified, not invented. Evangelos/Evangelina naming or identity history not reduced to a guessed gender-change explanation. Author interview supports universe continuity, not that a later planned novel actually appeared. Claim only six published novels confirmed.
