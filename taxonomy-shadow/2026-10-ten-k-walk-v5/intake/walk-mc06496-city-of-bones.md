## Verdict
Speculative fiction > Fantasy > Fantasy realms > Sword and Sorcery > Technofantasy > Magitech. Existing leaf2to3. No structural change, definition widening or user decision required. Moderate-confidence governing-engine call. Most serious rivals are Gothic Technomancy and Caper fantasy, with the substantial menace/intrigue strands retained below.

## Corpus and reality

Creator places it among Other Fantasy Novels; independent reviewers consistently call it standalone, not an Ile-Rien/Raksura/Murderbot connection. No such cross-universe connection invented. ## Actual whole novel, including resolution
Ancient magic/technology summoned Inhabitants whose presence nearly destroyed the world; the Ancients' struggle to repel them scorched seas into the Waste. The engineered krismen were made to survive it. A millennium later, relic trader Khat and human partner Sagai live precariously in tiered Charisat. Warder Elen and a Patrician hire/coerce Khat to explore a Remnant and recover artifacts thought capable of assembling an arcane engine to amplify magical powers. Pirate attack leaves Khat and Elen cooperating. Back in the city, relic recovery, translation of an Ancient book and factional betrayals expose the device's real purpose and a bargain with an Inhabitant. Opening the Ancient doorway would release predatory beings from their doomed reality and repeat the catastrophe. Khat, Elen and Constans prevent it, at personal cost. Elen becomes MasterWarder; Khat/Sagai and their families leave for Kenniliar. Kirkus1995and Reactor2025support the reveal and stakes, not merely publisher mystery language. No literal time travel is claimed; millennium backstory is chronology, not transport.

## Full root-down
Fantasy: real sorcery, mental powers, trans-reality beings and arcane engines. The SF edge and engineered biology are genuine but do not erase positive magic or justify inventing scientific explanation. Creator calls it fantasy/secondaryworld. Pure mundane horror fails. RealEarth fails, explicitly invented Charisat not our future Earth; consequently Futurefantasy/PostMagicalypse fails its ancestor despite apocalypse similarity. Portal requires Earth-to-otherworld; Inhabitants arriving from another reality do not establish Earth. This rule is applied before any mechanism shortcut.

Fantasyrealms: not inherited fairy-tale grammar or retelling. Not comedy/parody, bond-led romantasy/cozy/horse, nor a fiction about a particular counterpart culture. Khayal is an understandable marketing cousin (old LibraryJournalMiddleEasternlegends blurb), but authornote and specific narrative are relic-engine adventure; Reactor questions whether any particular Arabian source is material. Setting flavor alone fails Farshores aboutness.

Epic tested: magic is not a comprehensively learnable/costed system central to reading, so Hardfantasy not justified by machinery language alone. Military fails, no soldier/campaign story. Mythological fails, invented-cosmology register is not the book's main work. Political intrigue is important, but the book is not principally succession/statecraft/revolution; factions seek the devices and an Inhabitant's release is the decisive danger. Highfantasy requires consequential journey through inherited heroic-quest tradition. Most action is Charisat inquiry/relic procurement, not fellowshiproad or markedone'srise. Revisionist refusedmantle/aftermathhero tests fail: living after catastrophe is not aftermath-of-heroic-quest reckoning. Heroicclass routes: Khat's wilderness resilience, bodily gifts, cunning and relic expertise matter, but he is a dealer coerced into ancient-engine retrieval, not a showcase of trained thief/assassin/ranger class. Elen's mageoffice is one major perspective, not reason to file whole fiction as Magehero. NPChero's ordinary civic/social authority likewise undersells actual apparatus retrieval and dangerous adventure.

Gothic tested carefully: real soul/ghostlike predation, sanity-dangerous magic and bonedivination supply menace. Technomancy is strongest rival because arcane machinery and predatory spirits overlap its ominous apparatus language. But it inherits Gothicrealm's governing menace register; multiple completed reviews characterize an immersive adventure/puzzle/intrigue and book's movement is recovering, assembling, interpreting and preventing misuse of ancient magical machines. Bones valuable for divination does not itself establish necromancy-as-engineering. No claim that the machines run on raiseddead or souls was found. AncientInhabitants are predatory interdimensional beings, not necessarily ghosts of deceased people. Darkrealms/Godforsaken fails as governing dread rather than adventure; Haunted/Hungry likewise. FantasyMystery has a genuine artifact investigation, but Khat is not hired gumshoe or official investigator; relic dealer and coerced expedition guide, Elen a warder accompanying procurement, not institution's uncanny criminal case. No police/PI leaf stretched. Manners/Isolation fail.

SwordandSorcery: action-forward intimate expedition, city scrapes and dangerous artifact adventure fits existing broad scope, not only conventional swordsmen. Caper is a serious alternative: illegal relictrade, artifacts stolen and criminals/politics matter. But hired theft/gangcriminalcaper is not whole spine; ancient machinery and its supernatural operation/revelation supplies the positive more specific technological adventure engine. ClassicS&Swordsmen/pirate/tournament/swordplanet fail. Technofantasy passes because actual ancient magical devices and their engineering are central to both the repeated retrieval tasks and terminal threat. Magitech rather than Steampunkrealms: steam trucks are supporting transport and author explicitly separates the world from steampunk; the decisive apparatus is magic-powered arcane engines/doorway, not a steam-defined industrial world. Dieselpunk fails, no diesel register. No gate change required. Magitech's necromancy exclusion acknowledged, not bypassed; no demonstrated necromantic engineering and adventure register rules over the Gothic rival.

## Strongest uncertainty
This is not a pure gadget romp. Charisat's hierarchy, Khat's discrimination, Elen's psychological arc and the Inhabitants' dread are substantial. Reviewer tags vary SF, highfantasy, postapocalyptic, steampunk, NewWeird and mystery; none substitutes for this tree's ancestor/engine tests. Authornote disavows several easy labels. The choice rests on arcane-engine recovery/operation as the plot's repeated action, not cover/desertbioengineering vibe. Same source evidence could support GothicTechnomancy if register were weighted differently; no false precision. Existing Magitech already admits dangerous investigative magical-technology adventures (Hexologists). No threshold avoidance, Magitech2to3 while Godforsaken9/officialmystery9 never determines the walk.
