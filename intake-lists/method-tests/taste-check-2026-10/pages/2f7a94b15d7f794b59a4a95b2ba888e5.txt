#### suciul

In 2025 I read about 110 books of which 51 were 2025 releases and 21 so far made my top 25 of the year list; 8 and 1/2 sf, 5 1/2 mainstream, 5 fantasy and 2 history (the 1/2-1/2 is the Ian McEwan What We Can Know part sf novel). Top (2025 releases) SF: Shadows Upon Time - Ruocchio Sun Eater (1 overall and 1 sf), Pilgrim and the Wolf - Garrand, Species Seventeen 3 , Regent's Mate, Valkyrie Stratagem (G Stewart Adamant 3-4), Off Javelin Station - (Edelheit Guardians of the Dark 2) and To Face the Whirlwind (Thorensen Harbinger 2) Top (2025 releases) Fantasy: Legalist (Modesitt Grand Illusion standalone prequel 2 overall and 1 fantasy), Boutan Caliphate and The Hounds (AlwaysRollsAOne Soldier's Tale 4-5), A Drop of Corruption (Bennett Leviathan/Anna and Din 2), SubMajer's Challenge (Modesitt Alyiakal 3; part of Recluce - actually earliest chronologically)
