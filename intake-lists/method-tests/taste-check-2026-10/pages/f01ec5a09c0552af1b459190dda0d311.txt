# NEWS: 2025 Arthur C Clarke Award Winner announced

 Mark Yon June 26, 2025 0 Comment

![](https://www.sffworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/ClarkeAward.jpg) The winner of the 39th Arthur C. Clarke Award Science Fiction Book of the Year has been announced.

The list of nominees is as follows, with the winner highlighted in red:

Private Rites – Julia Armfield (4 th Estate)

The Ministry of Time – Kaliane Bradley (Sceptre)

Extremophile – Ian Green (AdAstra)

Annie Bot – Sierra Greer (The Borough Press)

Service Model – Adrian Tchaikovsky (Tor UK)

Thirteen Ways to Kill Lulabelle Rock – Maud Woolf (Angry Robot)

Congratulations to all nominees and the winner!
