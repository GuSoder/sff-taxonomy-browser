## Description

Sunward: A Novel by William Alexander — a cozy debut sci-fi where space-courier Captain Tova Lir raises AI teen bots across the solar system. When a derelict ship and an assassin turn routine into chaos, Tova must race through space to heal her charge, Agatha Panza von Sparkles, and gather her found-family AI for protection. Fast-paced, emotionally charged, and brimming with quirky sci-fi charm (think Becky Chambers meets robot rebellion), Sunward is short but unforgettable.
