# The Most Anticipated Fall Books, According to Goodreads Readers

Goodreads readers’ most anticipated fall 2026 books presents a packed season featuring major comebacks, big names, and series finales.

S. Zainab Williams Aug 17, 2026

Executive Director, Content

Goodreads has announced its readers’ most anticipated fall 2026 books, and it is a packed season featuring major comebacks, big names, and series finales. Many of the books featured on Goodreads’ list are repeats from other big fall previews.

The buzziest fiction reads of the season include:

- American Hagwon by Min Jin Lee
- Partita by Barbara Kingsolver
- Taipei Story by R.F. Kuang
- The True Confessions of First Lady Freeman by Deesha Philyaw

Readers of speculative fiction can’t wait for:

- ACOTAR Book 6 by Sarah J. Maas
- As You Wake, Break the Shell by Becky Chambers
- Mazywood by Tananarive Due
- Off the Reservation by Stephen Graham Jones

- Pulitzer Prize-winning author Hernan Diaz is back with a futuristic look at America in Ply
- I was beginning to doubt we’d ever see a followup to The Library at Mount Char but Scott Hawkins is back with Blacktail
- Alice Hoffman brings us a new witchy story, The Witches of Cambridge, just in time for Practical Magic 2 in theaters
- Malcolm Gladwell is entering the gun violence conversation with The American Way of Killing: The Invention of an Epidemic

Finally, we have two notable series conclusions:

- Dead Beat by Leigh Bardugo concludes the Ninth House books
- Victorious by V.E. Schwab concludes the Villains series

Brace yourself for October 6th when many of Goodreads’ anticipated fall 2026 books hit shelves.
