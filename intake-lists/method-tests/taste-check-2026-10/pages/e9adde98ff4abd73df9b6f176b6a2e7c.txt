Becky Chambers is one of my favorite Feelgood authors so here's your beginner's guide to Becky Chambers books if you haven't read a lot of sci-fi and you would like to try out the genre for yourself I recommend starting with a short noela called to be taught if fortunate set in the future this is a story of four astronuts who are on an expedition in order to find a planet that can be habitable by humans and this is so much a story about complexity of Human Relationships as much as is a story about the joy of Discovery if you have previously read cozy fantasy and you really enjoyed the genre or you prefer a more comforting read then I would suggest a monk and robot series by Becky Chambers this is a series about sibling decks and they are traveling with a rather unlikely companion a wild robot moscap this books are filled with a lot of very cute sense of humor but also many philosophical questions and honestly these are the ultimate feel-good books both of these books are also really short so they can be read in one sitting and if you ready to dive into a longer series or what is arguably Becky chamber's best work in my opinion then you can start with the wayf series so starting with a long way to a small angry Planet this is a character-driven feel-good interconnected four book series that I personally found very moving and emotional my favorite is book two of this series a closed and common orbit and honestly ever since I've completed this series I've been looking forward to rereading it again regardless of where you decide to start Becky chambers's books are the ultimate Comfort reads and her characters will stay in your heart for a really long time
