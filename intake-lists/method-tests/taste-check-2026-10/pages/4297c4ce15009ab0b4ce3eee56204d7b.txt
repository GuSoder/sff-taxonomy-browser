# More, More, More: 3 Exciting SFF Sequels Coming in 2026

Here are three of the most-anticipated sci-fi and fantasy sequels coming out in 2026, from pirates to superheroes to dragon riders!

Liberty Hardy Dec 18, 2025

### Liberty Hardy

Senior Contributing Editor

One of the many exciting things about a new year is all the sequels that are released. And there are a lot that I am looking forward to in 2026! There are editions to big series like the eighth Murderbot, Platform Decay, and the eleventh Wayward Children book, Through Gates of Garnet and Gold. I am over the moon that we get another adventure with Ana and Din, A Trade of Blood, from Robert Jackson Bennett’s Shadow of the Leviathan series, and I am going to try and make 2026 the year I finally read Nghi Vo’s Singing Hills Cycle, which will grow in May with A Long and Speaking Silence.

Of course, my evergreen wish every year is for news of Alecto the Ninth from Tamysn Muir, the next book in the Locked Tomb series, but I understand we will get it when we get it. I would rather put my energy into reading other books (and re-reading the first three in the series) while we wait than whining about it. (But we wantsssss the precioussssssss.)

So, those are just a few of the sequels I am excited about, plus these three below that also made me go “EEEEEEEE!” when I learned about them. Maybe you’ll go “EEEEEEEE!” too—let’s find out! (Note: All the descriptions are for the first in the series to avoid any spoilers.)

All Access members, read on for three of the most anticipated SFF sequels of 2026.

In the first award-winning fantasy in this series, To Shape a Dragon’s Breath, a young woman named Anequs discovers the first dragon’s egg on her island of Masquapaug in centuries. Hoping to raise it with her people, Anequs is instead forced to attend school on the mainland by the colonizers, being told she must learn how to control her dragon or else. Can she (and her dragon) bring change to a racist system?

What’s better than a fantasy novel about pirates? Another fantasy novel about pirates! Retired pirate captain Amina al-Sirafi went off for one more voyage in The Adventures of Amina al-Sirafi, to help find a crewman’s missing daughter. (For a lot of money, of course.) But the further she got out to sea, the more she felt she hadn’t been told the whole truth about the job. What’s going to happen next?! 

And finally (finally) the follow-up to Hench is coming this spring! It’s had its release date pushed several times, and there’s no guarantee it won’t again, but it has been listed at this date for a long time, so I’m feeling pretty confident about it. And I’m also really excited, because I loooooved Hench! It’s about a temp worker injured on the job during a battle between a superhero and a villain. When it turns out neither one will compensate her for her injuries, she joins up with the city’s biggest supervillain to change the system, with the help of her fabulous spreadsheets. More sci-fi spreadsheet nerdiness, please!
