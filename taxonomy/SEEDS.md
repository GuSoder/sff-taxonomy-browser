# How new leaves are carved

Living doc of seeding doctrine, recorded from the curator's rulings in live workshops on the project channel. Reference for future leaf creation. Refine as the project goes. Rulings cited by message id.

## The principle: non-overlap is the constraint, coverage is the objective

(the curator, design question phonemsg-01M3KRHP3NNKH0RFCX6MCPZ7V3; doc commission phonemsg-01M3KSB5EF8F8KESYRHTKEKWM1; coverage steer phonemsg-01M3KTBRFM2GADKK8YPBCX7RJB)

A new leaf exists to absorb space. Carve it by non-overlap, then widen it to the honest ceiling: the def should take in as much of the uncovered territory as the corpus honestly supports - never narrower for comfort. The method, as demonstrated by the Body swap carve (2026-09-28):

1. **The full walk comes first.** Walk root-down and test every umbrella's children on each plausible route. A new leaf is the answer ONLY when the walk reaches a terminus and nothing fits. Never anchor on a mechanism and skip the walk: the Jessica fault matched the wish mechanism to Wicked wishes without walking ("No, this is not even nocturne fantasy, did you eve do the walk?", phonemsg-01M3KQRS2H8936BW7P6PCJG03D). The mechanism is the door, not the subject.
2. **Read the existing children as a partition.** Ask what single set of axes the siblings divide by. (Contemporary fantasy: source/site of magic - mythic power awake / the protagonist's own emerging ability / the household as site.)
3. **Define the leaf by the uncovered slot - all of it.** The def names what the leaf is AND why it is not each sibling, one line per sibling and per near-miss branch. Then widen it until it covers the whole honest slot, not just the seed's own shape: a leaf that holds only the seed's exact premise wastes the carve. (Body swap: not mythic, not ability-grown, not household-sited, not damnation-priced, not accepted-as-ordinary.)
4. **The ceiling report keeps wideness honest.** Ground membership in the corpus: seed plus verified kin, and check the look-alikes before claiming them (the two Jessica look-alikes walked elsewhere: Lakelore is portal/MR, Aria Patel is multiverse). Report the honest ceiling - what the wide def truly holds today and what it is built to catch. The ceiling report, not a narrow def, is the brake on overreach.
5. **Single-card leaves are legal; remainder buckets are not.** A wide honest def with a real about-ness is never a slush bucket, even at one card. A slush bucket is a remainder bucket with no about-ness; that is the only illegal shape.

Naming follows NAMES.md: established term first (Body swap).

Leaf def: a one-time magical identity trade in the present-day world; the swap is the problem to survive or undo; the price never damnation.
