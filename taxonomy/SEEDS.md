# How new leaves are carved

Living doc of seeding doctrine, recorded from the curator's rulings in live workshops on the project channel. Reference for future leaf creation. Refine as the project goes. Rulings cited by message id.

## The principle: non-overlap is the constraint, coverage is the objective

(the curator, design question phonemsg-01M3KRHP3NNKH0RFCX6MCPZ7V3; doc commission phonemsg-01M3KSB5EF8F8KESYRHTKEKWM1; coverage steer phonemsg-01M3KTBRFM2GADKK8YPBCX7RJB)

A new leaf exists to absorb space. Carve it by non-overlap, then widen it to the honest ceiling: the def should take in as much of the uncovered territory as the corpus honestly supports - never narrower for comfort. The method, as demonstrated by the Body swap carve (2026-09-28):

1. **The full walk comes first.** Walk root-down and test every umbrella's children on each plausible route. A new leaf is the answer ONLY when the walk reaches a terminus and nothing fits. Never anchor on a mechanism and skip the walk: the Jessica fault matched the wish mechanism to Wicked wishes without walking ("No, this is not even nocturne fantasy, did you eve do the walk?", phonemsg-01M3KQRS2H8936BW7P6PCJG03D). The mechanism is the door, not the subject.
2. **Read the existing children as a partition.** Ask what single set of axes the siblings divide by. (Contemporary fantasy: source/site of magic - mythic power awake / the protagonist's own emerging ability / the household as site.)
3. **Define the leaf by the uncovered slot - all of it.** The def names what the leaf is AND why it is not each sibling, one line per sibling and per near-miss branch. Then widen it until it covers the whole honest slot, not just the seed's own shape: a leaf that holds only the seed's exact premise wastes the carve. (Body swap: not mythic, not ability-grown, not household-sited, not damnation-priced, not accepted-as-ordinary.)
4. **The ceiling report keeps wideness honest.** Ground membership in the corpus: seed plus verified kin, and check the look-alikes before claiming them (the two Jessica look-alikes walked elsewhere: Lakelore is portal/MR, Aria Patel is multiverse). Report the honest ceiling - what the wide def truly holds today and what it is built to catch. The ceiling report, not a narrow def, is the brake on overreach.
5. **Single-card leaves are legal; remainder buckets are not.** A wide honest def with a real about-ness is never a slush bucket, even at one card. A slush bucket is a remainder bucket with no about-ness; that is the only illegal shape.

Naming follows NAMES.md: established term first (Body swap).

## Case study: Body swap (2026-09-28)

Terminus walk: fantasy -> real-earth-fantasy -> contemporary-fantasy; all three children failed (no mythic power grants the wish; magic happens TO the protagonist once, no ability developed; the site is the academy and social world, not the household). The uncovered slot: a one-time external magic event rewrites the terms of one ordinary life - the whole traded life is the site. Leaf def: a one-time magical identity trade in the present-day world; the swap is the problem to survive or undo; the price never damnation. Seeded by I Am Not Jessica Chen (the curator, "Execute" phonemsg-01M3KS8AAZRA6WWW7FCDPH92FJ). Kin noted, not moved: Turnabout (Thorne Smith 1931) stays in comic-fantasy on the comedy-engine exception.

Doctrine note: the def shipped tight to the seed's evidence; the curator's coverage steer (phonemsg-01M3KTBRFM2GADKK8YPBCX7RJB) governs future carves - widen to the honest ceiling first, let the ceiling report do the braking. A wider Body swap def (any wished or cursed rewritten life, the 13 Going on 30 shape) remains open for his ruling.

## Ruling: the level-3 limit is relaxed for Period SF and Hidden history SF (2026-10-06)

The curator, phonemsg-01M48J741HQYF2DJNMQ57TY9ZV (2:15:05 PM): "there are some lvl3 genres that actually are reasonable to expand ... I worry you have not touched them because of my lvl3 rule. One of them is Period SF (could do with more genres, split nice due to time, but Steampunk should not be buried.) Another is Hidden history SF (seems niche, but do not let my lvl3 rule stop this from growing)".

Applies to: Period SF (speculative-fiction > science-fiction > historical > period-sf) and Hidden history SF (same parent). Both may take new children by seed or split. Period SF splits by time period, and new period leaves are siblings of Steampunk and Deco SF under Period SF: Steampunk stays exactly where it is and is never moved down a level. Every other level-3 genre keeps the level-3 rule (avoid seeding at level 3 or higher). Approval flow is unchanged: one "Approve seed?:" or "Approve split?:" at a time.

## Ruling: Animal fantasy added to the level-3 relaxation (2026-10-06)

The curator, phonemsg-01M48JCHHMJTXJ57A1JMQHSJHZ (2:18:03 PM): "Another 'lvl3 genre allowed for growth' is Animal Fantasy, almost silly to have as a thin leaf up here, could perhaps grow into a 'Fable' umbrella category."

Animal fantasy (speculative-fiction > fantasy > real-earth-fantasy > animal-fantasy, 5 fictions) may now take children by seed or split. A Fable umbrella is a structural change: it is brought to him as an "Approve seed?:" or "Approve split?:" proposal when the books justify it, never created on my own. The relaxed level-3 genres are now Period SF, Hidden history SF and Animal fantasy; all others keep the limit.

The curator, phonemsg-01M48JHGJX6H98Z6YYFAJ1HZ40 (2:20:45 PM): "Another 'open lvl3' is Far shores, this one can have geographical bundles like silkpunk when the other children grow numerous".

The relaxed level-3 genres are now Period SF, Hidden history SF, Animal fantasy and Far shores fantasy.
