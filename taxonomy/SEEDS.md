# How new leaves are carved

Living doc of seeding doctrine, recorded from the curator's rulings in live workshops on the project channel. Reference for future leaf creation. Refine as the project goes. Rulings cited by message id.

## The principle: carve by non-overlap

(the curator, design question phonemsg-01M3KRHP3NNKH0RFCX6MCPZ7V3; doc commission phonemsg-01M3KSB5EF8F8KESYRHTKEKWM1)

A new leaf is justified only as the uncovered slot in an existing partition. The method, as demonstrated by the Body swap carve (2026-09-28):

1. **The full walk comes first.** Walk root-down and test every umbrella's children on each plausible route. A new leaf is the answer ONLY when the walk reaches a terminus and nothing fits. Never anchor on a mechanism and skip the walk: the Jessica fault matched the wish mechanism to Wicked wishes without walking ("No, this is not even nocturne fantasy, did you eve do the walk?", phonemsg-01M3KQRS2H8936BW7P6PCJG03D). The mechanism is the door, not the subject.
2. **Read the existing children as a partition.** Ask what single set of axes the siblings divide by. (Contemporary fantasy: source/site of magic - mythic power awake / the protagonist's own emerging ability / the household as site.)
3. **Define the leaf by the uncovered slot** - by what it is AND why it is not each sibling. The def carries its own non-overlap proof, one line per sibling and per near-miss branch. (Body swap: not mythic, not ability-grown, not household-sited, not damnation-priced, not accepted-as-ordinary.)
4. **Ground membership honestly.** Seed plus verified kin only; check the look-alikes before claiming them (the two Jessica look-alikes walked elsewhere: Lakelore is portal/MR, Aria Patel is multiverse). Report the honest ceiling - a tight def that nets 1-2 cards beats a wide memberless one.
5. **Single-card leaves are legal.** A thin leaf is a named home for future intake, not a failure. What is not legal: a remainder bucket. No slush buckets.

## Case study: Body swap (2026-09-28)

Terminus walk: fantasy -> real-earth-universe -> contemporary-fantasy; all three children failed (no mythic power grants the wish; magic happens TO the protagonist once, no ability developed; the site is the academy and social world, not the household). The uncovered slot: a one-time external magic event rewrites the terms of one ordinary life - the whole traded life is the site. Leaf defined tight: a one-time magical identity trade in the present-day world; the swap is the problem to survive or undo; the price never damnation.
