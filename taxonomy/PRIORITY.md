# Priority order

Living doc of work-order doctrine, commissioned by the curator on the project channel (phonemsg-01M3KV6R6P1F98SX7KACK4D4E3). Reference for what gets done first. Refine as the project goes. Rulings cited by message id.

## The order

(the curator, phonemsg-01M3KV6R6P1F98SX7KACK4D4E3)

1. **Keep the golden rule: anything at 10? -> Split.**
2. **Any book hanging? -> Seed new leaf.**
3. **Intake.**

## The seed-placement caveat (priority 2)

(the curator, same message)

You cannot just spam seed leaves in the branches close to the trunk. Rule of thumb:

- **All siblings are leaves: probably ok.** If they reach 10 leaves, the golden rule takes care of it later - although that will be handled by a "bundle" process (not covered yet).
- **1/2/3 steps from the trunk, where all other genres are broad genres with lots of subgenres: no new sibling.** There should hopefully already be a plan in place for them, or they are not meant to have a new sibling. If you find a work that does not walk further than this, some sort of reorg should be DISCUSSED with the curator - do not seed.
- **Medium distance: ok to add a new leaf** if you see the pattern, and the branch is not crowded.
