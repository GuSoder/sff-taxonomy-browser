# Glossary

Terms used in the taxonomy docs, work files and reports. Use them exactly.

- **Fiction** - what one entry in the taxonomy is. A fiction can be anything from a stand-alone novel to a whole shared universe of books: one novel, a trilogy, a long series or a franchise. Each fiction sits in exactly one leaf. (In the work files and in index.html the data still calls this a "work" or "card"; the data keys stay as they are. In prose and reports, say "fiction".)
- **Book** - one individual book: a single volume. A fiction holds one or more books.
- **Leaf** - a genre node with no children; only leaves hold fictions.
- **Umbrella** - a genre node that has children and holds no fictions of its own.

Counting rules:
- "Fictions" = entries in the tree (what used to be reported as "cards").
- "Books" = individual volumes across all fictions. A fiction with no volume list counts as one book.
- Report both numbers when giving a total, and name which is which.

- **Era test** - the gate between Contemporary fantasy and Historical fantasy: where the BULK of the book is set. A co-lead present-day strand does not make a book contemporary; if the bulk is set in a past era, the book walks Historical. (The curator, phonemsg-01M52BETD579YERZZTANJ2807V, 2026-10-16: "if the bulk is not contemporary it's not a contemporary book.")
- **Setting test** - the gate between Real earth fantasy and Fantasy realms: what the book itself states. If the book states an invented world, it walks Fantasy realms, however Earth-like; veiled-analogue readings (their world standing for ours) do not count. Alternate-history Earth is Earth: a forked timeline of our world stays Real earth (the curator, phonemsg-01M52PRS9JQ47MSHXP7DEGYPPK, 2026-10-16: "Earth forked is earth, not fantasy realm."). (The curator, phonemsg-01M52MD1GQ9V2Y5MDZ81JCX4NN, 2026-10-16: "What is stated in the book matters, even if you and reviewers say things are veiled earth analogues, if it is a fantasy realm the book should be in Fantasy realms.")
