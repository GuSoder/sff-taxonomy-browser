# Spelling and capitalization

Adopted September 30, 2026. Applies to every genre display label, including new nodes, renamed nodes, sibling tiles and breadcrumb names.

## Default: sentence case

Capitalize the first word. Lowercase ordinary words after it. Preserve proper nouns, proper adjectives and abbreviations as listed below.

Examples:

- Space navy
- The comforting dead
- Comic fantasy realms
- Books in books
- Bowler hat fantasy
- Hard conceptual SF
- NPC heroes

This is a display-label rule, not a change to genre meaning. Capitalization-only edits do not change node IDs, slugs, paths, links, portraits or book titles. When quoting a genre label in a definition or placement rationale, use its canonical spelling.

## Keep names and abbreviations

Keep SF, WWI, WWII, NPC and S&S uppercase wherever they occur.

Keep proper names and proper adjectives, including Orwellian, Huxleyan, Greek, Anansi, Arthur, Mars, Albion, Ming, Chinese and Jamaican. Use the same rule for other genuine proper names, not only this list.

Named historical events, periods, regions and mythic ages retain their proper-name capitals:

- Alt Civil War
- Alt Cold War
- Alt WWI
- Dust Bowl fantasy
- Weird West fantasy
- Fifth Sun

Generic terms such as golden age, gaslight, crusade, spice route, flintlock and diesel do not acquire capitals merely because they name a shelf.

## Settled exceptions

Keep these established labels exactly:

- Postcolonial Gothic
- Suburban Gothic
- War Gothic
- Mad-science Gothic
- Cosmic Gothic
- New Weird
- Modern Goth

Capital-G Gothic is retained for the literary form. New Weird keeps the named movement's customary spelling. These are house-style choices; they do not justify title case for other genre labels.

Use Post-holocaust survival. Its holocaust means a general catastrophe, not the historical Holocaust.

Use Real earth fantasy exactly as chosen by the curator. This is an intentional lowercase-earth exception. Elsewhere, keep Earth when it names the planet, as in Future of Earth fiction, Future history of Earth and Ruined Earth. The existing Dying earth label is retained; this capitalization pass did not reopen previously settled lowercase-earth names.

## The curator's phone messages

The curator's messages may be autocorrected on the phone. Capitalization in those messages does not override this document. Normalize proposed or dictated labels to this style, even when the phone capitalizes them differently.

A substantive naming decision still belongs to the curator. An explicit instruction to change this spelling rule is a rule change; incidental capitalization in an ordinary message is not.

## Before adding or renaming a label

1. Apply sentence case.
2. Preserve genuine proper names and abbreviations.
3. Check the settled exceptions above.
4. Use the canonical label consistently in the browser and its genre record.
5. Do not alter slugs or book-title spelling for a capitalization-only change.
